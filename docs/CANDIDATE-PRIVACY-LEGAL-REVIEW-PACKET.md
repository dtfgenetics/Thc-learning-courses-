# Candidate Privacy & Legal Review Packet

**Controls:** `CANDIDATE-GOVERNANCE-CONTROLS-001@0.2.0`  
**State:** prepared — privacy/legal approval pending

This packet is the review companion to `registry/candidate-privacy-legal-review-preparation.json`.

## Current issuer baseline

The Academy already defines:

- data minimization;
- role-based access;
- auditability and preservation of original scored evidence;
- limited public verification;
- accommodation confidentiality;
- retest/appeal/security controls;
- a proposed retention schedule.

Those policy choices may continue to guide development. They do **not** replace jurisdiction-specific privacy/legal review before operational use.

## Methodological/legal guidance already controlled

- `REF-ISO-17024-2026-001` — certification-person governance, records, confidentiality, security and appeals.
- `REF-ADA-TESTING-ACCOMMODATIONS-001` — high-stakes testing accommodation/accessibility requirements and construct preservation.
- `REF-FTC-DATA-MINIMIZATION-001` — data minimization, purpose-based retention, written retention policy and secure disposal guidance.

## Review areas

The controlled matrix requires disposition of:

1. data inventory and minimization;
2. candidate notice and processing basis/policy disclosure;
3. accommodation confidentiality;
4. retention and disposal;
5. candidate access/correction/appeal/data-rights handling;
6. public credential verification;
7. access control and vendors/processors;
8. security incident/breach handling;
9. jurisdiction/employment/education/records applicability.

## Required final decision

The review authority must either:

- approve the exact `0.2.0` controls and retention schedule;
- approve with explicit jurisdiction/contract overrides; or
- require revision.

Operational use must remain false until that exact-version privacy/legal decision is recorded and applied.

This document organizes review. It is not legal advice and does not itself close the privacy/legal gate.
