# Curriculum and Credential Governance

## Roles

- Author: drafts instructional content.
- Editor: reviews clarity, structure, and accessibility.
- Subject Matter Reviewer: validates scientific accuracy and references.
- Assessment Reviewer: validates item quality and objective alignment.
- Final Approver: authorizes credential-bearing publication.

For credential-bearing content, the author should not be the only final approver.

## Review states

`draft -> editorial-review -> scientific-review -> assessment-review -> approved -> published -> superseded -> retired`

Not every non-credential object requires every stage, but all summative assessment items and credential definitions do.

## AI-assisted work

AI may draft, transform, classify, summarize, suggest objectives, propose questions, or identify coverage gaps. AI-generated or AI-assisted material must not become credential-bearing content until a qualified human reviewer approves it.

## Versioning

Published course, lesson, assessment, and credential definitions are immutable. Any material change creates a new version. Historical learner records must reference the exact version completed.

## Appeals and corrections

The runtime platform must support cases for score disputes, accessibility complaints, credential corrections, revocation disputes, and scientific-content challenges. Changes must preserve an auditable event history.
