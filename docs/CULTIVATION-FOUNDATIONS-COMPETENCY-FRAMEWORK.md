# Cultivation Foundations Competency Framework

This framework defines the first body-of-knowledge spine for the THC Academy Cultivation Foundations pathway. Each domain must ultimately map to competencies, measurable learning objectives, lessons, assessment blueprints, approved item pools, and reviewed scientific sources.

## Domain map

1. Plant Biology and Lifecycle — anatomy, plant functions, lifecycle stages, photosynthesis/respiration context.
2. Environmental Management — temperature, relative humidity, VPD, airflow, environmental interactions.
3. Lighting and Photosynthesis — PPFD, DLI, spectrum, photoperiod, fixture/application fundamentals.
4. Water and Irrigation — water quality, pH, EC, alkalinity context, irrigation timing and dryback fundamentals.
5. Root-Zone and Media Management — media physical/chemical properties, drainage, aeration, moisture, CEC context.
6. Plant Nutrition — macro/micronutrients, mobility, availability, deficiency patterns, toxicity and antagonism.
7. Integrated Pest Management — prevention, sanitation, scouting, identification, thresholds and control hierarchy.
8. Propagation — seed biology, germination, seedlings, cuttings/cloning, transplant readiness.
9. Plant Training and Canopy Management — apical dominance, LST/HST, topping, SCROG, canopy architecture and recovery.
10. Flowering, Maturity and Harvest — floral development, maturity indicators, harvest planning and handling.
11. Drying, Curing and Storage — water loss, environmental control, quality preservation, storage fundamentals.
12. Genetics and Breeding Foundations — genotype/phenotype, inheritance, sex expression, selection and breeding vocabulary.

## Implemented foundation objects

The repository currently contains first competency/objective objects for plant biology, environmental VPD, lighting, water, root-zone management, nutrition, IPM and propagation. Remaining domains are explicitly queued rather than silently omitted.

## Required coverage before a certificate can publish

A credential-bearing version of Cultivation Foundations must not publish until every required domain has: at least one approved competency; measurable objectives; instructional coverage; reviewed evidence; a summative assessment blueprint; an approved item pool; defined passing/retake policy; and successful publication-readiness validation.

## Depth model

Foundation competencies focus on accurate recognition, interpretation and application. Advanced pathways may reuse the same domain taxonomy while defining higher-level competencies at analyze/evaluate/create levels rather than inflating a foundation course with specialist material.

## Separation from regulatory authorization

The educational credential documents completion and demonstrated learning against the THC Academy curriculum. It is not a state license, occupational authorization, or permission to cultivate, possess, manufacture, distribute or sell cannabis.
