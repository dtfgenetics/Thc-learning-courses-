# Certification Assessment Review Criteria

These criteria govern exact-version human review of certification assessment definitions and question items.

## Methodology basis

- **ETS item-development guidance** supports review for objective/standard match, technical quality, appropriate difficulty and cognitive level, accessibility, fairness, style, grammar and accuracy.
- **U.S. DOJ testing-accommodation guidance** requires high-stakes certification/credentialing tests to measure the intended aptitude, achievement or skill rather than disability-related barriers when the affected ability is not itself the construct being tested.
- THC Academy's course objectives, controlled evidence, JTA/occupational scope and role-authority boundaries remain the controlling local context.

Controlled sources:
- `REF-ETS-ITEM-FAIRNESS-001`
- `REF-ADA-TESTING-ACCOMMODATIONS-001`

## Assessment-definition review

Confirm:
- traceability to exact-version course objectives/competencies and occupational scope;
- appropriate blueprint breadth and cognitive target;
- provisional cut-score language until formal standard setting;
- secure attempts, feedback, randomization and form construction;
- accommodation rules that preserve the intended construct;
- fail-closed operational use until human review, required pilot/item evidence, active-item depth and secure-store/form evidence exist.

## Item review

Confirm:
- direct objective/competency alignment;
- evidence-supported factual premises, key and rationale;
- one defensible keyed answer;
- plausible but clearly incorrect distractors;
- no trick wording, hidden assumptions or irrelevant reading burden;
- cognitive demand comes from the intended skill, not obscure vocabulary or trivia;
- fairness/bias review for irrelevant demographic, cultural, socioeconomic or disability barriers;
- accessible alternate presentation where sensory/motor presentation is not the intended construct;
- role/legal/safety authority boundaries;
- exact-version security of answer/rationale material.

## Decision

Use **APPROVE**, **REVISE**, or **REJECT** with reviewer identity, exact object version, date, findings and evidence checked.

A machine-quality pass or owner approval of source-backed educational content does not substitute for exact-version human assessment review.
