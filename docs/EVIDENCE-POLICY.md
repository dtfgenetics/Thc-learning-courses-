# THC Academy Evidence Policy

## Purpose

Scientific and technical educational claims must be traceable to reviewed evidence before credential-bearing publication.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Peer-reviewed primary literature and systematic reviews.
2. University extension and recognized academic horticulture sources.
3. ASTM, ISO, ANSI, government, and other recognized standards/guidance.
4. Established textbooks and technical references.
5. Reputable industry technical material.
6. Experiential cultivation guidance, explicitly labeled as experiential rather than established science.

## Claim lifecycle

`draft -> review -> approved -> published -> superseded -> retired`

Evidence status is tracked separately:

`needs-evidence -> evidence-collected -> reviewed`

A claim may also become `disputed` or `superseded` when evidence changes.

## Publication rules

- Published scientific claims require at least one reviewed reference.
- Credential-bearing assessment items must not depend on claims with `needs-evidence`, `disputed`, or `superseded` evidence status.
- A changed or retracted source triggers review of every linked claim, lesson, competency, and assessment item.
- Experiential claims must be clearly identified and must not be represented as established scientific consensus.

## Review freshness

Claims may specify `reviewDueAt`. Regulatory, safety, rapidly changing technology, and applied cultivation guidance should receive shorter review intervals than foundational plant biology.

## Provenance

Future production objects will record author, reviewer, scientific reviewer, approval date, content hash, and whether AI materially assisted drafting.
