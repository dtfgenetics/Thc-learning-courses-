# Technician II Practical D — IPM Trend & Treatment Follow-Up

## Purpose
Demonstrate senior-technician interpretation of repeated scouting data and intervention follow-up while maintaining authorization boundaries.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-IPM-001`
- `COMP-IPM-ADV-001`
- `COMP-PRO-QA-001`

## Candidate receives
Fixed scouting map/sample counts, 3–4 scouting rounds, incidence/severity data, trap counts where relevant, crop/bench/batch IDs, intervention history, biological-control release or approved treatment records, and environmental context.

## Candidate must
Verify comparability of sampling effort; map spatial spread/contraction; distinguish incidence from severity; classify trend; evaluate intervention response; identify missing/confounded evidence; document affected scope and follow-up priority; escalate treatment decisions outside assigned authority.

## Critical errors
Unauthorized pesticide/treatment action; declaring control success from isolated data contradicted by the trend; erasing scouting observations; breaking quarantine/biosecurity handling.

## Scoring
Sampling/data quality 15; trend/spatial interpretation 25; intervention follow-up 25; scope/escalation 20; documentation 15.

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
IPM Trend & Treatment Follow-Up Report, annotated scouting map, and next-round follow-up plan.
