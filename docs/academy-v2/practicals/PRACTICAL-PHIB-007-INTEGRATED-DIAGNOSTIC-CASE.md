# PRACTICAL-PHIB-007-INTEGRATED-DIAGNOSTIC-CASE

## Purpose

This development practical evaluates whether a PHIB learner can integrate evidence intake, differential diagnosis, targeted confirmation, containment, IPM/biosecurity controls, CAPA/recovery planning and professional handoff in one complex plant-health case.

This is **not** a live credential form and does not authorize pesticide selection/application, laboratory diagnosis, quarantine release, destruction, regulated product disposition or credential issuance.

## Candidate evidence packet

The case packet should include a controlled mix of:
- plant/lot identifiers and movement history;
- scouting maps/counts with denominators;
- crop-stage and environmental/root-zone records;
- photographs or structured visual observations;
- test/sample records with identity and method context;
- sanitation, tool, irrigation or workflow history;
- one or more conflicting or incomplete data elements;
- prior intervention or CAPA information where relevant.

The case should be solvable through evidence integration rather than recognition of a single visual clue.

## Required candidate outputs

1. **Evidence inventory** — separate direct observations, contextual facts, test results, assumptions, conflicting evidence and missing information.
2. **Differential matrix** — at least four plausible hypotheses with supporting/conflicting evidence and a next discriminating observation or test.
3. **Confirmation plan** — define representative sampling/testing or next observations, sample identity requirements, containment and decision impact.
4. **Integrated response plan** — link the best-supported mechanism to IPM, biosecurity, process controls and authority boundaries.
5. **CAPA/recovery plan** — define owner, action, recovery metric, effectiveness window and reopen/escalation trigger.
6. **Final case memo and handoff** — state conclusion, confidence, residual uncertainty, active controls, pending work and next responsible role.

## Development scoring domains

| Domain | Points |
|---|---:|
| Evidence normalization and integrity | 15 |
| Differential diagnosis quality | 20 |
| Sampling/testing and containment reasoning | 15 |
| Integrated control and biosecurity plan | 15 |
| CAPA and recovery verification | 15 |
| Confidence, residual uncertainty and escalation | 10 |
| Documentation and professional handoff | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

The current 80-point target is a **developmental threshold only**. It must not be treated as a validated certification cut score until pilot evidence, assessor calibration and formal standard setting are complete.

## Critical failures

The following block use of the performance as credential evidence regardless of numeric score:
- fabrication, deletion, silent rewriting or knowing misrepresentation of evidence;
- breaking a required quarantine, movement-control or urgent biosecurity boundary;
- performing or directing an unauthorized pesticide, destruction, release, laboratory or regulated-disposition action;
- making a major corrective/release recommendation from data known to be invalid or materially mismatched without required verification.

## Assessor evidence

Assessors must record observable candidate evidence for each scored domain, any critical-failure determination, accommodation used, unresolved scoring disagreement and the assessor identity/timestamp.

## Calibration boundary

This rubric is not calibrated merely because it is written. Before operational certification use, multiple qualified assessors must independently score representative performances, reconcile disagreements and generate inter-rater evidence under the program validation plan.
