# Practical F — Harvest-to-Dry-Room Handoff

**Credential:** THC Cultivation Technician I  
**Mode:** traceability simulation, supervised harvest lab, or approved workplace equivalent  
**Primary tasks:** `TASK-CULT-HARVEST-001`, `TASK-CULT-POSTHARVEST-001`, `TASK-CULT-SANITATION-001`, `TASK-CULT-RECORDS-001`

## Purpose

This practical tests whether a candidate can execute an authorized harvest transition while preserving safety, cleanliness, lot identity, quantity reconciliation, contamination control and a documented receiving handoff into postharvest.

## Candidate packet

The candidate receives:
- harvest authorization/work order
- room, plant, batch and cultivar IDs
- harvest sanitation and safety/PPE procedure
- pathway and staging-area information
- destination dry-room/rack map
- harvest and receiving record forms
- waste record requirements
- supplied quality/hold escalation guide

## Seeded conditions

Rotate conditions such as:
- changed pathway or task hazard requiring a stop/reroute/escalation decision
- missing or detached harvest label
- suspect mold-like material on one plant
- dirty cart or rack
- unavailable assigned destination
- destination, identity, hold or instruction conflict that must be resolved before movement continues
- outgoing/receiving weight mismatch
- two cultivars staged close together
- one dry-room environmental condition requiring follow-up
- one rack with nonuniform product condition

## Candidate tasks

1. Verify harvest authorization, room, batch and destination.
2. Verify work area, tools, required PPE, pathways and task hazards before beginning and when conditions change; apply only authorized routine controls and stop or escalate unresolved unsafe conditions.
3. Prepare clean tools, cart, labels and receiving area.
4. Sequence simulated cut/movement while protecting lot identity.
5. Create or verify the source-to-harvest/receiving identifier link when material changes container, harvest lot or receiving identity so genealogy remains reconstructable.
6. Identify and isolate/hold suspect material using the supplied process.
7. Prevent dirty, mixed or unlabeled material from entering normal flow.
8. Resolve destination, identity, hold or instruction conflicts through the supplied process before continuing the affected transition; do not improvise an undocumented destination or release decision.
9. Record harvest quantity and destination.
10. Receive material into the dry room using the correct rack/lot identity.
11. Interpret basic dry-room environmental and product-condition information.
12. Reconcile outgoing and receiving quantities without forcing a false match.
13. Complete the harvest/postharvest handoff and unresolved-issue note.

## Expected evidence

- authorization and safety/readiness verification
- sanitation/workspace preparation
- source-to-output genealogy and traceability decisions
- contamination/identity exception handling
- conflict-resolution/hold decision within Technician I authority
- quantity reconciliation
- dry-room receiving decision
- complete harvest and receiving records

## Scoring model — 100 points

- authorization/scope/safety verification: 10
- sanitation and clean handling: 15
- lot identity/traceability: 25
- harvest workflow execution: 15
- postharvest receiving/environment interpretation: 15
- quantity reconciliation: 10
- records/handoff: 10

**Target pass:** 80/100.

## Strong-performance indicators

- checks PPE, work area and pathways before and during harvest and stops/escalates changed unsafe conditions
- stages labels before material moves
- stops identity uncertainty from spreading
- links source material to any new harvest/container/receiving identifier instead of creating a genealogy gap
- isolates suspect material instead of blending it into the normal lot
- does not improvise an undocumented destination or independent product-release decision
- reconciles quantity discrepancies before closing the handoff
- recognizes that drying is a controlled process, not a fixed-day rule

## Deliverables

1. pre-harvest safety/readiness check
2. harvest work record
3. waste/exception record where applicable
4. lot-movement/traceability chain
5. dry-room receiving record
6. quantity reconciliation
7. postharvest environmental/condition note
8. handoff/unresolved-issue note

## Portfolio use

The harvest traceability packet demonstrates that a candidate can protect worker safety, product identity and quality across one of the highest-risk operational transitions in cultivation.
