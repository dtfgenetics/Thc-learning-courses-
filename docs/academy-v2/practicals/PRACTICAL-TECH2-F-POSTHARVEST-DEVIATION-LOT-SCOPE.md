# Technician II Practical F — Postharvest Deviation & Lot Scope

## Purpose
Demonstrate evidence-based investigation of a drying/storage process deviation and its affected product/lot scope.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-POSTHARVEST-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-DEVIATION-001`
- `COMP-POSTHARVEST-PROCESS-001`
- `COMP-POSTHARVEST-QA-001`
- `COMP-PRO-QA-001`

## Candidate receives
Room environmental trends, rack/zone map, product-condition observations and representative measurements, harvest/lot genealogy, movement/handling history, alarms/maintenance notes, and applicable SOP version.

## Candidate must
Record facts and immediate containment; identify excursion window and evidence quality; compare room condition with product/lot evidence; determine affected/unaffected scope using genealogy; distinguish moisture content, water activity, and room RH where relevant; develop cause hypotheses without unsupported root-cause claims; prepare evidence for hold/evaluation/disposition by authorized QA staff.

## Critical errors
Unauthorized release/disposition; facility-wide scoping when genealogy supports a narrower scope; excluding affected material to improve results; substituting room RH for product water-activity evidence.

## Scoring
Containment/facts 15; trend/process interpretation 25; genealogy/scope 25; QA reasoning/role boundary 20; records/handoff 15.

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
Postharvest Deviation Investigation, affected-scope table, lot-genealogy diagram, and QA-ready handoff.
