# Technician II Capstone — Senior Technician Diagnostic Shift

## Purpose
Integrate Technician II competencies across a simulated senior-technician shift. The capstone measures prioritization, diagnostic discipline, data quality, operational execution, communication, and traceability rather than isolated recall.

## Candidate role
The candidate is the senior technician for a shift spanning at least two cultivation/process areas. A supervisor is available for escalations but does not direct every action.

## Required case elements
Each generated form contains one crop/environment problem; one irrigation/root-zone or fertigation problem; one IPM trend/follow-up decision; one process, postharvest, or traceability deviation; one production-metric review; one peer execution inconsistency; at least one misleading/low-quality data signal; and competing priorities that require sequencing.

## Candidate workflow
1. **Shift intake:** review prior handoff, work orders, active holds, alarms, room status, and scheduled tasks.
2. **Prioritize:** identify safety, crop-protection, quality, and traceability priorities with justification.
3. **Diagnose:** perform a structured crop/environment workup and verify data quality before major corrective action.
4. **Irrigation/fertigation:** execute or verify the assigned process and investigate abnormal root-zone/delivery evidence.
5. **IPM:** interpret repeated scouting/intervention evidence and define follow-up or escalation.
6. **Deviation/traceability:** contain the issue, establish facts, preserve genealogy/records, and prepare affected scope.
7. **Metrics/peer support:** calculate selected operational metrics and provide one SOP-based peer task correction without acting as a formal supervisor.
8. **Handoff:** produce a supervisor-ready report covering completed actions, unresolved risks, evidence, affected IDs, decisions requiring authorization, and recommended next checks.

## Scoring
Safety/critical prioritization 15; crop/environment diagnosis 15; irrigation/fertigation/root-zone reasoning 15; IPM interpretation 10; deviation/traceability integrity 15; metrics/data interpretation 10; peer support/role boundary 5; documentation/final handoff 15.

**Pass:** 80/100 overall and no critical error.

## Critical errors
Knowingly unsafe action or unauthorized high-risk repair/chemical application; deliberate fabrication/deletion/falsification of traceability or quality evidence; release/disposition outside authority; ignoring a clearly identified urgent safety/biosecurity/quality containment condition; taking major corrective action from data known to be invalid without verification.

## Portfolio output
Senior Technician Diagnostic Shift Report, priority log, crop diagnostic workup, root-zone/fertigation analysis, IPM follow-up note, deviation/traceability package, KPI summary, and supervisor-ready handoff.

## Delivery modes
THC Virtual Cultivation Facility, supervised practical lab, or approved workplace-equivalent assessment using standardized evidence and scoring.
