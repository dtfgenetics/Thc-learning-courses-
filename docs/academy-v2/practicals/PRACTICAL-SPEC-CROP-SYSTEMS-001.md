# Grow-Space Systems and Crop Planning Specialist Practical

**Credential:** THC Grow-Space Systems and Crop Planning Certificate  
**Competencies:** `COMP-SPACE-BIOSEC-001`, `COMP-CROP-PLAN-001`

## Purpose

Demonstrate that the candidate can turn facility constraints, biosecurity requirements, genetic-source information and production targets into a traceable crop plan that is operationally feasible.

## Candidate scenario

A small multi-room facility is preparing an eight-week production plan. The candidate receives room capacities, propagation output, flowering-space availability, sanitation turnaround times, quarantine rules, cultivar/lot inventory, expected transition windows, labor constraints and a target harvest cadence. One planned transfer conflicts with quarantine flow and another exceeds available finishing capacity.

## Candidate tasks

1. Define clean, transition, production, postharvest and quarantine zones.
2. Map personnel, plant, tool and waste movement to minimize preventable cross-zone risk.
3. Calculate usable capacity by stage rather than using nominal room size alone.
4. Reconcile propagation output with veg/flower capacity and turnover time.
5. Build a staged crop schedule with source identity, planned transitions and destination rooms.
6. Identify bottlenecks and distinguish hard constraints from adjustable scheduling assumptions.
7. Preserve genetic-source and lot genealogy through every planned transfer.
8. Identify where sanitation turnaround, room reset or equipment availability changes the schedule.
9. Respond to an assessor inject such as delayed rooting, room downtime or rejected incoming material.
10. Revise the plan without losing traceability or exceeding downstream capacity.
11. Produce a final plan with assumptions, risks, contingencies and required records.

## Evidence packet

- facility map
- room/stage capacities
- sanitation and reset times
- propagation success/output history
- cultivar/source inventory
- quarantine requirements
- labor/equipment availability
- target crop cadence
- historical deviation note

## Required calculations

- stage capacity and utilization
- expected plant flow by week
- propagation-to-flower demand reconciliation
- buffer capacity
- effect of downtime or delayed establishment on later stages

## Evidence injects

### Inject A — delayed propagation
One cultivar roots seven days later than planned. Candidate must revise downstream transitions without overloading veg space.

### Inject B — room outage
A flowering room loses one cycle of availability. Candidate must identify which batches move, hold or are rescheduled and preserve lot genealogy.

### Inject C — quarantine rejection
Incoming material fails intake criteria. Candidate must keep it out of normal production flow and revise the plan from verified available stock.

## Required outputs

- zoning/biosecurity flow map
- capacity worksheet
- eight-week crop schedule
- transfer and lot genealogy table
- bottleneck/constraint register
- revised plan after inject
- final planning handoff

## Critical errors

- uncontrolled contamination pathway designed into normal workflow
- hard capacity limit knowingly exceeded without identifying it
- source/lot identity lost in the plan
- supplied planning evidence falsified or altered
