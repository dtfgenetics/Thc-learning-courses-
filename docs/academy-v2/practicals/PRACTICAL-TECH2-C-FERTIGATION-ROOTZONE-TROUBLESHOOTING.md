# Technician II Practical C — Fertigation & Root-Zone Troubleshooting

## Purpose
Assess approved fertigation-batch execution together with root-zone and irrigation troubleshooting.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-FERTIGATION-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-ROOTZONE-001`
- `COMP-WATER-QUALITY-ADV-001`
- `COMP-IRRIGATION-ADV-001`
- `COMP-NUTRIENT-DIAG-001`
- `COMP-PRO-SOP-001`

## Part 1 — Batch verification
Candidate receives an approved recipe, source-water data, material identities, target final volume, expected EC/pH range, and batch record. Candidate verifies identity, calculations, sequence, final measurements, and accept/hold/escalate disposition within role authority.

## Part 2 — Root-zone investigation
Candidate receives multi-day irrigation timestamps/volumes, representative moisture trends, root-zone EC data, emitter-output checks, crop stage/environment context, and plant observations. Candidate distinguishes likely delivery, timing, accumulation, measurement, and plant-demand issues and selects the next verification step.

## Critical errors
Unapproved recipe/chemical change; incorrect material identity ignored; unsafe chemical handling; acting on an abnormal measurement without required verification; assuming a universal dryback target without crop/media context.

## Scoring
Batch preparation/verification 30; measurement quality 15; root-zone interpretation 20; distribution/scheduling reasoning 20; records/escalation 15.

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
Completed batch record, measurement-verification record, root-zone/irrigation diagnostic memo, and next-check/correction recommendation.
