# Technician II Practical C — Fertigation & Root-Zone Troubleshooting

## Purpose
Assess whether the candidate can execute an approved solution-preparation work order, verify measurement quality, interpret irrigation/root-zone evidence, distinguish likely failure modes, and produce a defensible handoff without exceeding assigned authority.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-FERTIGATION-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-ROOTZONE-001`
- `COMP-WATER-QUALITY-ADV-001`
- `COMP-IRRIGATION-ADV-001`
- `COMP-NUTRIENT-DIAG-001`
- `COMP-PRO-SOP-001`
- `COMP-SAFETY-WORK-001`

## Candidate receives
An approved work order, source-water data, material identities, target final volume, expected measurement ranges, device-verification records, a blank batch record, multi-day irrigation timestamps/volumes, representative root-zone moisture trends, root-zone EC data, emitter-output checks, environmental context and crop observations.

## Part 1 — Work-order and batch verification
Candidate must:
1. Confirm the work order, material identities, units and target final volume.
2. Verify measurement-device status before relying on final readings.
3. Complete required calculations and document each one clearly.
4. Follow the approved sequence without introducing an unapproved change.
5. Record actual final measurements and compare them with the work-order acceptance range.
6. Select accept, hold or escalate within role authority and document the reason.

## Part 2 — Irrigation and root-zone investigation
Candidate must:
1. Build a timeline from irrigation events, measurements and observations.
2. Check whether abnormal readings are supported by verified instruments.
3. Compare representative emitter-output evidence rather than assuming nominal output.
4. Interpret root-zone moisture/dryback trends in the context of media, container, stage and environmental demand.
5. Evaluate root-zone EC and drainage/return evidence without assuming feed EC describes the whole system.
6. Classify the leading problem as measurement, distribution, timing, accumulation, source-water, plant-demand or unresolved.
7. Identify the strongest next verification step before recommending a correction.
8. Separate measured facts, hypotheses and uncertainty in the final handoff.

## Role boundary
The candidate may execute approved work, verify measurements, identify likely irrigation/root-zone faults, perform permitted routine checks and recommend next verification or escalation. The practical does not authorize unapproved recipe changes, unsafe handling, plumbing/electrical repair, bypass of controls or facility-level system redesign.

## Critical errors
- Makes an unapproved formulation or material change.
- Ignores an incorrect or incompatible material identity.
- Uses unsafe handling practices.
- Acts on an abnormal measurement without required verification.
- Assumes a universal dryback target without media, container, stage and measurement context.
- Changes multiple variables at once in a way that prevents meaningful verification.
- Represents an unresolved hypothesis as confirmed fact.

## Scoring
- Work-order and batch preparation/verification: 30 points
- Measurement quality: 15 points
- Root-zone trend interpretation: 20 points
- Distribution and scheduling reasoning: 20 points
- Records, uncertainty and escalation: 15 points

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
- Completed batch/work-order record
- Measurement-verification record
- Root-zone and irrigation diagnostic memo
- Annotated timeline or trend table
- Next-check or correction recommendation
- Supervisor-ready handoff

## Assessor guidance
Score the evidence trail, not whether the candidate selects the assessor's preferred hypothesis. Full credit requires verified measurements, a coherent timeline, correct separation of facts from interpretations, attention to spatial/distribution evidence, and a next check that meaningfully reduces uncertainty before major changes are recommended.

## Pilot / calibration requirement
Before credential authorization, run multiple pilot cases with at least two assessors, compare scoring consistency, identify ambiguous criteria and revise the evidence packet/rubric before production release.
