# PRACTICAL-EIFS-008-COMMISSIONING-FAILURE-RESPONSE

## Purpose

This development practical evaluates whether an EIFS learner can commission and troubleshoot an automated cultivation-system function without crossing safety or authorization boundaries.

It is **not** a live credential form and does not authorize controller-program changes, electrical work, compressed-gas service, pressure-system repair, alarm/interlock bypass or credential issuance.

## Candidate scenario

The candidate receives a staged environmental/irrigation automation system containing:
- at least two sensors;
- one controlled environmental output;
- one controlled irrigation output;
- one alarm or interlock;
- one intentionally introduced data, configuration, equipment-response or capacity fault;
- controller/alarm history;
- representative crop-zone or delivery measurements.

## Required outputs

1. **Commissioning matrix** — system ID, input, command, expected response, actual response, representative measurement, alarm/interlock behavior and acceptance criterion.
2. **Failure classification** — separate sensor/data-path, command/configuration, equipment-response, distribution and capacity hypotheses.
3. **Safe-state plan** — identify immediate life-safety/crop-protection action, degraded-mode controls, monitoring frequency, stop criteria and responsible role.
4. **Recovery verification** — repeat the failed test and relevant regression checks after authorized correction.
5. **Qualified handoff packet** — timeline, evidence, temporary controls, recovery status, residual risk and next responsible role.

## Development scoring

| Domain | Points |
|---|---:|
| Commissioning plan and acceptance criteria | 15 |
| Command-response and data-path verification | 20 |
| Alarm/interlock/fail-safe reasoning | 15 |
| Failure classification and temporary controls | 15 |
| Recovery/regression verification | 15 |
| Evidence integrity and uncertainty | 10 |
| Qualified handoff | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

The 80-point target is developmental only and is not a validated credential cut score.

## Critical failures

The performance cannot be used as credential evidence if the candidate:
- bypasses or directs bypass of a life-safety alarm, interlock or safeguard;
- performs/directs unauthorized electrical, controls, compressed-gas, pressure-system or protected equipment service;
- fabricates/deletes/misrepresents commissioning or recovery evidence;
- declares a material fault restored without the required recovery verification.

## Calibration boundary

Operational use requires human technical review, assessor calibration, accessibility review, controlled pilot evidence and formal release approval.
