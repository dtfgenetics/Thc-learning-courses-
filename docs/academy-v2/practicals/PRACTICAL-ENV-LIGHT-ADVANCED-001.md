# Technician II Practical — Environment & Lighting Verification

## Purpose
Demonstrate that the candidate can verify environmental and lighting measurements, identify spatial nonuniformity, distinguish likely measurement faults from real room/canopy conditions, and produce a defensible escalation or correction recommendation without exceeding assigned authority.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-SENSOR-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-EQUIPMENT-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-LIGHTING-001`
- `COMP-ENV-ADV-001`
- `COMP-LIGHT-ADV-001`

## Candidate receives
A controlled-environment room diagram, sensor locations, temperature/RH trend data, controller state, selected calibration/verification records, fixture-state information, photoperiod schedule, a top-canopy PPFD grid, a mid-canopy PPFD grid, and a short crop-observation summary.

## Candidate must
1. Check measurement timestamps, units, sensor position/orientation and available verification/calibration history.
2. Identify any suspect sensor or data-quality condition before interpreting the room.
3. Describe the environmental spatial pattern using the supplied map/trend data.
4. Distinguish likely local airflow/mixing, heat/moisture-load, sensor, controller or equipment-response problems.
5. Calculate or interpret DLI when the supplied PPFD/photoperiod data permit it.
6. Evaluate top- and mid-canopy PPFD distribution using average, minimum, maximum and spatial variation.
7. Identify where a single center PPFD value would misrepresent crop exposure.
8. Compare measured fixture state/output with the expected work instruction.
9. State the strongest next verification step before recommending a system change.
10. Produce a supervisor-ready handoff separating measured facts, hypotheses, confidence limits, permitted actions and escalation items.

## Role boundary
The candidate may verify measurements, identify likely data/equipment faults, perform approved routine checks and recommend next verification steps. The practical does not authorize electrical repair, safety-control bypass, unrestricted controller changes, facility redesign or other work outside assigned authorization.

## Critical errors
- Bypasses a safety boundary or attempts an unauthorized repair.
- Changes controller/fixture settings primarily to hide a failed measurement or equipment condition.
- Treats one unverified sensor or one center PPFD reading as definitive room-wide evidence when contradictory data are supplied.
- Discards contradictory data without documenting why it is invalid.
- Recommends a major control change without first resolving a clear measurement-quality problem.
- Represents a hypothesis as confirmed fact when the supplied evidence is insufficient.

## Scoring
- Measurement and sensor-quality verification: 20 points
- Environmental spatial/trend interpretation: 20 points
- PPFD/DLI and light-distribution analysis: 25 points
- Fault classification and next-test selection: 15 points
- Role boundary and safety: 10 points
- Documentation and supervisor handoff: 10 points

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
- Environment & Lighting Verification Report
- Annotated sensor/environment map
- Top/mid-canopy PPFD analysis
- Fault classification and next-test plan
- Supervisor-ready handoff

## Assessor notes
Score the evidence trail, not whether the candidate happens to choose the same hypothesis as the assessor. Full credit requires the candidate to verify measurement quality, use spatial evidence, preserve uncertainty and choose an efficient next check before recommending a major system change.

## Pilot / calibration requirement
Before credential use, run pilot cases with multiple assessors and compare scoring consistency. Revise ambiguous evidence packets or rubric language before authorization.
