# Professional SOP and Quality Systems Specialist Practical

**Credential:** THC Professional Cultivation SOP and Quality Systems Certificate  
**Competencies:** `COMP-PRO-SOP-001`, `COMP-PRO-QA-001`

## Purpose

Assess whether the candidate can convert cultivation expectations into controlled, executable procedures and manage training, change control, deviations, CAPA and audit evidence as a connected quality system.

## Scenario

A cultivation department has an SOP that says staff should “check the room regularly,” but it does not define responsibility, frequency, acceptance criteria, records or exception handling. An equipment change was introduced informally, the same deviation has occurred three times, and training records show attendance but not demonstrated competence. An internal audit is scheduled.

## Candidate tasks

1. Identify what makes the existing SOP non-executable or non-auditable.
2. Rewrite the critical workflow into clear responsibilities, sequence, acceptance criteria, required evidence and exception/escalation paths.
3. Define document identification, versioning, approval and effective-date controls.
4. Design a training and qualification plan that distinguishes knowledge training from demonstrated task competence.
5. Create a controlled change record for the equipment/process change, including rationale, risk, affected documents, training and verification.
6. Investigate the recurring deviation using objective evidence and distinguish immediate correction from root-cause work.
7. Build a CAPA plan tied to the likely cause and define measurable effectiveness checks.
8. Review a sample of records for completeness, traceability and contemporaneous documentation.
9. Conduct a focused mock audit and classify findings by evidence and system impact.
10. Produce an executive-quality handoff summarizing open risks, owners and due actions.

## Evidence packet

- flawed SOP excerpt
- work records with inconsistent completion
- training attendance records
- equipment-change email
- three deviation records
- sample production logs
- prior corrective action
- audit checklist

## Evidence injects

### Inject A — repeat deviation
A third recurrence appears after a prior action was marked complete. Candidate should challenge CAPA effectiveness rather than simply reopen the same correction.

### Inject B — training gap
A worker attended training but cannot perform a critical step consistently. Candidate should distinguish attendance from qualification.

### Inject C — undocumented change
A supervisor reports that staff have been following a modified process for two weeks without controlled documentation. Candidate should preserve evidence, assess impact and route formal change/deviation handling.

## Required outputs

- revised SOP section
- training/qualification matrix
- change-control record
- deviation investigation
- CAPA plan with effectiveness criteria
- focused internal audit report
- management handoff
