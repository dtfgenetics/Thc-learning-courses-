# Practical D — Propagation Workstation

**Credential:** THC Cultivation Technician I  
**Mode:** simulated workstation, supervised propagation lab, or approved workplace equivalent  
**Primary tasks:** `TASK-CULT-PROP-001`, `TASK-CULT-SANITATION-001`, `TASK-CULT-RECORDS-001`

## Purpose

This practical tests whether a candidate can execute a routine propagation work order while protecting donor identity, sanitation, consistency, rooting-environment records and count reconciliation.

## Candidate packet

The candidate receives:
- propagation work order
- donor/cultivar IDs and acceptance criteria
- sanitation SOP
- tray map and labels
- media/rooting instructions
- environmental set-point/monitoring instructions
- acclimatization plan
- batch record

## Seeded conditions

Rotate conditions such as:
- one donor with suspicious symptoms
- one unlabeled or ambiguous tray position
- one sanitation break between donors
- one rooting tray running too wet or too dry
- one cultivar rooting slower than another
- mismatch between started and handed-off counts
- abrupt acclimatization response

## Candidate tasks

1. Verify donor identity, cultivar, target count and work order.
2. Prepare a clean workstation and stage labels before cutting.
3. Inspect donor material and flag anything outside acceptance criteria.
4. Sequence tool sanitation correctly.
5. Execute a simulated cutting/placement workflow.
6. Maintain unambiguous tray/lot identity.
7. Interpret supplied rooting-environment and progress data.
8. Identify a batch exception without redesigning the protocol.
9. Reconcile started, rooted, lost and handed-off counts.
10. Complete the propagation batch record and handoff.

## Expected evidence

- clean workstation setup
- donor acceptance decisions
- tool sanitation sequence
- tray labeling/map
- rooting-progress interpretation
- count reconciliation
- deviation/escalation note
- completed batch record

## Scoring model — 100 points

- work-order/donor verification: 15
- sanitation: 20
- propagation sequence consistency: 20
- labeling/traceability: 15
- rooting/acclimatization interpretation: 10
- count reconciliation: 10
- documentation/escalation: 10

**Target pass:** 80/100.

## Strong-performance indicators

- refuses to guess identity when labels fail
- does not create a new batch from visibly questionable donor material without escalation
- treats sanitation as part of the workflow, not an optional cleanup step
- records genotype-specific rooting timing rather than assuming all cultivars behave identically
- reconciles production counts accurately

## Deliverables

1. workstation setup checklist
2. tray map
3. propagation batch record
4. rooting-performance summary
5. deviation/handoff note

## Portfolio use

The completed simulated batch record and rooting-performance summary demonstrate controlled propagation execution and production-record literacy to an employer.
