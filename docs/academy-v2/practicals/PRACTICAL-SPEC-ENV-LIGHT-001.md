# Advanced Environment and Lighting Specialist Practical

**Credential:** THC Advanced Environment and Lighting Certificate  
**Competencies:** `COMP-ENV-ADV-001`, `COMP-LIGHT-ADV-001`

## Purpose

Assess the candidate's ability to integrate sensor quality, spatial environmental conditions, airflow, heat/moisture loads and light quantity/distribution into a defensible production decision.

## Candidate scenario

A flowering room reports acceptable controller averages but uneven plant performance. The candidate receives time-series temperature/RH data, canopy and wall sensor locations, calibration/verification history, PPFD mapping, photoperiod schedule, fixture layout, irrigation timing, HVAC/dehumidification events and a room map. One sensor is biased, one zone has a repeatable lights-off humidity excursion, and one canopy band receives substantially different light dose.

## Candidate tasks

1. Verify sensor identities, locations and recent verification/calibration evidence.
2. Identify suspect measurements and explain whether they should be excluded, corrected, or independently verified.
3. Build a spatial environment interpretation rather than relying on room averages.
4. Calculate DLI from supplied PPFD/photoperiod values where required.
5. Evaluate PPFD uniformity and identify whether geometry, fixture output, canopy height or measurement placement could explain the pattern.
6. Relate lights-on/lights-off transitions to sensible and latent load changes.
7. Interpret airflow evidence and identify stagnant or short-circuit patterns.
8. Rank at least three hypotheses for the crop-performance pattern.
9. Choose the smallest set of verification checks that can discriminate among those hypotheses.
10. Recommend an integrated response that distinguishes localized correction from room-wide control changes.
11. Define success metrics and a follow-up verification window.

## Assessor evidence packet

- room map with sensor/fixture locations
- 24–72 h temperature/RH time series
- sensor verification history
- canopy/leaf-temperature spot checks
- PPFD grid
- fixture power/dimming schedule
- photoperiod
- airflow visualization or velocity observations
- equipment event log
- irrigation event timing
- plant-performance map

## Calculations

Candidate should be prepared to:
- convert PPFD × photoperiod to DLI
- compare average/minimum/maximum PPFD
- calculate percent deviation from a reference sensor
- compare event timing across environment, irrigation and lighting records

## Mid-assessment inject

Provide one of:
- a colocated reference sensor showing one probe is biased
- a fixture-output verification showing one zone is under-delivering
- an airflow smoke-test result showing recirculation short-circuiting

Candidate must update the conclusion and document why.

## Required outputs

- sensor-quality table
- spatial environment map
- PPFD/DLI worksheet
- airflow/load diagnosis
- ranked differential
- integrated recommendation
- verification and handoff plan

## Scoring — 100 points

| Domain | Points |
|---|---:|
| Sensor and measurement quality | 15 |
| Spatial environment interpretation | 20 |
| Light quantity, distribution and dose analysis | 20 |
| Airflow and environmental load diagnosis | 20 |
| Integrated control recommendation | 15 |
| Verification plan and documentation | 10 |

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Critical errors

- major control decision based on a known-unverified sensor
- materially incorrect interpretation of PPFD/photoperiod/DLI
- room-wide correction recommended despite clear localized evidence without justification
- contradictory data intentionally discarded or falsified
