# Advanced Canopy and Flowering Specialist Practical

**Credential:** THC Advanced Canopy, Flowering and Reproductive Biology Certificate  
**Role context:** Cultivation Technician II / Cultivation Specialist  
**Competencies:** `COMP-CANOPY-ADV-001`, `COMP-FLOWER-ADV-001`

## Purpose

Demonstrate that the candidate can evaluate canopy architecture and flowering development as connected production systems rather than as isolated visual traits. The candidate must use spatial measurements, plant observations, reproductive-development evidence and representative sampling to make a defensible recommendation.

## Candidate scenario

The candidate receives a flowering-room case with uneven canopy height, a PPFD map, canopy temperature/RH observations, plant images from upper/interior/lower positions, cultivar identity, training history, flowering-day records and representative trichome images. The room average appears acceptable, but one zone shows lower flower development and another zone has excessive upper-canopy load.

A second evidence packet is introduced during the assessment showing either a pollen-risk observation, a late-stage stress response, or contradictory maturity evidence. The candidate must update the workup rather than defend the first conclusion.

## Candidate tasks

1. Confirm room, cultivar, stage and sampling locations before interpreting results.
2. Build a canopy architecture map identifying height, density and light-distribution patterns.
3. Compare canopy geometry with PPFD, temperature/RH and flower-development evidence.
4. Identify at least three plausible contributors to the observed nonuniformity and rank them by evidence.
5. Distinguish structural canopy issues from environmental, developmental and measurement-quality issues.
6. Evaluate whether additional training or tissue removal is appropriate for the current stage and explain tradeoffs.
7. Interpret reproductive-development evidence and identify any pollination/sex-expression concern requiring escalation.
8. Design a representative maturity-sampling plan spanning meaningful canopy positions.
9. Produce a crop-level harvest-readiness conclusion that states uncertainty and does not rely on one trichome image or a universal color percentage.
10. Recommend the smallest useful corrective action or verification step and identify what should not be changed simultaneously.
11. Complete a specialist handoff report with evidence, conclusion, remaining uncertainty and next action.

## Evidence packet

The assessor should provide:
- room and bench map
- cultivar and batch identifiers
- canopy-height measurements
- PPFD grid or equivalent light-distribution data
- canopy/leaf temperature and RH observations
- before/after training notes
- representative flower-development images
- upper/interior/lower maturity images
- a short event history covering irrigation, environmental alarms and recent canopy work
- one mid-assessment evidence inject

## Evidence inject options

### Inject A — reproductive risk
A suspicious floral structure is found on two plants in one corner. Candidate should isolate the observation from a confirmed diagnosis, protect adjacent crop, document location and escalate for verification.

### Inject B — sensor conflict
One canopy sensor is substantially different from adjacent measurements. Candidate should verify placement/calibration context before using it to justify a room-wide change.

### Inject C — maturity conflict
Top flowers appear advanced while lower/interior samples remain substantially less mature. Candidate should revise sampling and avoid a single-position harvest call.

## Required outputs

- annotated canopy architecture map
- evidence table linking observations to hypotheses
- uniformity diagnosis
- flowering/reproductive-risk note
- maturity sampling plan
- corrective action or verification recommendation
- final specialist handoff

## Scoring rubric — 100 points

| Domain | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Canopy evidence collection and mapping | 20 | Correct identity/stage, representative positions, accurate mapping and measurement context |
| Architecture and uniformity diagnosis | 20 | Connects height/density/light/microclimate patterns without overclaiming cause |
| Flowering and reproductive risk interpretation | 20 | Correctly interprets stage, flags supplied reproductive risk and separates evidence from diagnosis |
| Maturity sampling and harvest decision logic | 15 | Uses representative sampling and a crop-level conclusion with uncertainty |
| Corrective action and tradeoff reasoning | 15 | Chooses a targeted action/test, explains timing and avoids unnecessary simultaneous changes |
| Documentation and handoff | 10 | Traceable, concise record with locations, evidence, uncertainty and next action |

**Passing standard:** 80/100 with no critical error.

## Critical errors

- irreversible canopy/harvest action recommended without verifying identity, stage or representative evidence
- one flower or sensor treated as conclusive despite contradictory spatial evidence
- supplied reproductive/contamination risk ignored when escalation is required
- measurement or observation evidence fabricated or altered

## Portfolio artifact

**Advanced Canopy and Flowering Diagnostic Packet** containing the annotated map, evidence matrix, maturity plan and final specialist handoff.
