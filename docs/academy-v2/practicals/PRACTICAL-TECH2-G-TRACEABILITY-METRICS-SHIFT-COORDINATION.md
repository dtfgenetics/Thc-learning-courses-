# Technician II Practical G — Traceability, Metrics & Shift Coordination

## Purpose
Assess operational data integrity, metric interpretation, peer task support, and supervisor-ready handoff.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-TRACE-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-METRICS-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-PEER-001`
- `COMP-PRO-SOP-001`
- `COMP-PRO-QA-001`
- `COMP-CROP-PLAN-001`

## Candidate receives
A mixed operational packet with physical crop/lot counts, electronic-style records, genealogy events, planned/actual dates, production figures, task status, and a peer handoff containing at least two inconsistencies.

## Candidate must
Reconcile physical and recorded identities/status; preserve genealogy through split/move/harvest transitions; identify missing, duplicate, or mismatched records without inventing corrections; calculate selected KPIs; distinguish meaningful trend from one-cycle noise; explain an SOP-based task correction to a peer; produce a shift priority and supervisor-ready handoff.

## Critical errors
Fabricating a record; overwriting planned history instead of preserving planned vs actual; unauthorized controlled-record changes; using an incorrect KPI denominator after the definition is provided.

## Scoring
Reconciliation/genealogy 25; KPI calculation/interpretation 25; data integrity 20; peer support 15; final handoff 15.

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
Traceability Reconciliation, KPI worksheet, peer coaching note, and Senior Technician Shift Handoff.
