# Post-Harvest Quality Assurance Specialist Practical

**Credential:** THC Post-Harvest Quality Assurance Certificate  
**Competencies:** `COMP-POSTHARVEST-PROCESS-001`, `COMP-POSTHARVEST-QA-001`

## Purpose

Assess the candidate's ability to manage maturity evidence, drying/storage trends, representative sampling, traceability, deviations and disposition as one controlled post-harvest quality system.

## Scenario

A harvested lot is split across two drying zones. One zone experiences a humidity excursion and a sensor gap. Later samples show different moisture/water-activity behavior across positions. Transfer records contain one identifier mismatch, and a release decision is approaching. The candidate must determine affected scope, protect identity, preserve evidence and recommend the next defensible action.

## Candidate tasks

1. Review representative pre-harvest maturity evidence and explain sampling limitations.
2. Reconstruct harvest-to-dry-room lot genealogy.
3. Evaluate drying temperature/RH and product-condition trends across zones.
4. Identify data gaps and determine whether they invalidate or merely limit interpretation.
5. Build a representative sampling plan for the potentially affected lot.
6. Establish hold/segregation boundaries while identity or quality questions remain unresolved.
7. Investigate the identifier discrepancy without overwriting the original record.
8. Create a deviation statement describing what happened, when, where and what evidence is missing.
9. Rank plausible causes and contributing factors.
10. Recommend corrective/preventive actions tied to the investigated cause.
11. Define effectiveness checks before CAPA closure.
12. Produce a lot disposition recommendation: release, continue hold, rework where authorized, or escalate.

## Evidence packet

- maturity sampling images/records
- harvest and transfer records
- drying-zone sensor history
- product-condition measurements
- sampling locations
- lot genealogy identifiers
- storage/packaging records
- deviation history

## Evidence injects

### Inject A — traceability mismatch
One tote label conflicts with the electronic transfer record. Candidate should quarantine/hold the ambiguous material and reconstruct identity before normal flow.

### Inject B — sensor gap
A drying sensor has a four-hour data gap. Candidate should use corroborating evidence where available and document remaining uncertainty.

### Inject C — heterogeneous sample
One sample meets a quality criterion while another from the same lot does not. Candidate should not average away meaningful heterogeneity without a defensible sampling plan.

## Required outputs

- maturity sampling interpretation
- lot genealogy map
- drying trend worksheet
- hold/sampling plan
- deviation and cause analysis
- CAPA/effectiveness plan
- disposition recommendation
