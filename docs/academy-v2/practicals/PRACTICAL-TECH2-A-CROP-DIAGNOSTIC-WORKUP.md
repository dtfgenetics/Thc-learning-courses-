# Technician II Practical A — Crop Diagnostic Workup

## Purpose
Demonstrate that a senior cultivation technician can turn incomplete crop, environment, irrigation, and work-history evidence into a defensible diagnostic workup without overclaiming certainty.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-DIAG-001`
- `COMP-ENV-ADV-001`
- supporting plant-biology, root-zone, nutrition, and professional-QA competencies

## Delivery modes
Simulation, supervised lab, or approved workplace-equivalent case.

## Candidate receives
Room/bench map; crop/cultivar/stage IDs; plant images and observations; 48–72 hours of temperature/RH/VPD trends; irrigation and root-zone history; recent work/change log; selected sensor-verification records. At least one evidence stream is incomplete or mildly contradictory.

## Candidate must
1. Define the problem by location, tissue, timing, scope, and observed condition.
2. Separate observations from hypotheses.
3. Identify the dominant spatial/temporal pattern.
4. Verify whether surprising data are trustworthy.
5. Rank at least three plausible hypotheses.
6. Identify evidence for and against each.
7. Choose the smallest useful next verification step.
8. Recommend an SOP-authorized routine action or escalation.
9. Document uncertainty explicitly.

## Critical errors
Treating an unverified single reading as proof; changing multiple major systems without diagnostic reason; presenting a hypothesis as confirmed cause; ignoring an urgent safety/crop-protection escalation; altering records to fit the case.

## Scoring
Problem definition 15; pattern/timing 15; data quality 15; hypothesis quality 20; next-test selection 15; action/escalation 10; documentation 10.

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
Crop Diagnostic Workup, ranked-hypothesis table, next-verification plan, and supervisor-ready summary.
