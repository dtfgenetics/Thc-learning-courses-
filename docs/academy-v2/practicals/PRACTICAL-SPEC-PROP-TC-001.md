# Advanced Propagation and Tissue Culture Specialist Practical

**Credential:** THC Advanced Propagation and Tissue Culture Certificate  
**Competencies:** `COMP-TC-ASEPTIC-001`, `COMP-TC-REGEN-001`

## Purpose

Assess the candidate's ability to manage aseptic establishment, contamination control, multiplication, rooting, acclimatization, passage history, identity and fidelity evidence as one propagation system.

## Scenario

A tissue-culture line has increasing contamination during establishment, uneven multiplication after several passages and higher losses during acclimatization. Vessel sterilization records are acceptable, but operator workflow and explant preparation differ between shifts. One source-line label also conflicts with a passage record.

## Candidate tasks

1. Reconstruct source identity and passage history before making culture decisions.
2. Map the aseptic workflow from source material through vessel closure and identify exposure points.
3. Separate contamination prevention, detection, segregation and investigation steps.
4. Review establishment success by source and operator and identify meaningful patterns.
5. Calculate multiplication factor from supplied starting and usable-shoot counts.
6. Compare multiplication performance across passages without assuming faster multiplication means better fidelity.
7. Evaluate rooting and acclimatization losses using stage-specific evidence.
8. Identify where environmental transition, root development or handling could contribute to losses.
9. Resolve the label/passage discrepancy using traceable records; quarantine ambiguous material until identity is established.
10. Define a fit-for-purpose fidelity plan using provenance, passage history, phenotype and appropriate additional testing rather than appearance alone.
11. Recommend targeted process corrections and metrics for the next production cycle.

## Evidence packet

- source-line intake records
- explant preparation procedure
- operator workflow observations
- contamination counts by batch/operator
- vessel sterilization records
- multiplication and rooting counts
- passage history
- acclimatization survival data
- label discrepancy

## Calculations

Candidate should calculate:
- contamination percentage
- establishment success percentage
- multiplication factor
- rooting percentage
- acclimatization survival percentage
- cumulative loss across stages when supplied sufficient data

## Evidence injects

### Inject A — contamination cluster
Contamination clusters around one handling step and operator shift. Candidate should focus investigation on the process point rather than blaming the genotype without evidence.

### Inject B — identity conflict
A vessel label does not match the electronic passage record. Candidate should hold the line from further normal use until identity is resolved.

### Inject C — visual uniformity
Plants appear uniform after many passages but have incomplete provenance. Candidate should explain why appearance alone cannot establish fidelity.

## Required outputs

- aseptic workflow risk map
- contamination investigation
- establishment/multiplication/rooting metrics
- acclimatization-loss analysis
- identity/passage reconciliation
- fidelity monitoring plan
- corrective-action handoff
