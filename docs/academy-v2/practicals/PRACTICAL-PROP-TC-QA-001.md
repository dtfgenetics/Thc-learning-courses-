# Advanced Propagation and Tissue Culture Practical — QA Review

## Purpose
Demonstrate that the candidate can review an aseptic propagation workflow, interpret culture-establishment and contamination evidence, compare multiplication/acclimatization outcomes, evaluate germplasm-quality risk, and produce a traceable corrective-action handoff without overstating certainty.

## Competencies
- `COMP-TC-ASEPTIC-001`
- `COMP-TC-REGEN-001`

## Candidate receives
A clean-work process map, donor/explant identities, operator and transfer records, medium lots, contamination timing, establishment outcomes, genotype-by-treatment multiplication data, acclimatization/survival records, passage history, morphology observations, and a retained-reference comparison.

## Candidate must
1. Map clean/dirty workflow boundaries and identify likely cross-contamination opportunities.
2. Verify donor, explant, genotype, medium-lot and operator traceability.
3. Separate visible contamination observations from suspected source and confirmed cause.
4. Use timing and clustering evidence to rank likely contamination contributors.
5. Compare establishment and multiplication outcomes by genotype and treatment.
6. Evaluate multiplication quality using more than shoot count alone.
7. Interpret rooting/acclimatization as a connected performance transition.
8. Review passage history and regeneration route when evaluating germplasm-quality risk.
9. Recommend the strongest next verification or corrective action without changing several variables at once.
10. Produce a supervisor-ready QA handoff that clearly labels facts, hypotheses, uncertainty and escalation items.

## Critical errors
- Opens or handles known contaminated material in a way that breaches the defined clean-work boundary.
- Discards or alters original batch records to make the process appear cleaner than it was.
- Declares a donor, operator, medium lot or genotype to be the confirmed cause without supporting evidence.
- Treats one genotype response as a universal propagation protocol.
- Ignores passage history or obvious abnormal morphology when making a germplasm-quality disposition.
- Changes several process variables simultaneously while claiming the result identifies root cause.
- Represents a QA hypothesis as a validated genetic or pathogen diagnosis.

## Scoring
- Aseptic workflow and traceability: 20 points
- Contamination-pattern analysis: 20 points
- Establishment/multiplication interpretation: 20 points
- Rooting/acclimatization and fidelity QA: 20 points
- Corrective action, uncertainty and handoff: 20 points

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
- Aseptic workflow risk map
- Culture-establishment and contamination review
- Multiplication/acclimatization comparison table
- Germplasm QA and corrective-action report
- Supervisor-ready handoff

## Assessor guidance
Score the evidence trail and reasoning quality rather than agreement with one preferred hypothesis. Full credit requires traceable use of batch data, preservation of genotype and treatment context, explicit uncertainty, and a next step that reduces uncertainty without destroying interpretability.

## Pilot / calibration requirement
Before credential authorization, run at least two contrasting case packets with multiple assessors. Compare scoring consistency, revise ambiguous rubric language, and document which evidence is necessary for each major conclusion.
