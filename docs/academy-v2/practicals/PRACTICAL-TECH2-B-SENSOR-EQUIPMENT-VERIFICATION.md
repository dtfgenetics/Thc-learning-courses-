# Technician II Practical B — Sensor & Equipment Verification

## Purpose
Demonstrate that the candidate can distinguish a real environmental condition from sensor/data-quality error, equipment-response failure, control-sequence problems, or a condition requiring maintenance escalation.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-SENSOR-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-EQUIPMENT-001`
- `COMP-ENV-ADV-001`
- `COMP-IRRIGATION-ADV-001`
- `COMP-PRO-QA-001`

## Candidate receives
Controller trends, canopy sensors, equipment-command history, alarms, maintenance notes, and crop observations.

## Candidate must
Verify placement/timestamps; compare colocated sensors; review verification/calibration status; distinguish controller command from equipment response; identify gradients/logging gaps; perform only permitted routine checks; classify the issue as measurement, control sequence, equipment output, or unresolved; create a maintenance/escalation record.

## Critical errors
Unsafe bypass or repair; unauthorized repair; changing setpoints to hide a failed sensor/equipment condition; discarding inconvenient data without justification.

## Scoring
Sensor/data quality 25; controller/equipment response 25; safe troubleshooting 20; spatial/trend interpretation 15; escalation/documentation 15.

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
Sensor & Equipment Verification Report, annotated trend/data table, fault classification, and maintenance handoff.
