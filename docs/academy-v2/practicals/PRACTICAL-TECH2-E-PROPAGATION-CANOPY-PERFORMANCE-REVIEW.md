# Technician II Practical E — Propagation / Canopy Performance Review

## Purpose
Assess performance troubleshooting in one generated primary domain while the other appears as a secondary scenario.

## Tasks / competencies
- `TASK-CULT2-PROP-001`
- `TASK-CULT2-CANOPY-001`
- propagation, advanced canopy/flowering, IPM, and QA competencies

## Propagation form
Candidate receives donor/batch identities, rooting-rate history, losses by batch/cultivar, sanitation records, environmental trends, and acclimatization notes. Candidate calculates performance, detects meaningful patterns, ranks process contributors, selects a next verification step, and distinguishes genotype timing from process failure when evidence allows.

## Canopy/flowering form
Candidate receives work order, stage/cultivar context, before/after images, canopy measurements, environment/light context, recovery observations, and prior intervention history. Candidate evaluates whether execution matched the production objective, identifies abnormal response/nonuniformity, and documents what should be repeated, modified within SOP, or escalated.

## Critical errors
Unsupported contamination/genotype diagnosis; unauthorized high-risk intervention; ignoring sanitation/traceability controls; altering original records.

## Scoring
Performance calculation/data use 20; pattern/evidence interpretation 25; troubleshooting 25; role-boundary judgment 15; documentation 15.

**Pass:** 80/100 and no critical error.

## Evidence produced
Propagation Performance Review or Canopy/Flowering Performance Review plus supervisor handoff.
