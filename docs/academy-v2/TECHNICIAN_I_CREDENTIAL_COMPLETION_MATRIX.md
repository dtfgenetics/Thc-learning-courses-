# THC Cultivation Technician I — Credential Completion Matrix

## Credential purpose

THC Cultivation Technician I is the first employment-facing credential in the THC Academy ladder. It is designed to show that a learner can safely execute routine cultivation work, interpret basic crop and environmental information, preserve traceability, document work, and escalate abnormal conditions appropriately.

It is not intended to certify independent facility design, advanced diagnosis, pesticide-applicator authority, breeding-program leadership, or supervisory competence.

## Required learning foundation

The Technician I pathway reuses existing THC Academy science and professional-practice content rather than duplicating it.

Required knowledge domains:
1. Safety and responsible workplace practice
2. Sanitation and biosecurity
3. Plant biology and crop observation
4. Environmental management
5. Water, pH and EC
6. Root-zone and irrigation fundamentals
7. IPM and scouting
8. Propagation
9. Canopy management
10. Flowering and harvest context
11. Postharvest handling
12. SOPs, records, traceability and handoff

## Employment job-practice units

The credential includes twelve job-practice units:
- Workplace Safety
- Sanitation & Biosecurity
- Crop Observation & Scouting
- Environmental Monitoring
- Water, pH & EC Measurement
- Irrigation Execution
- IPM Scouting
- Propagation
- Canopy Work
- Harvest Workflow
- Postharvest Handling
- SOP Execution, Records & Shift Handoff

Each unit connects science to actual technician behavior, documentation and decision-making.

## Knowledge/scenario assessment

### Initial bank

The first-pass Technician I bank contains **120 credential-purpose items**, with ten initial role-focused items in each task area.

The production target remains **300+ items** so future exam forms can rotate broadly without teaching to one fixed test.

### Credential exam form

Recommended first implementation:
- 72 items
- randomized equivalent forms
- mostly application/scenario items
- selected calculations/interpretation items
- 80% passing target
- competency-level score reporting

The 72-item credential form should sample all major job-task domains rather than simply mirror the order of course lessons.

## Required integrated practicals

The candidate completes six integrated practicals:

### Practical A — Safe Room Entry & Crop Inspection
Measures safety, sanitation, crop observation, environmental interpretation and records.

### Practical B — Water & Irrigation Shift
Measures pH/EC workflow, irrigation execution, delivery/root-zone interpretation and records.

### Practical C — IPM Scouting Route
Measures biosecurity, signs/symptoms, incidence/severity, spatial trend and escalation.

### Practical D — Propagation Workstation
Measures donor/work-order verification, sanitation, propagation sequence, labeling, counts and batch records.

### Practical E — Canopy Maintenance Work Order
Measures work-order interpretation, crop inspection, sanitation, routine canopy execution and exception handling.

### Practical F — Harvest-to-Dry-Room Handoff
Measures authorized harvest execution, lot identity, contamination handling, quantity reconciliation and postharvest receiving.

Recommended first implementation: **80% target on each practical**, with remediation/retry available.

## Capstone

### One Cultivation Shift

The capstone integrates all Technician I domains in a 120–180 minute simulated or supervised shift.

The candidate must:
- prioritize work
- enter safely
- inspect crop
- interpret environment
- perform water/irrigation decisions
- scout IPM
- execute propagation or canopy work
- handle a harvest/postharvest transition
- respond to an unexpected event
- reconcile records
- complete a professional handoff

Recommended first implementation: **160/200 (80%)**.

## Portfolio requirement

A learner should leave Technician I with an employment portfolio, not only a certificate.

Recommended portfolio artifacts:
1. crop inspection and room map
2. water/irrigation record
3. IPM scouting trend report
4. propagation batch record
5. canopy work-order execution report
6. harvest traceability packet
7. dry-room monitoring/lot-movement report
8. Cultivation Shift Performance Summary

The learner can choose which sanitized artifacts are public or shared with an employer.

## Credential issuance recommendation

Issue the Technician I credential when the learner has:
- completed the required learning pathway
- passed the Technician I written/scenario assessment
- passed all six integrated practicals
- passed the One Cultivation Shift capstone
- generated the required competency transcript/performance record

Keep retries straightforward. Learners who miss a practical should remediate that skill and retry the relevant practical rather than restarting the entire program.

## Employment-facing language

The certificate should communicate that the holder demonstrated role-specific competencies through:
- structured instruction
- knowledge/scenario assessment
- practical performance assessment
- integrated cultivation-shift simulation
- traceable work products

Avoid overstating the credential as a government license, pesticide license, state compliance credential or ISO/ANAB-accredited certification unless those recognitions are separately obtained.
