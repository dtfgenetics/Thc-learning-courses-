# Technician I Equivalent Secure Form Rules

**Credential:** `CREDPROG-CULT-TECH-I-001`  
**Status:** development rules — final equivalence criteria require pilot and standard-setting evidence

## Purpose

Credential forms must measure the same occupational blueprint without exposing identical surface content or allowing an easier retest path. Equivalent does not mean identical, and random choice order alone does not create a new secure form.

## Fixed blueprint requirements

Every operational written/scenario form must preserve the approved:

- JTA-domain coverage;
- competency coverage;
- cognitive/decision demand;
- safety/traceability/authority-boundary representation;
- total scored opportunity;
- accommodation-compatible presentation requirements;
- final decision rule once formally approved.

Every practical/capstone form must preserve its controlled task and scoring blueprint even when room names, cultivar names, readings, images, fault locations, dynamic events or work-order details vary.

## Form construction rules

1. Use only approved secure operational items/materials from the private assessment store.
2. Do not use public repository development items as secure credential items.
3. Track every operational form with a unique form ID and immutable revision.
4. Record the exact secure item/task revisions assigned to the form.
5. Keep answer keys, scoring rationales and unreleased forms outside the public repository.
6. Review content overlap and exposure before assigning a form for retest.
7. Retest forms must be equivalent but not simply the same form with shuffled options.
8. Retire or quarantine exposed/compromised items and forms through the security process.
9. Pilot/equating evidence must inform any final quantitative equivalence tolerances; none are invented in this development document.

## Practical and capstone variation

Allowed surface variation includes identifiers, maps, numerical readings, observation images, event timing, problem location, primary workstation and exception type. Variation must not remove a required competency, reduce a critical decision, change scoring-domain weights without approval, or bypass a controlled critical-failure opportunity.

## Retest assignment

A candidate must not knowingly receive an operational form that materially duplicates the failed form when an approved equivalent alternative exists. Form assignment and exposure history are private operational records.

## Release gate

`equivalentSecureForms` remains unresolved until multiple secure forms are built, reviewed, pilot-tested as needed, and approved. This document defines the construction contract only.
