# Technician II Candidate Evidence Retention & Privacy Draft

**Applies to:** Technician II Courses 1–8, Practicals A–G, Senior Technician Diagnostic Shift capstone and professional credential evidence  
**Status:** governance draft — legal/privacy/security approval required before live credential use

## Purpose and minimization

Collect only evidence required to administer, evaluate, audit, remediate, appeal and verify the credential. Do not collect unrelated sensitive information merely because the platform can store it.

## Controlled evidence categories

Private records may include candidate identifier, eligibility/enrollment state, secure form/version identifiers, timestamps and decision events, submitted work artifacts, evaluator rubric evidence, minimum accommodation-administration notes, critical-failure/adjudication records, remediation/retest history, appeal/security holds, issuance/revocation status and verification identifier.

## Public/private separation

Private operational evidence includes secure items/keys, form configuration, raw candidate events, detailed evaluator notes, adjudication discussion, medical/disability documentation, fraud/security indicators and non-public account/contact data.

Public verification exposes only approved minimum fields such as credential title/level, issuer, credential identifier, issue date, approved status/expiration if applicable, program version and verification result. Raw scores, test content, private evidence, assessor notes and accommodation information are excluded by default.

## Role-based access

At minimum distinguish candidate, instructor/remediation role, evaluator/assessor, assessment administrator, credential approver, security/audit administrator and public verifier. Each role receives only the minimum information required.

## Retention schedule — approval dependent

Final retention periods require legal/privacy, audit, appeals and business/accreditation review. Until approved, the system must not silently delete records or apply undocumented indefinite retention.

The approved schedule must separately address failed/practice attempts, readiness attempts, credential attempts, evaluator/calibration evidence, appeal/adjudication records, issued/revoked credential records and security/audit logs. Legal/security holds override routine disposal until formally released.

## Integrity and auditability

Preserve form/rubric version, authorship/modification audit events, original scored evidence, controlled corrections, status transitions, approval/revocation actions and links between practical/capstone evidence and credential decisions. Administrators may not overwrite original scored evidence without an audit trail.

## Candidate access/correction

The approved policy must define candidate access to records, factual identity/contact correction, scoring-dispute routing to appeal, withholding of secure content needed to protect assessment integrity and legally applicable privacy/data-rights request handling.

## Accommodation confidentiality

Store only administration information needed to implement an approved accommodation. Public verification and ordinary assessor views must not expose underlying medical/disability documentation when the assessor only needs the approved administration rule.

## Incident response and disposal

Unauthorized access, assessment-content exposure, candidate-data exposure, credential fraud or evidence-integrity compromise requires controlled incident handling and evidence preservation. When approved retention periods expire, deletion/disposal must be auditable and consistent with backup/log behavior.

## Required approval before live credential use

Privacy/legal review, security review, retention schedule approval, candidate notice/lawful-basis review where applicable, access-control review, incident-response approval and public-verification field approval are all required.

## Current state

The control structure is defined; final retention periods and legal/privacy approvals remain open. This draft does not close `candidateEvidenceRetentionPrivacyPolicy`.
