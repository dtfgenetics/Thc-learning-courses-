# Technician II Equivalent Secure Form Rules

**Credential:** `CREDPROG-CULT-TECH-II-001`  
**Status:** development rules — final equivalence criteria require pilot, calibration and standard-setting evidence

## Purpose

Equivalent Technician II forms must measure the same occupational blueprint without exposing identical surface content or creating an easier retest path. Shuffled answer choices do not create a new secure form.

## Fixed requirements

Every operational written/scenario form must preserve approved competency/domain coverage, cognitive/diagnostic demand, safety and authority-boundary representation, total scored opportunity, accommodation-compatible presentation and the final decision rule once approved.

Practicals A–G and the Senior Technician Diagnostic Shift capstone must preserve their controlled task/scoring blueprint even when identifiers, cultivar names, readings, images, fault locations, work-order details or event timing vary.

## Construction rules

1. Use only approved secure operational items/materials from the private assessment store.
2. Never use public repository development items as operational credential items.
3. Give each form a unique form ID and immutable revision.
4. Record exact item/task revisions assigned to each form.
5. Keep answer keys, scoring rationales and unreleased forms private.
6. Review overlap and exposure before retest assignment.
7. Retest forms must be substantively equivalent, not merely reordered.
8. Quarantine compromised items/forms through the security process.
9. Quantitative equivalence tolerances must come from pilot/equating evidence; none are invented here.

## Practical/capstone variation

Allowed surface variation may include maps, numerical readings, observation images, event timing, equipment/interface details, problem location and exception type. Variation cannot remove required competencies, reduce a critical decision, change scoring-domain weights without approval, remove a critical-failure opportunity or weaken the no-compensatory-averaging rule.

## Retest assignment

A failed required practical must be repassed individually under the approved policy. A candidate should not knowingly receive a materially duplicative operational form when an approved equivalent exists. Exposure and assignment history are private operational records.

## Release gate

`equivalentSecureForms` remains unresolved until multiple secure forms are built, reviewed, pilot/equivalence-tested as required and approved.
