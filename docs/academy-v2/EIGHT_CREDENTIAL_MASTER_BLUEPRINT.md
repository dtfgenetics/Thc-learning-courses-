# THC Academy Eight-Credential Master Blueprint

**Status:** Design baseline for credential-specific curriculum and assessment  
**Depends on:** `docs/THC_ACADEMY_SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md`

## Purpose

This document defines the initial occupational scope, certification-course planning range, performance emphasis, and assessment pattern for the eight THC Academy professional credentials. Exact course counts and exam weights remain provisional until job-task analysis and SME/employer validation are complete.

The 420 Comprehensive Educational Resources are supporting knowledge assets. They are not the certification curriculum.

## Common design rules

Every credential must:

- begin with a defined occupational use case;
- map job tasks to measurable competencies;
- define expected proficiency for every competency;
- use purpose-built Learning Hub certification courses;
- use Comprehensive Educational Resources only as mapped references/support;
- separate formative practice, course tests, and secure credential assessment;
- include practical/performance evidence whenever the credential claims workplace performance;
- use evidence-backed content and reviewed assessment items;
- include accessibility, retake/remediation, appeals, integrity, and version controls;
- generate an employer-readable competency record after award.

## 1. THC Cultivation Technician I

### Occupational claim

The credential holder can safely execute routine cultivation work under established SOPs, make required observations and measurements, preserve identity/traceability, document work accurately, and escalate abnormal conditions appropriately.

### Proficiency emphasis

Applied / operational routine execution.

### Planned dedicated courses

**6–8 courses**

Recommended structure:

1. Safety, Responsible Practice & Cultivation Workflows
2. Plant Observation, Growth Stages & Crop Records
3. Environmental, Light & Sensor Fundamentals
4. Water, Root Zone, Nutrition & Irrigation Fundamentals
5. Propagation, Canopy, IPM Scouting & Crop Care
6. Harvest, Postharvest, Traceability & Shift Handoff
7. Integrated Technician Practice (if separated)
8. Credential Preparation / Simulation Lab (if separated)

### Required performance domains

- safety/PPE/stop-work
- sanitation and biosecurity
- plant identification and routine observation
- environmental measurement
- pH/EC and basic meter handling
- routine irrigation execution
- propagation/transplanting
- canopy work
- IPM scouting and escalation
- harvest/postharvest workflow
- traceability and records
- shift communication

### Credential assessment pattern

- secure knowledge/scenario exam
- image/data interpretation
- routine measurement/calculation tasks
- practical work-order simulations
- integrated cultivation-shift capstone

## Cross-credential prerequisite model

Recommended default sequence:

`Safety & Responsible Practice Certificate -> Cultivation Foundations Certificate -> Technician I -> Technician II / Specialist Credentials -> Lead & Operations Professional`

Specialists may have alternate eligibility pathways after validation, including demonstrated equivalent experience or prerequisite competencies. The initial release should prioritize controlled instructional pathways until challenge/exam-only routes are independently validated.

## Endorsement layer

Do not create additional professional certifications merely because a specialized subject exists. Preferred endorsements include:

- Tissue Culture
- Outdoor Production
- Greenhouse Production
- Advanced Crop Steering
- Advanced Lighting
- Automation & Controls
- Living Soil / Biological Systems
- Commercial Hydroponics
- Advanced Diagnostics
- Cannabis Traceability Systems
- Research & Cultivar Trials
- Quality Systems / SOP Administration

## Validation work still required

Before final release of any credential:

- perform formal job-task analysis;
- validate tasks with SMEs/employers;
- set competency proficiency levels;
- finalize exact certification-course count;
- finalize assessment blueprint weights;
- build and review secure item banks;
- conduct practical-rubric calibration;
- establish provisional and then defensible passing standards;
- define retake/remediation/maintenance rules;
- complete accessibility and governance review;
- document release approval.
