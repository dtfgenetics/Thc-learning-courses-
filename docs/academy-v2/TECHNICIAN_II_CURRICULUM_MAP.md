# THC Cultivation Technician II — Curriculum Map

## Role purpose

Cultivation Technician II is the advanced-operator/senior-technician credential that sits between Technician I and Cultivation Lead.

The role is designed for a worker who can reliably perform Technician I tasks and now needs to:
- troubleshoot routine crop and system deviations
- verify sensors, equipment and measurement quality
- execute and verify approved fertigation work
- interpret root-zone and irrigation trends
- perform higher-level IPM follow-up
- investigate propagation underperformance
- execute advanced canopy/flowering work
- interpret postharvest deviations
- reconcile traceability records
- calculate production KPIs
- support peer execution and handoff

Technician II is **not** a formal supervisor. Staff scheduling, performance management, cultivation-program design, pesticide recommendations outside authorization, environmental-strategy ownership and department management belong in Lead/Supervisor credentials.

## Prerequisite

Recommended prerequisite:
- THC Cultivation Technician I credential, or
- equivalent demonstrated competency through placement assessment/workplace evidence

## Job-practice unit sequence

### Unit 01 — Multi-Factor Crop Diagnosis
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-DIAG-001`

Learner integrates:
- morphology
- plant stage
- environment
- irrigation
- recent work
- spatial pattern
- crop history

New job layer:
- differential diagnosis workflow
- evidence ranking
- identifying missing measurements
- distinguishing likely cause from confirmed cause
- deciding verify/correct/escalate

### Unit 02 — Sensors, Controls & Data Quality
**Tasks:** `TASK-CULT2-SENSOR-001`, `TASK-CULT2-EQUIPMENT-001`

New job layer:
- cross-sensor comparison
- calibration/verification records
- controller response checks
- local vs room-wide condition
- equipment alarm triage
- safe maintenance boundary
- data-quality flags

### Unit 03 — Fertigation Batch Execution & Verification
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-FERTIGATION-001`

New job layer:
- approved recipe interpretation
- material identity
- mixing sequence
- volume/concentration checks
- pH/EC verification
- actual vs expected results
- batch documentation
- safe chemical handling boundary

### Unit 04 — Root-Zone & Irrigation Troubleshooting
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-ROOTZONE-001`

New job layer:
- dryback curves
- root-zone EC trend
- irrigation volume/frequency history
- emitter/output checks
- plant demand
- accumulation vs delivery vs timing problems
- localized vs systemic faults

### Unit 05 — Advanced IPM Trend Interpretation
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-IPM-001`

New job layer:
- multiple-round trend analysis
- treatment follow-up
- trap counts
- spatial spread
- incidence/severity change
- biological-control follow-up
- treatment effectiveness evidence
- authorization boundary for pesticide decisions

### Unit 06 — Propagation Performance Troubleshooting
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-PROP-001`

New job layer:
- donor/batch comparison
- rooting-rate analysis
- loss distribution
- sanitation history
- environmental trend
- genotype timing
- process-deviation review
- next verification step

### Unit 07 — Advanced Canopy & Flowering Execution
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-CANOPY-001`

New job layer:
- advanced work-order interpretation
- crop response evaluation
- cultivar/stage differences
- recovery assessment
- canopy uniformity evidence
- flowering-stage response
- documenting outcomes rather than relying on technique names

### Unit 08 — Postharvest Process Investigation
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-POSTHARVEST-001`

New job layer:
- dry-room trend analysis
- rack/lot comparison
- moisture/process uniformity
- genealogy/affected scope
- hold and follow-up support
- storage/environment history

### Unit 09 — Cultivation Deviation Investigation
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-DEVIATION-001`

New job layer:
- immediate containment
- facts vs hypotheses
- affected scope
- governing SOP/version
- evidence package
- cause hypotheses
- disposition handoff

### Unit 10 — Traceability & Inventory Reconciliation
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-TRACE-001`

New job layer:
- physical vs electronic record reconciliation
- plant/batch/lot genealogy
- movement history
- missing/duplicate/mismatched record investigation
- seed-to-sale style workflow literacy
- correction/escalation without unsupported edits

### Unit 11 — Production Metrics & Trend Review
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-METRICS-001`

Metrics should include examples such as:
- propagation success rate
- plant-loss rate
- task completion rate
- irrigation usage
- IPM incidence trend
- harvest yield
- dry-room exception rate
- labor/task timing where appropriate

The learner should interpret operational meaning, not merely calculate percentages.

### Unit 12 — Peer Task Support & Handoff
**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-PEER-001`

New job layer:
- demonstrate routine work
- identify execution inconsistency
- explain SOP expectations
- communicate priorities
- provide corrective coaching within role boundaries
- give supervisor-ready handoffs

Each Technician II job-practice unit should contain:

2. prerequisite retrieval
3. advanced concept bridge

6. troubleshooting workflow
7. guided analysis
8. decision scenarios
9. calculations where relevant
10. documentation exercise
11. practical mapping
12. portfolio artifact

## Assessment model

### Knowledge/scenario bank

Initial production target:
- 20 items per major diagnostic/system domain
- 10–15 items per operations/records domain
- target initial bank: **220+ Technician II items**
- mature bank target: **400+ items**

Credential exam target:
- 90 generated items
- strongly weighted to apply/analyze/evaluate
- approximately 15–20% calculation/data interpretation
- 80% initial passing target

### Integrated practicals

Recommended Technician II practicals:

**A — Crop Diagnostic Workup**  
Multi-source plant/environment/root-zone case.

**B — Sensor & Equipment Verification**  
Sensor drift, alarm, controller and output checks.

**C — Fertigation & Root-Zone Troubleshooting**  
Recipe execution plus dryback/EC/delivery investigation.

**D — IPM Trend & Treatment Follow-Up**  
Multi-round scouting, trap and intervention evidence.

**E — Propagation/Canopy Performance Review**  
One primary workstation per generated form; other domain appears as scenario.

**F — Postharvest Deviation & Lot Scope**  
Dry-room excursion, product condition, genealogy and affected scope.

**G — Traceability, Metrics & Shift Coordination**  
Record reconciliation, KPI interpretation and peer handoff.

## Capstone — Senior Technician Diagnostic Shift

The Technician II capstone should simulate responsibility across two or more rooms/process areas.

Candidate must:
- prioritize multiple competing deviations
- verify data quality before acting
- troubleshoot one crop/environment problem
- troubleshoot one irrigation/root-zone problem
- interpret an IPM trend
- investigate a process/traceability deviation
- review production metrics
- support a peer task correction
- create a supervisor-ready end-of-shift report

- Senior Technician Shift Summary

## Separation from Cultivation Lead

Technician II may:
- troubleshoot
- verify
- interpret
- support peers
- recommend verification/correction within SOP
- prepare evidence for supervisor decisions

Cultivation Lead will later own additional capabilities such as:
- daily team/task coordination
- labor allocation
- training sign-off
- schedule execution
- environmental program adjustments within assigned authority
- cultivation supply/inventory planning
- production target management
- cross-department coordination
- formal escalation and decision ownership

This boundary preserves a meaningful credential ladder instead of turning every higher credential into a renamed copy of the previous one.
