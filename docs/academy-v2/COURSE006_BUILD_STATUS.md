## Instructional emphasis

The course trains Technician I learners to verify harvest authorization and safe/clean work readiness, execute authorized cut and transfer steps, keep suspect material under controlled hold, preserve source-to-output genealogy, receive identified lots into controlled postharvest locations, reconcile outgoing and receiving counts/weights without forcing a false match, and produce a receiving-shift handoff that makes partial completion, holds, discrepancies and unresolved conditions explicit.

## Authority and evidence boundaries

Course 006 does not confer independent product-release, disposition, destination-redesign or technical-repair authority. Drying is treated as a controlled moisture-removal process rather than a universal fixed-day rule. New harvest identifiers extend source genealogy rather than replacing it. Quantity discrepancies preserve the original attributable measurements until resolved through the controlled process.

## Assessment design

The formative and summative banks are non-overlapping. The 24-item summative-development form allocates four items each to harvest safety, postharvest handling, traceability, biosecurity/hold control, SOP/authority decisions and QA/reconciliation/handoff records. Authored answer-key positions are balanced by construction and runtime choice randomization remains enabled.
