## Authority and evidence boundaries

Course 006 does not confer independent product-release, disposition, destination-redesign or technical-repair authority. Drying is treated as a controlled moisture-removal process rather than a universal fixed-day rule. New harvest or receiving identifiers extend source genealogy rather than replacing it. Quantity discrepancies preserve the original attributable measurements until resolved through the controlled process.
