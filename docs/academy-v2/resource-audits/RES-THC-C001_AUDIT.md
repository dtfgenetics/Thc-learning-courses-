## Executive decision

The legacy package is scientifically strong enough to preserve as source material, but it should **not remain structured or labeled as a certification course**.

Under the current architecture it becomes a **Comprehensive Educational Resource** in the THC Learning Academy. Its core explanatory content, claim-audit framework, provenance exercise, glossary, source register, and visual concepts remain valuable. Course-only elements should be separated or reframed rather than discarded.

## Science-content audit

### Overall assessment

The package demonstrates good scientific restraint. Strong features include:

- separating species identity from accession, line, clone, plant, organ, sample, and result;
- treating origin/domestication conclusions as model- and sample-dependent rather than settled historical fact;
- separating legal/commercial labels from taxonomy and genetic identity;
- distinguishing direct cannabis evidence from transferred general plant science;
- warning against pseudoreplication and overgeneralization;
- treating provenance as record continuity rather than proof that an upstream label is correct;
- keeping medical/legal conclusions outside the scope of plant-science evidence.

No major conceptual error was identified that requires discarding the resource.

### Claims that should retain explicit limits

1. **Domestication geography** — retain conditional language. Ren et al. 2021 supports an East Asian domestication model from the sampled genomic dataset, but the conclusion remains dependent on sampling, provenance, demographic assumptions, and integration with other historical evidence.
2. **Commercial strain-name reliability** — retain sample-bounded language. The cited studies support caution, not a universal statement that all named cultivars are unreliable.
3. **Landrace** — retain the operational/cautious treatment. Do not imply a universally accepted purity threshold or verification rule.
4. **Chemotype** — continue defining it as measured chemical phenotype under stated material, sampling, handling, and analytical conditions rather than a complete ancestry or human-effect category.
5. **Provenance** — continue distinguishing traceability from genetic authentication.

## Assessment audit

The existing 20-item final is serviceable as an educational self-check, but under the new architecture:

- it is **not a certification examination**;
- the 80% passing rule should not live in the resource;
- answer keys may remain with an educator/self-study companion but should not be confused with secure credential item pools;
- strong items may later seed a certification-course question bank only after objective mapping, item review, versioning, and security classification;
- the identity/provenance practical is educationally valuable and may be mapped into Technician I/II, Genetics/Breeding, or Lead/Operations certification courses after competency review.

## Visual-content audit

The existing ten visual briefs and two infographic briefs are useful, but the old fixed-count rule is retired.

For RES-THC-C001, prioritize the assets with the strongest learning value:

1. identity hierarchy: species -> accession/line -> clone/plant -> organ -> sample -> result;
2. evidence-aware origin/domestication map with uncertainty visible;
3. use-type versus taxonomy comparison;
4. name-versus-evidence matrix;
5. study-design / experimental-unit diagram;
6. provenance chain;
7. claim-audit decision tree.

Additional visuals should be produced only if they improve a defined learning purpose.

## New-value opportunities

The resource would become significantly stronger with lightweight interactive elements:

- **Identity classifier:** learner classifies species, cultivar, clone, trade name, chemotype, legal category, organ, sample, and result.
- **Claim-audit builder:** guided form that produces a bounded claim and lists missing evidence.
- **Provenance-chain builder:** drag/link source -> accession/lot -> propagation -> plant -> organ/sample -> test/result.
- **Replication check:** short scenarios distinguishing biological replicates from subsamples/pseudoreplication.
- **Evidence-type sorter:** direct cannabis evidence, authoritative identity/method, mechanistic transfer, operational definition, hypothesis/observation.

These are resource-library interactions, not credential examinations.
