# Technician II Job-Practice Unit — Traceability & Inventory Reconciliation

**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-TRACE-001`  
**Target proficiency:** Operational  
**Primary competencies:** `COMP-PRO-SOP-001`, `COMP-PRO-QA-001`

## Purpose
Professional cultivation requires the physical crop and electronic/paper records to describe the same reality. Technician II should detect and investigate mismatches while preserving plant/batch/lot genealogy and avoiding unsupported record edits.

## Reconciliation model
Compare:
- physical identity/tag/label
- expected location
- recorded location
- quantity/count/weight as applicable
- parent/source genealogy
- movement history
- status/stage
- timestamps
- user/work-order history

## Workflow
1. Freeze ambiguous movement when identity is uncertain.
2. Confirm physical evidence.
3. Review the last known-good record.
4. Reconstruct movements chronologically.
5. Identify whether mismatch is missing event, duplicate event, wrong identity, wrong quantity, wrong location or timing problem.
6. Document the evidence-supported correction path or escalate.
7. Never invent a transaction simply to make totals match.

## Worked cases

### Physical plant present, electronic move missing
Reconstruct source/destination and work history; do not create an unsupported timestamp or operator.

### Duplicate transfer entry
Confirm physical state and transaction genealogy before any correction.

### Unreadable lot label
Stop downstream mixing and use controlled genealogy records to resolve identity.

### Quantity mismatch after harvest
Compare source plant count, harvest record, waste/destruction and movement records before assigning loss.

## Common mistakes
- changing the electronic record before understanding the physical state
- forcing numbers to balance with unsupported entries
- ignoring timestamps and genealogy
- combining uncertain material
- treating traceability as clerical work rather than product/crop identity control

## Guided practice
1. Reconcile a plant-room map against electronic records.
2. Find duplicate and missing events in a movement log.
3. Reconstruct genealogy from source plant through harvest lot.
4. Write an evidence-based correction request.
5. Identify which mismatches require immediate movement hold.

## Practical mapping
**Practical G — Traceability, Metrics & Shift Coordination** includes a physical/electronic mismatch and requires a defensible reconciliation.

## Portfolio artifact
**Traceability Reconciliation Report** with last-known-good state, discrepancy, evidence trail and correction/escalation.

## Completion standard
The learner can reconcile routine cultivation inventory/traceability mismatches while preserving identity and evidence integrity.
