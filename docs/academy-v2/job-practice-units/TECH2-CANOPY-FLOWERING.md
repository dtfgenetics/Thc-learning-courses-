# Technician II Job-Practice Unit — Advanced Canopy & Flowering Execution

**Task:** `TASK-CULT2-CANOPY-001`  
**Target proficiency:** Diagnostic execution  
**Primary competencies:** `COMP-CANOPY-ADV-001`, `COMP-FLOWER-ADV-001`  
**Prerequisite:** Technician I Canopy/Harvest; advanced canopy and flowering course

## What changes from Technician I
Technician I executes routine canopy work orders. Technician II performs higher-skill approved interventions, evaluates crop response against a defined production objective, and reports cultivar/stage exceptions that should influence future plans.

## Evidence-driven canopy workflow
- define the intended outcome: height, access, airflow, light distribution, flower-site distribution, recovery or labor efficiency
- document cultivar, stage and vigor
- confirm the approved intervention
- record area/tissue/manipulation
- evaluate short-term recovery
- compare canopy/light/plant response against baseline or control area when available
- document outcome instead of declaring success because a named technique was used

## Worked cases

### Same defoliation, different cultivar response
Cultivar A recovers rapidly while Cultivar B shows prolonged slowdown.

Interpretation: document cultivar-specific response and avoid universalizing the same intensity.

### Flat canopy, uneven PPFD
Visual height is uniform but measurement shows substantial light differences.

Interpretation: canopy appearance is not proof of light uniformity. Use measured distribution and fixture/spacing context.

### Late aggressive intervention
A major high-stress intervention is requested near a sensitive flowering transition in plants already recovering from stress.

Strong response: verify the work-order intent/authority and flag recovery risk rather than treating the technique as routine.

## Common mistakes
- scoring success from appearance alone
- repeating fixed removal percentages across cultivars
- ignoring recovery time
- conflating canopy work with lighting-system performance
- changing the production strategy without authorization
- failing to document labor and outcome

## Guided practice
1. Compare pre/post canopy maps and identify whether the stated objective was achieved.
2. Evaluate cultivar response data to the same intervention.
3. Identify when a work order requires clarification before execution.
4. Write a response report that separates measured outcome from preference.
5. Compare two intervention intensities with recovery and labor data.

## Practical mapping
**Practical E — Propagation/Canopy Performance Review** may generate a canopy form using pre/post images, PPFD data, vigor notes and work records.

## Portfolio artifact
**Canopy Intervention Response Review** showing objective, intervention, measurements, recovery and recommended follow-up.

## Completion standard
The learner can execute advanced approved canopy/flowering work and evaluate response with measurable evidence while preserving cultivar, stage and recovery context.
