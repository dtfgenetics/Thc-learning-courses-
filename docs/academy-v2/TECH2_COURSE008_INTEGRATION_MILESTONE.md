## Performance completion model

The readiness assessment is formative only and is not credential evidence. Each of the seven practicals must independently meet at least 80% with zero critical errors. The capstone must independently meet at least 80% with zero critical errors. Compensatory averaging is not allowed.

A failed practical must be remediated and repassed on an equivalent form. A critical error requires affected-domain reevaluation and cannot be erased by a stronger score elsewhere.
