## Instructional emphasis

The course trains a Technician I to perform representative crop walks, describe growth and reproductive stage from observable morphology, separate observation from diagnosis, preserve spatial/population patterns, create reconstructable crop/photo records, and escalate abnormal or unresolved conditions without exceeding role authority.

## Assessment design

The dedicated bank contains 32 Course 002 items: 12 formative items and 20 summative-development items across plant observation, developmental morphology, record/photo quality and escalation/handoff decisions. The formative and summative item sets are non-overlapping, and authored answer-key positions are balanced. It remains a development bank, not a production-ready credential bank. The course assessment remains draft and retains a bank-expansion target plus human assessment review and practical-performance evidence before release.

## Item-quality repair

The independent Learning Hub item-quality gate correctly caught reuse of the original first 12 items in both formative and summative assessments. The repair created a separate 12-item formative bank, retained 20 distinct summative-development items, and rebalanced authored correct-answer positions without changing the keyed answer content. The repair workflow then reran the Learning Hub item-quality gate, schema validation, curriculum validation, registry validation, Technician I regressions and the complete deterministic test suite before committing the repaired source.
