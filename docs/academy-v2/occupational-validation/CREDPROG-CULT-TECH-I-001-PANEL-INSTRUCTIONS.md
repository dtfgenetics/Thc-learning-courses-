## Method

Each panelist completes the task-rating sheet independently before group reconciliation. Rate each task for relevance, importance, frequency, consequence, expected-at-entry status, and whether it should be represented in the credential assessment program.

Importance/frequency follow current OPM job-analysis practice. Relevance and separate write-in task capture follow O*NET task-analysis methodology. Consequence is a THC Academy design field added to capture worker, crop, quality, traceability and compliance impact.

## Rating cautions

- Rate the target Technician I role, not an ideal manager.
- Do not inflate importance because a topic is scientifically interesting.
- Frequency may vary by facility assignment.
- A task can be important but inappropriate for credential assessment if it belongs only after employer-specific onboarding.
- Explain facility or jurisdiction variation instead of forcing false agreement.
- Preserve pesticide, engineering, electrical/HVAC, laboratory and final-disposition authority boundaries.

## Emerging tasks

Use the separate emerging-task sheet for genuinely missing or substantially revised task families. Collect write-ins separately. If the panel accepts a new task, circulate it for a later common rating round rather than comparing partial write-in ratings with the original common task list.

## Reconciliation session

1. Review rating distributions, not just averages.
2. Identify major relevance disagreement.
3. Flag high-consequence tasks even when frequency is low.
4. Review expected-at-entry and role-level disagreements.
5. Compare facility-type and jurisdiction patterns.
6. Disposition each task as keep, adapt, split, merge, move role, or remove.
7. Decide target proficiency.
8. Decide whether evidence belongs mainly in written/scenario assessment, direct practical assessment, both, or neither.
9. Preserve unresolved dissent rather than forcing consensus.

## Boundary

No mean score, percentage, or automatic cutoff in this instrument approves a task or determines exam weight. Final decisions require documented panel reconciliation and exact-version approval.
