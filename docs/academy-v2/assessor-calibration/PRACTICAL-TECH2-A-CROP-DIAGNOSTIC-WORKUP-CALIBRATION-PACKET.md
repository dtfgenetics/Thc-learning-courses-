## Critical-failure decisions

- Treats an unverified single reading as proof.
- Changes multiple major systems without a diagnostic reason.
- Presents a hypothesis as a confirmed cause without evidence.
- Ignores an immediate safety or crop-protection escalation trigger.
- Alters records to make the case fit.

## Non-negotiable rule

Any disagreement on whether a critical failure occurred must be reviewed and resolved by the designated human authority before operational use. A high overall score-agreement statistic does not override unresolved critical-failure disagreement.
