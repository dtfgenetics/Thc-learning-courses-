## Calibration method

1. Freeze the exact assessment version.
2. Select common representative samples spanning strong, borderline, weak, and critical-failure performance when feasible.
3. Two or more assessors score independently before discussion.
4. Preserve total score, every domain score, critical-failure decisions and assessor notes.
5. Aggregate with `npm run calibration:aggregate -- --input <private-paired-ratings.json>`.
6. Reconcile only after independent ratings are retained.
7. If scoring anchors, task conditions or critical-failure definitions change, version the assessment and repeat affected calibration.

## Scoring domains

1. **Work-order and identity verification** — 10 points
2. **Measurement technique and calibration awareness** — 20 points
3. **pH EC and nutrition-context interpretation** — 15 points
4. **Irrigation execution** — 20 points
5. **Delivery and root-zone problem recognition** — 15 points
6. **Records and reconciliation** — 15 points
7. **Escalation and handoff** — 5 points

## Critical-failure decisions

- Uses an unverified measurement to make an unauthorized room-wide irrigation or nutrient change.
- Knowingly applies the wrong solution or work order to a room, batch, or zone after an unresolved identity conflict.
- Bypasses a serious electrical, chemical, pressure, or slip hazard instead of stopping and escalating.
- Falsifies pH EC delivery quantity or completion records to conceal a discrepancy.

## Required evidence outputs

- Meter and calibration check record

## Agreement evidence

- sample count;

- mean absolute total-score difference;
- per-domain exact agreement;
- per-domain mean absolute score difference;
- critical-failure agreement;
- unresolved critical-failure disagreement count;
- assessor notes and reconciliation decision.

## Boundary

Any critical-failure disagreement must be resolved by the designated authority before operational use. Aggregate agreement alone does not establish occupational validity, fairness, construct validity or authorization.
