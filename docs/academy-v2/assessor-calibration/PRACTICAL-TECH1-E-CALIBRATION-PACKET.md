## Calibration method

1. Freeze the exact assessment version.
2. Select common representative samples spanning strong, borderline, weak, and critical-failure performance when feasible.
3. Two or more assessors score independently before discussion.
4. Preserve total score, every domain score, critical-failure decisions and assessor notes.
5. Aggregate with `npm run calibration:aggregate -- --input <private-paired-ratings.json>`.
6. Reconcile only after independent ratings are retained.
7. If scoring anchors, task conditions or critical-failure definitions change, version the assessment and repeat affected calibration.

## Scoring domains

1. **Work-order and objective interpretation** — 15 points
2. **Pre-task crop inspection** — 10 points
3. **Sanitation and workspace control** — 15 points
4. **Task execution and tissue preservation** — 25 points
5. **Consistency against criteria** — 15 points
6. **Exception and escalation handling** — 10 points
7. **Records and handoff** — 10 points

## Critical-failure decisions

- Performs irreversible tissue removal after identifying an unresolved identity or work-order conflict.
- Knowingly continues work that causes serious plant damage after a supplied stop condition is met.
- Carries suspect tools waste or material across a supplied biosecurity or quarantine boundary.
- Falsifies completed scope damage exceptions or plant identity records.

## Required evidence outputs

- Annotated canopy work plan

## Boundary

Any critical-failure disagreement must be resolved by the designated authority before operational use. Aggregate agreement alone does not establish occupational validity, fairness, construct validity or authorization.
