## Calibration method

1. Freeze the exact assessment version.
2. Select common representative samples spanning strong, borderline, weak, and critical-failure performance when feasible.
3. Two or more assessors score independently before discussion.
4. Preserve total score, every domain score, critical-failure decisions and assessor notes.
5. Aggregate with `npm run calibration:aggregate -- --input <private-paired-ratings.json>`.
6. Reconcile only after independent ratings are retained.
7. If scoring anchors, task conditions or critical-failure definitions change, version the assessment and repeat affected calibration.

## Critical-failure decisions

- Knowingly continues through an unresolved life-safety or serious uncontrolled hazard when stop or emergency procedure is required.
- Knowingly moves merges relabels or transforms material after an unresolved identity conflict in a way that breaks genealogy.
- Falsifies overwrites or forces a measurement count weight or record to hide a discrepancy.
- Performs an unauthorized pesticide treatment technical repair bypass or product-release decision outside supplied Technician I authority.
- Knowingly moves or releases material from an active contamination or quality hold without required authorization.
