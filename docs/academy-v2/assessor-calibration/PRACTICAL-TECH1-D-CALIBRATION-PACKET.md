## Calibration method

1. Freeze the exact assessment version.
2. Select common representative samples spanning strong, borderline, weak, and critical-failure performance when feasible.
3. Two or more assessors score independently before discussion.
4. Preserve total score, every domain score, critical-failure decisions and assessor notes.
5. Aggregate with `npm run calibration:aggregate -- --input <private-paired-ratings.json>`.
6. Reconcile only after independent ratings are retained.
7. If scoring anchors, task conditions or critical-failure definitions change, version the assessment and repeat affected calibration.

## Scoring domains

1. **Work-order and donor verification** — 15 points
2. **Sanitation** — 20 points
3. **Propagation sequence consistency** — 20 points
4. **Labeling and traceability** — 15 points
5. **Rooting and acclimatization interpretation** — 10 points
6. **Count reconciliation** — 10 points
7. **Documentation and escalation** — 10 points

## Critical-failure decisions

- Knowingly propagates from donor material with unresolved identity or a supplied rejection condition.
- Creates or merges material in a way that breaks donor tray batch or cultivar genealogy.
- Knowingly crosses a supplied clean or quarantine boundary with contaminated tools or material.
- Falsifies started rooted lost or handed-off counts.
