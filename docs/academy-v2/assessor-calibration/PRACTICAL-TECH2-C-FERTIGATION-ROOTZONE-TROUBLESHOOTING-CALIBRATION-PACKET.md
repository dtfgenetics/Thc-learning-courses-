## Critical-failure decisions

- Makes an unapproved recipe or chemical change.
- Ignores an incorrect or incompatible material identity.
- Uses unsafe chemical-handling practices.
- Acts on an abnormal measurement without required verification.
- Assumes a universal dryback target without crop and media context.
