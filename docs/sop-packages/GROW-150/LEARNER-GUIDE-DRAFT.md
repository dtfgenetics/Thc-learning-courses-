# THC-GROW-150 Learner Guide — Testing, QA, CAPA, and crop review
**Draft — not operationally released**

## Goal

Define a cultivation QA workflow for deviations, affected-scope reconstruction, evidence review, laboratory/testing context, CAPA, effectiveness checks and periodic crop/process review.

## What the evidence supports

1. Deviation handling should preserve the original event, immediate containment, actual affected scope, supporting measurements, relevant records and unresolved uncertainty before final disposition or CAPA is assigned.
   - **Boundary:** A corrected process condition does not by itself resolve material impact or close the investigation.
2. Root-cause analysis should distinguish evidence-supported causal factors from contributing conditions and hypotheses instead of treating temporal association or operator blame as proof.
   - **Boundary:** The framework does not require one named root-cause tool; method choice should match consequence and complexity.
3. Corrective and preventive actions are stronger when linked to the investigated cause or system weakness and followed by a defined effectiveness check rather than closed at implementation.
   - **Boundary:** An effectiveness metric must be appropriate to the failure mode and cannot be fabricated merely to close a record.
4. Laboratory or COA results should be interpreted with sample identity, method, chain of custody, specification context and lot/process history so testing remains connected to the material actually represented.
   - **Boundary:** The SOP should not invent jurisdiction-specific release limits or treat one laboratory result as universally representative without a defensible sampling plan.
5. Periodic crop review should compare defined, stable metrics such as deviations, yield/quality outcomes, rework/holds, recurring plant-health signals and process variability using consistent denominators and definitions.
   - **Boundary:** Changing a KPI definition breaks direct comparability unless the change is documented and historical interpretation is reconciled.
6. Quality decisions should remain traceable to the evidence and authorization that supported release, hold, rework, rejection or further investigation.
   - **Boundary:** This educational QA model does not grant authority to release regulated product outside applicable facility and jurisdiction controls.

## What you must verify before acting

Identity • stage/process • current procedure/plan • measurement quality • authority • required record • escalation trigger.

## Do not assume

- automatic root cause from correlation alone
- automatic product disposition without affected-scope evidence
- retroactive deletion or rewriting of deviations
- universal laboratory acceptance criteria detached from method/jurisdiction
- closing CAPA only because an action was assigned

## Strong work product

A strong record allows another qualified person to reconstruct what was planned, what actually happened, what evidence was used, what remained uncertain, what action was authorized, and what needs follow-up.
