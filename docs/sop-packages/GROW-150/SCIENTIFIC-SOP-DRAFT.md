# THC-GROW-150 — Testing, QA, CAPA, and crop review
**Version:** 0.1.0 draft  
**Status:** Controlled draft — review required  
**Release:** Blocked

## Purpose

Define a cultivation QA workflow for deviations, affected-scope reconstruction, evidence review, laboratory/testing context, CAPA, effectiveness checks and periodic crop/process review.

## Scope

**Included:** deviation intake; immediate containment; affected-scope reconstruction; root-cause analysis; CAPA; effectiveness checks; laboratory/COA context; batch/crop review; trend metrics; authorized disposition.

**Not universalized or not authorized by this draft:** automatic root cause from correlation alone; automatic product disposition without affected-scope evidence; retroactive deletion or rewriting of deviations; universal laboratory acceptance criteria detached from method/jurisdiction; closing CAPA only because an action was assigned.

## Scientific and operational control principles

### 1. Deviation handling should preserve the original event, immediate containment, actual affected scope, supporting measurements, relevant records and unresolved uncertainty before final disposition or CAPA is assigned.
**Evidence boundary:** A corrected process condition does not by itself resolve material impact or close the investigation.
**Sources:** `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`, `REF-ASTM-E2659-001`

### 2. Root-cause analysis should distinguish evidence-supported causal factors from contributing conditions and hypotheses instead of treating temporal association or operator blame as proof.
**Evidence boundary:** The framework does not require one named root-cause tool; method choice should match consequence and complexity.
**Sources:** `REF-ASTM-E2659-001`, `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`

### 3. Corrective and preventive actions are stronger when linked to the investigated cause or system weakness and followed by a defined effectiveness check rather than closed at implementation.
**Evidence boundary:** An effectiveness metric must be appropriate to the failure mode and cannot be fabricated merely to close a record.
**Sources:** `REF-ASTM-E2659-001`, `REF-ASTM-D8403-001`

### 4. Laboratory or COA results should be interpreted with sample identity, method, chain of custody, specification context and lot/process history so testing remains connected to the material actually represented.
**Evidence boundary:** The SOP should not invent jurisdiction-specific release limits or treat one laboratory result as universally representative without a defensible sampling plan.
**Sources:** `REF-POSTHARVEST-001`, `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`

### 5. Periodic crop review should compare defined, stable metrics such as deviations, yield/quality outcomes, rework/holds, recurring plant-health signals and process variability using consistent denominators and definitions.
**Evidence boundary:** Changing a KPI definition breaks direct comparability unless the change is documented and historical interpretation is reconciled.
**Sources:** `REF-ASTM-D8403-001`, `REF-ASTM-E2659-001`

### 6. Quality decisions should remain traceable to the evidence and authorization that supported release, hold, rework, rejection or further investigation.
**Evidence boundary:** This educational QA model does not grant authority to release regulated product outside applicable facility and jurisdiction controls.
**Sources:** `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`, `REF-POSTHARVEST-STABILITY-2026-001`

## Measurement, observation and decision quality

Distinguish planned/target values from actual observations and measurements. Preserve method/instrument context, stage, cultivar/lot, location, timing and relevant environmental/root-zone/process context. Do not turn one study treatment, visual cue or facility example into a universal cannabis specification.

## Records

The applicable form must preserve identity, date/time, location, stage/process, source procedure/version, required condition, actual condition, method/instrument when relevant, action, affected scope, deviation/escalation and release/approval state.

## Deviations

Contain immediate risk within authority, preserve the original evidence, define affected scope, notify the responsible role, and document follow-up. Never backfill a record to make execution appear compliant.
