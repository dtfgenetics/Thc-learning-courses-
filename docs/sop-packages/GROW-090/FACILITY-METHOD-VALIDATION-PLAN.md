## Evidence boundaries that must remain intact

- universal pest-count action thresholds
- universal pesticide product recommendations
- mixing rates, tank-mix recipes or off-label use
- assumption that research efficacy equals legal registration
- automatic release from quarantine based on one negative observation

## Minimum execution evidence

Record exact version, date/time, location, validator/operator, equipment/instrument status, material/crop identity where relevant, acceptance criterion, actual result, deviations, corrective action/retest and authorized disposition.

## Failure / stop conditions

Validation is incomplete if evidence is missing, pass/fail cannot be distinguished, an acceptance criterion lacks authority, safety/legal/engineering prerequisites are unresolved, or execution requires undocumented workarounds.
