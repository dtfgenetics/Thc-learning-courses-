# THC-GROW-160 — Forms, logs, checklists, and training records
**Version:** 0.1.0 draft  
**Status:** Controlled draft — review required  
**Release:** Blocked

## Purpose

Define the minimum controlled-record architecture needed to make cultivation work reconstructable without creating redundant or meaningless paperwork.

## Scope

**Included:** master forms index; record purpose; required fields; identity/time/context; corrections; training records; authorization status; retention/versioning; electronic/paper equivalence principles; handoff records.

**Not universalized or not authorized by this draft:** silent overwriting of original data; blanket requirement to record data with no defined purpose; private uncontrolled shadow logs replacing official records; training completion without competency/authorization context where required; one retention period applied universally regardless of law or record type.

## Scientific and operational control principles

### 1. A useful controlled record identifies what occurred, who or what system recorded it, when and where it occurred, the affected material or process, measured versus target information, actions taken and unresolved conditions when relevant.
**Evidence boundary:** Not every form needs every possible field; fields should be selected from the decision, traceability and evidence needs of the specific process.
**Sources:** `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`, `REF-ASTM-D8403-001`

### 2. Corrections should preserve the original entry or audit history so later reviewers can reconstruct the change rather than seeing a silently rewritten record.
**Evidence boundary:** Exact correction mechanics depend on the approved paper or electronic record system.
**Sources:** `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`

### 3. Forms should be linked to the procedure that requires them and should contain fields for the critical observations, verifications, decisions and approvals that the procedure expects users to perform.
**Evidence boundary:** A form cannot repair an ambiguous procedure; missing decision rules or authorization boundaries require SOP correction.
**Sources:** `REF-EPA-SOP-QAG6`, `REF-ASTM-D8403-001`

### 4. Training records should identify the controlled instruction or competency trained, version where relevant, learner, date, trainer/evaluator or controlled system and any required authorization or reassessment state.
**Evidence boundary:** Attendance alone does not prove job competence where supervised performance or authorization is required.
**Sources:** `REF-ASTM-E2659-001`, `REF-ASTM-D8403-001`

### 5. Shift handoff records should preserve completed work, current state, deviations, containment, open risk, required next check and ownership so the receiving person can continue without relying on memory.
**Evidence boundary:** A handoff is not a substitute for emergency notification or formal deviation escalation when those controls apply.
**Sources:** `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`, `REF-ASTM-D8403-001`

### 6. A master records index should identify the current template/version, owner, linked process, storage location, status and applicable retention/disposition rule.
**Evidence boundary:** Retention periods and privacy requirements must be set from applicable law, contract and facility policy rather than a generic course value.
**Sources:** `REF-ASTM-E2659-001`, `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`

## Measurement, observation and decision quality

Distinguish planned/target values from actual observations and measurements. Preserve method/instrument context, stage, cultivar/lot, location, timing and relevant environmental/root-zone/process context. Do not turn one study treatment, visual cue or facility example into a universal cannabis specification.

## Records

The applicable form must preserve identity, date/time, location, stage/process, source procedure/version, required condition, actual condition, method/instrument when relevant, action, affected scope, deviation/escalation and release/approval state.

## Deviations

Contain immediate risk within authority, preserve the original evidence, define affected scope, notify the responsible role, and document follow-up. Never backfill a record to make execution appear compliant.
