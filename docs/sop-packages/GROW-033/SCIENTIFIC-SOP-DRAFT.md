# THC-GROW-033 — Seedling establishment
**Version:** 0.1.0 draft  
**Status:** Controlled draft — review required  
**Release:** Blocked

## Purpose

Define early seedling establishment, root-zone/environment observation, watering and transplant-readiness evidence without universal stage setpoints.

## Scope and boundaries

**Included:** seedling identity; early morphology; root-zone moisture/aeration; watering verification; environment monitoring; damping-off/abnormality escalation; transplant readiness; handoff.

**Not authorized or not universalized in this draft:** universal seedling VPD target; fixed watering volume/frequency; transplanting by age alone; diagnosing every collapse as damping-off; feeding recipes detached from media/water/crop context.

## Scientific and operational control principles

### 1. Seedling establishment should be judged by observed development and root-zone/crop condition rather than age alone because early growth rate varies with genetics and environment.
**Evidence boundary:** Calendar age by itself does not establish transplant readiness.
**Sources:** `REF-PROP-001`, `REF-PLANT-BIO-001`

### 2. Young root systems require both water and oxygen, so seedling watering should be interpreted through media moisture, drainage, container size and root-zone condition instead of a fixed schedule.
**Evidence boundary:** The SOP should not prescribe one irrigation volume/frequency across all media and containers.
**Sources:** `REF-IRRIGATION-001`

### 3. Temperature and humidity/VPD influence plant water relations, but research treatment values should be used as evidence of relationships rather than universal seedling setpoints.
**Evidence boundary:** Facility crop plans and measured crop response govern operational setpoints.
**Sources:** `REF-VPD-002`

### 4. Seedling abnormalities such as collapse, discoloration or poor roots should trigger an evidence-based differential that includes water/oxygen, environment, injury and disease rather than an automatic single diagnosis.
**Evidence boundary:** Visual symptoms alone are insufficient to confirm a specific pathogen or nutrient cause.
**Sources:** `REF-PROP-001`, `REF-IRRIGATION-001`

### 5. Transplant readiness records should preserve seed/plant identity, developmental condition, root-zone status, abnormal findings and destination so weak or suspect plants are not silently mixed into healthy cohorts.
**Evidence boundary:** Exact acceptance criteria require facility validation for the chosen container, medium and crop plan.
**Sources:** `REF-PROP-001`, `REF-POSTHARVEST-001`

## Records and reconstructability

For every controlled event, preserve identity, date/time, location, responsible person or system, planned/required condition, observed/measured condition, action taken, unresolved condition, deviation/escalation reference and approval/release state where applicable.

## Deviations

Do not silently correct history or improvise outside assigned authority. Contain the immediate risk where permitted, preserve evidence, identify affected scope, notify the responsible role, and route the event through the applicable deviation/CAPA or technical-review process.

## Training boundary

Course completion supports education but does not by itself create facility authorization, trade licensure, pesticide authority, engineering authority, laboratory qualification or jurisdiction-specific certification.
