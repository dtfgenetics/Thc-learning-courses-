# THC-GROW-033 Learner Guide — Seedling establishment
**Draft — not operationally released**

## What this topic controls

Define early seedling establishment, root-zone/environment observation, watering and transplant-readiness evidence without universal stage setpoints.

## Core principles

1. Seedling establishment should be judged by observed development and root-zone/crop condition rather than age alone because early growth rate varies with genetics and environment.
   - **Limit:** Calendar age by itself does not establish transplant readiness.
2. Young root systems require both water and oxygen, so seedling watering should be interpreted through media moisture, drainage, container size and root-zone condition instead of a fixed schedule.
   - **Limit:** The SOP should not prescribe one irrigation volume/frequency across all media and containers.
3. Temperature and humidity/VPD influence plant water relations, but research treatment values should be used as evidence of relationships rather than universal seedling setpoints.
   - **Limit:** Facility crop plans and measured crop response govern operational setpoints.
4. Seedling abnormalities such as collapse, discoloration or poor roots should trigger an evidence-based differential that includes water/oxygen, environment, injury and disease rather than an automatic single diagnosis.
   - **Limit:** Visual symptoms alone are insufficient to confirm a specific pathogen or nutrient cause.
5. Transplant readiness records should preserve seed/plant identity, developmental condition, root-zone status, abnormal findings and destination so weak or suspect plants are not silently mixed into healthy cohorts.
   - **Limit:** Exact acceptance criteria require facility validation for the chosen container, medium and crop plan.

## Before acting

Verify: **identity → current status → applicable procedure/plan → measurement or observation quality → your authority → required record → escalation trigger**.

## What not to assume

- universal seedling VPD target
- fixed watering volume/frequency
- transplanting by age alone
- diagnosing every collapse as damping-off
- feeding recipes detached from media/water/crop context

## Evidence habit

Record what you observed or measured before writing the conclusion. Preserve uncertainty and the reason for any decision, especially when the final answer depends on cultivar, stage, facility, method or jurisdiction.
