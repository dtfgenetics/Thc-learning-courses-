# THC-GROW-031 — Seed intake, labeling, and storage
**Version:** 0.1.0 draft  
**Status:** Controlled draft — review required  
**Release:** Blocked

## Purpose

Define seed-lot identity, intake, storage, inventory and viability-record controls without claiming one universal storage condition or shelf life.

## Scope and boundaries

**Included:** seed-lot intake; labeling; source identity; inventory; storage-condition recording; sample/viability testing; chain of custody; disposition.

**Not authorized or not universalized in this draft:** universal cannabis seed shelf life; one storage temperature/RH for every seed lot and container; discarding source identity after repackaging; mixing seed lots because cultivar names match; viability conclusions from undocumented convenience samples.

## Scientific and operational control principles

### 1. Seed intake should preserve the lot's source and genetic identity before repackaging or sampling so viability and later crop performance remain attributable to the correct material.
**Evidence boundary:** Matching cultivar names do not justify combining distinct lots or suppliers.
**Sources:** `REF-GENETICS-001`, `REF-POSTHARVEST-001`

### 2. Cannabis propagation begins from biologically variable seed material, so germination/viability records should be tied to a defined seed lot, sample size, method and date.
**Evidence boundary:** A small undocumented sample cannot establish the viability of every remaining seed in a heterogeneous lot.
**Sources:** `REF-PROP-001`

### 3. Storage records should preserve container, location, date, relevant environmental condition and access/movement history so changes in viability can be investigated.
**Evidence boundary:** The course should not invent a universal cannabis seed shelf-life claim unsupported by lot-specific evidence.
**Sources:** `REF-PROP-001`, `REF-POSTHARVEST-001`

### 4. Seed labels should use stable lot identifiers rather than relying only on informal strain names, allowing splits, samples, germination tests and plant cohorts to be traced back to the source lot.
**Evidence boundary:** Informal naming conventions must not overwrite controlled lot identity.
**Sources:** `REF-GENETICS-001`, `REF-POSTHARVEST-001`

### 5. Inventory adjustments, losses, samples and disposition should be recorded contemporaneously enough that the physical seed count and controlled record can be reconciled.
**Evidence boundary:** Exact inventory/legal reporting requirements remain jurisdiction- and facility-specific.
**Sources:** `REF-POSTHARVEST-001`

## Records and reconstructability

For every controlled event, preserve identity, date/time, location, responsible person or system, planned/required condition, observed/measured condition, action taken, unresolved condition, deviation/escalation reference and approval/release state where applicable.

## Deviations

Do not silently correct history or improvise outside assigned authority. Contain the immediate risk where permitted, preserve evidence, identify affected scope, notify the responsible role, and route the event through the applicable deviation/CAPA or technical-review process.

## Training boundary

Course completion supports education but does not by itself create facility authorization, trade licensure, pesticide authority, engineering authority, laboratory qualification or jurisdiction-specific certification.
