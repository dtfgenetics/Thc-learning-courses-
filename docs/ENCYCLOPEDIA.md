# THC Plant Science Encyclopedia

The encyclopedia is the Academy's reusable plant-science reference layer. Courses teach sequenced competencies; encyclopedia entries explain concepts in depth and can be linked from multiple courses, the glossary, search results, infographics and future printable volumes.

## Editorial model

Every entry has an immutable ID, search-friendly slug, volume, domain, version, lifecycle status, substantial summary, at least three referenced sections and explicit links back to relevant lessons and competencies. Entries remain `draft` until scientific and editorial review is complete.

The encyclopedia must not be padded with empty topic stubs to meet a page count. CI enforces minimum content and evidence requirements. A future volume/page target is a planning goal, not permission to publish thin pages.

## Evidence and scope

Statements should preserve the limits of their sources. Treatment-specific cannabis research is not converted into universal cultivation targets. General horticulture sources may support foundational chemistry or plant physiology when clearly labeled as non-cannabis-specific. Legal and product-authority statements require jurisdiction-specific verification.

## Search and cross-linking

Stable slugs support public routes. `relatedLessons` and `relatedCompetencies` let the Academy deep-link between reference material and instructional sequences. Glossary entries link to encyclopedia IDs so a learner can move from a concise definition to a deeper treatment.

## Volumes

The initial architecture uses numbered volumes so the web knowledge base can later map cleanly into long-form or printable collections. Volume numbers organize subject families; they do not imply that a volume is publication-ready until its entries pass review.
