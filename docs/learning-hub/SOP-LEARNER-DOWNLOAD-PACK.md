# SOP learner download pack

Status: draft, internal preview  
Source boundary: repository-native training material only

This pack turns the repository's SOP-design lesson into four blank learner tools: an authoring template, execution checklist, deviation/change-control record, and training/authorization verification record. The tools support practice in writing and following controlled procedures without claiming to be approved facility SOPs.

## Evidence basis

- `LESSON-PRO-SOP-DESIGN-001` supplies the instructional model: purpose, scope, roles, prerequisites, ordered steps, records, exceptions, escalation, and references.
- `REF-EPA-SOP-QAG6` supplies the government quality-system reference. It is not treated as cannabis-specific law.
- `COURSE-PRO-SOP-001` is the primary course mapping. The execution and training records also support Technician I foundations where learners practice authority and read-and-follow behavior.

## Release boundary

All four downloads remain `draft`, `internal-preview`, and `partial` for accessibility status. They contain no learner or production data. Completion of a course or template does not approve an SOP, authorize controlled work, or replace site document control, safety, quality, training, or legal review.

The controlled Drive packages `GROW-042`, `GROW-050`, `GROW-060`, and `GROW-070` have now been located and reconciled by exact Drive file ID. Their scientific SOPs, learner guides, QA records, and handoff records remain controlled external sources and are not mirrored into the public repository. Each package remains release-blocked for independent technical/accessibility/safety or engineering review, field validation where applicable, and explicit release approval. Source discovery is provenance evidence only; it is not operational approval.

## Ingestion gate for external packages

An external SOP package can move beyond provenance reconciliation only when claims are reviewed against the current controlled source version, course/resource mappings are explicit, accessibility and applicable safety/engineering checks are complete, field/method validation is complete where required, and the appropriate release approval is recorded. The four located packages are currently `provenance-recorded-review-blocked`.
