## Purpose

This is the human review gate for the Technician II primary instructional visuals. Every candidate already has a source SVG, lesson placement, caption, learner text alternative and evidence references. None is learner-visible through the Academy renderer until its individual lesson metadata and registry lifecycle are explicitly approved.

A green automated test proves file/mapping/runtime integrity only. It does **not** prove scientific/technical accuracy, instructional usefulness, rendered accessibility, legal/regulatory suitability, or credential validity.

## Review criteria

For every candidate, reviewers must verify:

1. **Factual/source accuracy** — claims and relationships match the cited lesson references and do not overstate what those sources establish.
2. **Canonical terminology** — labels match the course/lesson language and do not introduce a conflicting concept.
3. **Authority boundary** — the visual does not imply independent pesticide treatment, recipe redesign, hazardous-energy servicing, record-edit approval, product disposition, formal supervision or credential authority where the course denies it.
4. **Accessibility content** — the learner text alternative communicates the instructional meaning, the caption is useful, and critical meaning is not color-only.
5. **Rendered usability** — readable at phone width and 200–400% zoom/reflow; no clipped labels, tiny text, overlapping content or dependency on decorative layout.
6. **Instructional value** — the visual reduces cognitive load or clarifies a decision/evidence relationship; it is not merely decorative.
7. **Decision** — approve, revise, or reject. Rejected/revised assets remain fail-closed.

## Approval recording

Approval must update both controlled locations for the same candidate:

- `visuals/TECH2-VISUAL-PRODUCTION-PLAN.json`: lifecycle state may advance only with `qaApproved: true`;
- mapped lesson `content.extensions.primaryVisuals[*].extensions.releaseApproved`: set to `true` only after the same candidate has passed review.

The visual lifecycle test must remain green after any status change. Do not bulk-approve the registry without reviewing the lesson placement and rendered result.

## Remaining non-visual gates

Visual approval does not close course or credential release. Technical/curriculum review, assessment review, practical/capstone validation, evaluator calibration/inter-rater evidence, controlled pilot evidence, standard setting, privacy/security/retention controls, secure operational assessment approval and final versioned release approval remain separate gates.
