## Controlled learning outcomes

1. Verify whether environmental, lighting and equipment measurements are representative and fit for decisions before changing controls.
2. Interpret commands, alarms, spatial maps and time-series response to classify likely sensor, control, equipment or distribution faults without overstating root cause.
3. Verify irrigation/equipment performance using measured output and process evidence while respecting operator-care, servicing and hazardous-energy boundaries.
4. Produce a reconstructable verification report, safe maintenance handoff and post-action confirmation plan.
