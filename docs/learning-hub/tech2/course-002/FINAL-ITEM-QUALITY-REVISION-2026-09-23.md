# Technician II Course 2 Final — Item Quality Revision

**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH2-002-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 24 summative items

## What changed

All 24 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality without changing the controlled blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- replacing obviously wrong distractors with plausible but premature operational interpretations;
- reducing cueing from words and constructions such as “definitely,” “never,” simplistic “only,” and exaggerated shortcuts;
- improving verification reasoning for sensors, PPFD mapping, irrigation distribution, controller/equipment response, alarms, stored energy and maintenance handoff;
- expanding rationales so they explain the verification logic and why competing choices are weaker;
- preserving the Technician II operator/verification boundary instead of rewarding unauthorized repair behavior.

## What did not change

The revision preserves:

- all 24 item IDs;
- all answer keys;
- objective and competency mappings;
- controlled references;
- Bloom levels and difficulty labels;
- final item membership;
- blueprint weighting;
- test-to-teaching mappings;
- course-derived provenance;
- the prohibition on Encyclopedia substitution;
- the prohibition on untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check:

1. distractor plausibility without introducing more than one defensible key;
2. safety and authority-boundary accuracy;
3. sensor/data-quality reasoning;
4. irrigation and lighting verification logic;
5. rationale accuracy relative to the controlled references;
6. occupational authenticity and accessibility of wording.

## Validation boundary

This revision improves academic assessment quality but does not establish psychometric validity, reliability, fairness, standard setting, secure credential-form suitability, practical validity, or professional credential authorization.
