## Controlled learning outcomes
1. Produce comparable scouting datasets and interpret incidence, severity, spatial scope and multi-round trend evidence.
2. Evaluate authorized IPM intervention follow-up against baseline while identifying confounding, missing evidence and unresolved risk.
3. Maintain quarantine, sanitation and movement controls while escalating high-risk or uncertain plant-health findings.
4. Document scope, data quality, outcome evidence and next-round priorities without altering observations or assuming treatment authority.
