# Technician II Course 4 Final — Item Quality Revision

**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH2-004-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 24 summative items

## What changed

All 24 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality while preserving the controlled blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- replacing obviously wrong or cueing distractors with plausible but premature interpretations/actions;
- improving denominator/sample-effort reasoning, spatial trend interpretation, intervention follow-up, quarantine, sanitation, containment and biosecurity decisions;
- strengthening treatment-authority boundaries so diagnosis confidence never substitutes for legal/facility authorization;
- expanding rationales so they explain why the keyed response is strongest and why the alternatives are weaker;
- preserving uncertainty, contradictory evidence and reconstructable scouting/handoff records.

## What did not change

The revision preserves:

- all 24 item IDs;
- all answer keys;
- objective and competency mappings;
- controlled references;
- Bloom levels and difficulty labels;
- final item membership;
- blueprint weighting;
- test-to-teaching mappings;
- course-derived provenance;
- the prohibition on Encyclopedia substitution;
- the prohibition on untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check:

1. distractor plausibility without multiple defensible keys;
2. IPM sampling/trend interpretation accuracy;
3. containment and biosecurity boundaries;
4. treatment-selection/application authority accuracy;
5. rationale proportionality to the controlled references;
6. occupational authenticity and accessible wording.

## Validation boundary

This revision improves academic assessment quality but does not establish psychometric validity, reliability, fairness, standard setting, secure credential-form suitability, practical validity, or professional credential authorization.
