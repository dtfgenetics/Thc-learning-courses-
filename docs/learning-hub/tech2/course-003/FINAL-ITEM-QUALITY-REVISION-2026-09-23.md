# Technician II Course 3 Final — Item Quality Revision

**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH2-003-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 24 summative items

## What changed

All 24 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality while preserving the controlled assessment blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- replacing obviously wrong or cueing distractors with plausible operational alternatives;
- improving fertigation batch verification, material identity, PPE/hazard controls, irrigation distribution, dryback interpretation, root-zone EC, tissue interpretation, data integrity, and controlled-document reasoning;
- expanding rationales so they explain why the keyed response is strongest and why competing choices are weaker;
- preserving Technician II authority boundaries by distinguishing verification and escalation from unauthorized recipe changes, servicing, or ad hoc chemical response.

## What did not change

The revision preserves:

- all 24 item IDs;
- all answer keys;
- objective and competency mappings;
- controlled references;
- Bloom levels and difficulty labels;
- final item membership;
- blueprint weighting;
- test-to-teaching mappings;
- course-derived provenance;
- the prohibition on Encyclopedia substitution;
- the prohibition on untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check:

1. distractor plausibility without multiple defensible keys;
2. chemical/PPE and safety-boundary accuracy;
3. fertigation and root-zone reasoning;
4. source-water, pH/EC and tissue-interpretation accuracy;

6. occupational authenticity and accessible wording.
