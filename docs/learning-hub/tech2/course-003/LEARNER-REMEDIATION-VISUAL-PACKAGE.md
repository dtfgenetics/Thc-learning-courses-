## Evidence-centered learning loop
Orient → observe/measure → compare → reason → act within authority → document → reassess in an equivalent context.

## Outcome-aligned applied work and remediation

### Outcome 1
**Outcome:** Execute approved fertigation batches with verified material identity, calculations, measurement quality and chemical-safety controls without independently redesigning the recipe.

**Applied artifact:** Create an evidence packet containing problem definition, identifiers/context, observations or measurements, comparison basis, bounded interpretation, uncertainty, authorized action, escalation trigger, and handoff.

**Failure pattern:** Premature causal conclusion, weak/absent baseline, missing context, or an action outside supplied authority.

**Remediation:** Rebuild the reasoning chain as **evidence → comparison → bounded inference → authorized action**. Require one plausible alternative or unresolved uncertainty and a source-backed reason for the next check.

**Equivalent reassessment:** Change identifiers, measurements, time/space pattern, crop/lot context, or operational constraint while preserving the same reasoning demand. Do not treat memorizing the original answer as evidence.

## Integrated practice
Use a multi-stage case requiring at least three outcomes. Introduce a new fact mid-case that changes an earlier assumption. The learner must preserve prior evidence, update the reasoning, and document unresolved work.

Required evidence: priority statement; identifiers/instruction source; observation table; comparison/baseline; ranked reasoning; authority decision; verification result when authorized; handoff.

## Visual production specification
At least **4 primary instructional visuals**, one per current outcome. Additional visuals may be added without limit.
