## Learning loop
Orient → observe/measure → compare → reason → act within authority → document → reassess in an equivalent context.

### Outcome 1
**Outcome:** Contain postharvest deviations and establish a fact/timeline baseline before causal or disposition conclusions.

**Applied artifact:** Evidence packet with decision problem, identifiers/context, observations or measurements, comparison basis, bounded interpretation, uncertainty, authorized response, escalation trigger and handoff.

**Failure pattern:** Conclusion outruns evidence, denominator/baseline/context is missing, history is overwritten, or proposed action exceeds authority.

**Remediation:** Reconstruct **evidence → comparison → bounded inference → authorized action**. State one alternative explanation or unresolved uncertainty and identify the next discriminating check.

**Equivalent reassessment:** Change the batch/crop/lot, measurements, time window, spatial pattern or operational constraint while preserving the same reasoning demand.

**Visual concept 1:** Outcome-specific evidence/decision diagram with comparison, uncertainty, authority boundary and handoff.

## Integrated application
Use a multi-stage case requiring at least three outcomes. Introduce new evidence after the learner's initial plan. The learner must revise the reasoning without deleting prior evidence and clearly transfer unresolved decisions.

Minimum evidence: priority statement; exact identifiers; source instruction; observation/measurement table; baseline/comparison; ranked reasoning; authority decision; verification result where authorized; unresolved-work handoff.

## Visual production
Produce at least **4 primary visuals**, one per current outcome. No maximum asset count. Visuals must use exact terminology, avoid universal SOP/legal/product claims, preserve authority limits, work at mobile size, include external caption/alt text, and not rely on color alone.

## Accessibility/manual review
Keyboard/focus; headings/landmarks; screen-reader order/semantics; 200%/400% zoom/reflow; mobile/tablet/desktop; contrast and non-color cues; form/table labels; assessment instructions/errors; visual text alternatives; print/download readability.
