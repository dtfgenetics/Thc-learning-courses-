## Controlled learning outcomes
1. Contain postharvest deviations and establish a fact/timeline baseline before causal or disposition conclusions.
2. Interpret room/process history together with representative product measurements while distinguishing room RH, moisture content and water activity.
3. Reconstruct lot genealogy and affected scope without unsupported facility-wide expansion or convenient exclusions.
4. Produce a QA-ready handoff while preserving controlled records, historical SOP versions and product-disposition authority boundaries.
