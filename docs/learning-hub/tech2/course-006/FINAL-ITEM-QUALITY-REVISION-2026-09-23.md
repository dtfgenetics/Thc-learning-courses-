# Technician II Course 6 Final — Item Quality Revision

**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH2-006-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 24 summative items

## What changed

All 24 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality while preserving the controlled blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- hold/segregation and disposition boundaries;
- spatial drying variability and representative product sampling;
- room RH versus product water-activity evidence;
- lot genealogy and evidence-supported scope;
- contemporaneous SOP/version reconstruction;
- planned-versus-actual record integrity;
- process excursion versus product-impact reasoning;
- QA-ready handoffs, conflicting evidence, and controlled record correction;
- replacing weak distractors and answer-echo rationales with plausible alternatives and explanatory feedback.

## What did not change

The revision preserves all item IDs, answer keys, objectives, competencies, controlled references, Bloom levels, difficulty labels, final item membership, blueprint weighting, test-to-teaching mappings, course-derived provenance, and the prohibitions on Encyclopedia substitution and untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check distractor plausibility, postharvest/process reasoning accuracy, product-versus-environment measurement distinctions, traceability scope, disposition authority, rationale proportionality to the controlled references, occupational authenticity, and accessible wording.
