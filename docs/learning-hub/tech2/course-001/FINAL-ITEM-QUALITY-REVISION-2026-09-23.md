# Technician II Course 1 Final — Item Quality Revision

**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH2-001-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 24 summative items

## What changed

All 24 conventional-final items were revised to improve review quality without changing the controlled assessment blueprint.

Changes focused on:

- replacing caricatured or obviously wrong distractors with plausible but premature interpretations/actions;
- reducing cueing from absolutes such as “definitely,” “never,” “proof,” and simplistic “only” options;
- making distractor sets require evidence-based discrimination rather than test-taking pattern recognition;
- expanding rationales to explain why the keyed response is strongest and why competing options are weaker;
- strengthening uncertainty, differential-diagnosis, data-quality, cohort-comparison, root-zone, environmental, nutrition, IPM and developmental reasoning.

## What did not change

The revision preserves:

- all 24 item IDs;
- all item keys;
- objective mappings;
- competency mappings;
- controlled references;
- Bloom levels;
- difficulty labels;
- final item membership;
- blueprint weighting;
- test-to-teaching mappings;
- course-derived provenance;
- the prohibition on Encyclopedia substitution;
- the prohibition on untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped assessment review tooling can reopen review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should focus on:

1. distractor plausibility without creating multiple defensible keys;
2. rationale accuracy and proportionality to the source evidence;
3. occupational authenticity of the diagnostic decisions;
4. absence of unintended cueing;
5. preservation of the intended Technician II authority boundary;
6. fairness and accessibility of wording.

## Validation boundary

This revision improves academic assessment quality but does not establish psychometric validity, reliability, fairness, standard setting, secure credential-form suitability, practical validity, or professional credential authorization.
