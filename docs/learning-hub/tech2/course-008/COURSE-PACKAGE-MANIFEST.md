## Controlled learning outcomes
1. Prioritize competing Technician II deviations by immediate safety, containment, data-validity, crop or lot consequence, timing dependency and assigned authority before selecting a next action.
2. Integrate crop pattern, environmental context and measurement quality into a ranked diagnostic workup that separates verified observations, hypotheses and required next checks.
3. Verify sensor and equipment evidence before acting, classify safe operator checks versus authorized maintenance escalation, and reject major corrective action based on data known to be invalid.
4. Integrate approved fertigation execution, water and measurement verification, irrigation delivery, root-zone trend and crop context into a bounded troubleshooting recommendation.
5. Integrate IPM trend evidence with propagation and canopy performance while protecting biosecurity, treatment-authority limits, cultivar or stage context and uncertainty.
6. Investigate postharvest deviations by preserving product measurements, lot genealogy, affected-scope evidence, holds and product-disposition authority boundaries.
7. Reconcile traceability, planned-versus-actual records and KPI definitions while providing SOP-based peer support and a priority-ranked shift handoff without unauthorized record changes.
8. Assemble a reconstructable Senior Technician Diagnostic Shift evidence packet that supports evaluator scoring, remediation decisions and supervisor handoff without unresolved critical errors.
