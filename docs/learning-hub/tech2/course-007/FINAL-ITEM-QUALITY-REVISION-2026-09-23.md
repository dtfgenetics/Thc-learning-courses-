**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH2-007-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 24 summative items

## What changed

All 24 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality while preserving the controlled blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- traceability mismatches, duplicate identities and genealogy continuity;
- KPI numerator/denominator/time-window integrity and comparable trend interpretation;
- planned-versus-actual schedule variance and recurring bottleneck analysis;
- controlled record correction and unreconstructable transaction gaps;
- peer-support versus approval/discipline authority;
- decision-ready shift handoffs and risk/dependency prioritization;
- replacing weak distractors and answer-echo rationales with plausible alternatives and explanatory feedback.

## What did not change

The revision preserves all item IDs, answer keys, objectives, competencies, controlled references, Bloom levels, difficulty labels, final item membership, blueprint weighting, test-to-teaching mappings, course-derived provenance, and the prohibitions on Encyclopedia substitution and untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check distractor plausibility, traceability and KPI reasoning accuracy, record-correction integrity, peer-support authority boundaries, handoff realism, rationale proportionality to the controlled references, occupational authenticity, and accessible wording.

## Validation boundary

This revision improves academic assessment quality but does not establish psychometric validity, reliability, fairness, standard setting, secure credential-form suitability, practical validity, or professional credential authorization.
