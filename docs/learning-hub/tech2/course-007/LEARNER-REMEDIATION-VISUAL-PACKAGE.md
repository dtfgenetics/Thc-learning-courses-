## Evidence-centered loop
Orient → observe/measure → compare → reason → act within authority → document → reassess in an equivalent context.

### Outcome 1
**Outcome:** Reconcile physical and recorded crop/lot identities while preserving genealogy and controlled correction evidence.

**Applied artifact:** Build a reconstructable evidence packet with exact identifiers/context, observations/measurements, comparison or baseline, bounded interpretation, uncertainty, authorized response, escalation/ownership, and handoff.

**Failure pattern:** Learner forces closure, ignores denominator/baseline or data quality, overwrites conflicting history, or crosses the supplied authority boundary.

**Remediation:** Rebuild the chain **evidence → comparison → bounded inference → authorized action**. State at least one unresolved alternative or dependency and the evidence needed to discriminate it.

**Equivalent reassessment:** Change crop/lot/equipment identifiers, measurement values, timeline, spatial distribution, constraints, or downstream consequence while preserving the same competency demand.

**Visual concept 1:** Outcome-specific workflow/evidence diagram showing source data, comparison, uncertainty, authority boundary, and next-owner handoff.

## Integrated application
Use a multi-stage shift case that forces reprioritization after new information. Earlier evidence must remain visible. The learner updates the decision, preserves controlled history, and transfers unresolved work with ownership and next verification.

Required outputs: priority/dependency map; source/identifier table; evidence-quality check; measurements/observations; ranked reasoning; authority decision; verification or containment result; reconciliation; final handoff.
