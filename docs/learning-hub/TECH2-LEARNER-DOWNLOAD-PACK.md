## Quality rules

All eight Technician II metadata objects are `published` + `public` for owner-approved academic learner use. Each CSV uses descriptive column headers, contains no executable formulas, and is served through the existing controlled downloads API. The automated download test verifies file existence, production visibility for these eight published assets, continued hiding of unrelated draft assets, safe route behavior, and complete Technician II course coverage.

## Content boundaries

The templates intentionally avoid universal pH, EC, VPD, irrigation, drying, water-activity, pesticide, nutrient-recipe, or disposition targets. Learners are directed to the effective site procedure, applicable law/label, assigned crop plan, calibrated measurement process, and authorized supervisor or quality role where a decision depends on local requirements.

## Maintenance

When a Technician II course objective, authority boundary, assessment evidence requirement, or controlled terminology changes, review the mapped download in the same change set. Add new learner aids freely when they improve instruction; this manifest is a coverage floor, not an asset-count ceiling.
