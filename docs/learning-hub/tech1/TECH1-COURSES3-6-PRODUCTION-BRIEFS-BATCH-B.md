# Technician I Courses 3–6 — Production Briefs Batch B

These briefs govern the remaining 20 production raster replacements. Canonical lesson/reference records remain authoritative.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-003-005 — Environmental measurement practice
**Purpose:** printable record separating supplied targets, observations, representativeness, verification, uncertainty and handoff.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG practice sheet.
**Evidence:** REF-VPD-002; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Required fields:** location, date/time, instrument ID, temperature, RH, units, supplied target/alarm context, verification action, status/handoff.
**Prohibited:** prefilled universal targets; tiny decorative fields.
**Acceptance:** usable in print and screen annotation; generous writing space.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-003-006 — PPFD/sensor verification practice
**Purpose:** practice repeatable canopy PPFD grid measurement and sensor-outlier verification.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG worksheet.
**Evidence:** REF-LIGHT-001; REF-CANNABIS-LIGHT-2021-646020; REF-VPD-002; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Required:** nine-point grid, measurement plane, instrument ID, photoperiod/context, outlier verification and escalation fields.
**Prohibited:** universal PPFD target.
**Acceptance:** PPFD unit notation accurate; grid remains readable when printed.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-004-004 — Irrigation work-order delivery
**Purpose:** distinguish controller/work-order state from verified crop delivery.
**Mode:** photoreal irrigation context plus raster workflow.
**Evidence:** REF-IRRIGATION-001.
**Composition:** controller/work order -> representative emitters/containers -> measured delivery -> compare/document -> escalate exception.
**Prohibited:** controller status presented as proof of delivery; universal irrigation volume.
**Acceptance:** representative verification is visually explicit.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-004-005 — Irrigation fault handoff
**Purpose:** teach verification and reconstructable handoff for delivery, meter or root-zone exceptions.
**Mode:** branded raster workflow.
**Evidence:** REF-IRRIGATION-001; REF-NUTRITION-001; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Required:** observation, verification, authority boundary, escalation, current status, next owner.
**Prohibited:** automatic nutrient diagnosis from EC or symptoms.
**Acceptance:** unresolved status cannot visually appear closed/resolved.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-004-006 — Water/solution measurement practice
**Purpose:** practice sample identity and pH/EC measurement documentation.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG worksheet.
**Evidence:** REF-UMASS-PH-EC-MONITORING-001; REF-WATER-ALKALINITY-EXT-001; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Required:** sample ID/source, meter ID/readiness, calibration/verification record, pH, EC with units, method/context, repeat check, evidence limits.
**Prohibited:** nutrient-specific conclusion field based only on EC.
**Acceptance:** sample identity remains linked to every recorded measurement.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-004-007 — Irrigation verification practice
**Purpose:** practice representative delivery, root-zone trend description, deviation handling and handoff.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG worksheet.
**Evidence:** REF-IRRIGATION-001; REF-NUTRITION-001; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Required:** work order, representative sample points, delivered amount/time, drainage/root-zone observations, context, deviation/handoff.
**Prohibited:** one dryback duration labeled ideal.
**Acceptance:** multiple sample locations supported.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-002 — Canopy work-order scope/stop boundary
**Purpose:** show controlled canopy work within authorization and clear stop/escalate boundaries.
**Mode:** photoreal canopy-work scene plus decision overlay.
**Evidence:** canonical Course 5 canopy/safety references.
**Composition:** verify plant/zone/work order -> inspect conditions -> perform authorized task -> document; separate STOP branch for mismatch/unsafe/unapproved condition.
**Prohibited:** unsafe cutting posture; uncontrolled aggressive plant manipulation as universal practice.
**Acceptance:** work-order identity and stop boundary are prominent.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-004 — Sign versus symptom evidence
**Purpose:** distinguish observed plant response from direct evidence of a causal agent.
**Mode:** photoreal macro comparison board.
**Evidence:** REF-IPM-001; REF-IPM-GREENHOUSE-2024-001.
**Composition:** symptom examples (chlorosis/necrosis/distortion) contrasted with observable signs/evidence where appropriate; verification/context checklist.
**Prohibited:** appearance-only diagnosis; fabricated pest/pathogen morphology.
**Acceptance:** captions use observation language and uncertainty correctly.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-005 — Biosecurity/quarantine flow
**Purpose:** show how sanitation, isolation and controlled movement reduce cross-contamination risk.
**Mode:** photoreal sanitation/quarantine context plus raster flow.
**Evidence:** REF-IPM-001; REF-IPM-GREENHOUSE-2024-001; REF-CANNABIS-PROPAGATION-2020-958.
**Required:** clean/dirty boundary, tools/PPE or sanitation context, incoming material decision, isolation/escalation.
**Prohibited:** pesticide as substitute for biosecurity.
**Acceptance:** movement direction and boundary are immediately understandable.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-006 — Integrated crop-care handoff
**Purpose:** connect observation, authorized crop care, documentation and unresolved-condition handoff.
**Mode:** educational composite.
**Evidence:** REF-IPM-001; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY and lesson-specific crop-care references.
**Required:** before observation, action authorized/performed, after observation, exceptions, next owner/time.
**Prohibited:** undocumented corrective action.
**Acceptance:** chronology reconstructable without explanatory prose.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-007 — Propagation workstation practice
**Purpose:** practice source identity, sanitation, propagation setup and traceability.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG worksheet.
**Evidence:** REF-PROP-001; REF-CANNABIS-PROPAGATION-2020-958; REF-IPM-001.
**Required:** source/donor ID, propagule IDs/count, tool/workstation sanitation, media/environment context, exceptions, disposition.
**Prohibited:** fixed rooting hormone or environmental recipe presented universally.
**Acceptance:** traceability from source through resulting propagules.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-008 — IPM scouting practice
**Purpose:** practice systematic scouting and evidence recording.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG scouting sheet.
**Evidence:** REF-IPM-001; REF-IPM-GREENHOUSE-2024-001.
**Required:** room/zone map, route, date/time, observation location, sign/symptom, incidence/severity field, photo/sample reference, uncertainty, escalation.
**Prohibited:** diagnosis required for every observation.
**Acceptance:** supports “unknown/unresolved” honestly.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-009 — Canopy work-order practice
**Purpose:** practice authorization, before/after evidence, exceptions and handoff for canopy tasks.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG worksheet.
**Evidence:** canonical Course 5 canopy/safety/data-integrity references.
**Required:** zone/plant group, authorized task, before state, completed action, after state, exception/stop condition, operator/time.
**Prohibited:** universal training schedule.
**Acceptance:** task scope cannot be confused with horticultural target.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-006-002 — Controlled harvest receiving flow
**Purpose:** preserve identity and controlled handoff from harvest action into receiving.
**Mode:** photoreal harvest/receiving context plus raster workflow.
**Evidence:** Course 6 safety/traceability references; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY where mapped.
**Required:** source/batch identity, container identity, transfer, receiving verification, discrepancy path.
**Prohibited:** unlabeled harvested material; potency/quality inferred from appearance.
**Acceptance:** source and receiving records visibly reconcile.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-006-003 — Source-to-output genealogy
**Purpose:** explain traceability relationships from source material to downstream output records.
**Mode:** branded raster genealogy diagram.
**Evidence:** canonical Course 6 traceability/data-integrity references.
**Required:** unique IDs, parent/source link, split/merge representation where lesson permits, timestamps/status.
**Prohibited:** ambiguous arrows or reused IDs.
**Acceptance:** learner can reconstruct ancestry from output back to source.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-006-004 — Reconciliation discrepancy
**Purpose:** teach expected-versus-observed reconciliation and exception handling.
**Mode:** branded raster comparison/decision diagram.
**Evidence:** canonical Course 6 traceability/data-integrity references.
**Required:** expected record, observed record, compare, match closeout, mismatch hold/document/escalate.
**Prohibited:** silently editing a record to force agreement.
**Acceptance:** discrepancy remains visible until resolved by authorized process.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-006-005 — Dry-room handoff
**Purpose:** teach controlled transfer into postharvest drying with identity, environment/status and receiving responsibility.
**Mode:** photoreal dry-room handoff scene plus controlled annotations.
**Evidence:** REF-CANNABIS-POSTHARVEST-2025-414; canonical Course 6 safety/traceability references.
**Required:** batch/container identity, receiving location, time, condition/status, receiver, exception path.
**Prohibited:** one drying temperature/RH/duration as universally correct.
**Acceptance:** transfer ownership and identity are unambiguous.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-006-006 — Harvest readiness practice
**Purpose:** practice pre-harvest authorization, safety, identity, destination and stop-work checks.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG worksheet.
**Evidence:** REF-NIOSH-CANNABIS-HAZARDS-2024; REF-OSHA-1910-22-WALKING; Course 6 traceability references.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-006-007 — Traceability reconciliation practice

**Evidence:** canonical Course 6 traceability/data-integrity references.

**Prohibited:** fields encouraging retroactive fabrication.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-006-008 — Dry-room handoff practice
**Purpose:** practice complete postharvest transfer documentation.
**Mode:** high-resolution PNG worksheet.
**Evidence:** REF-CANNABIS-POSTHARVEST-2025-414; Course 6 traceability references.
**Required:** batch/container, source, destination, transfer time, receiving status, environmental/context record where required by SOP, sender/receiver, exception.
