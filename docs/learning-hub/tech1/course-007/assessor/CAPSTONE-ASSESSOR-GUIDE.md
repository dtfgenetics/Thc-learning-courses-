## Evaluator principle

Score evidence of integrated technician performance, not isolated correct answers. A candidate should demonstrate controlled execution, traceable evidence, role discipline and continuity across the shift.

## Domain guidance

### Safety and biosecurity — 30

Observe hazard recognition, stop-work behavior, sanitation, clean/dirty transitions, containment and protection of people/crop/material. Serious unresolved safety continuation is governed by `CF-SAFETY-001`.

### Crop observation and environment — 25

Score representative observation, location/time/sensor context, bounded interpretation and appropriate escalation rather than unsupported diagnosis.

### Water and irrigation — 25

Score work-order/sample identity, pH/EC measurement context, representative delivery verification, actual-versus-planned records and local-fault handling within authority.

### IPM scouting — 25

Score route completeness, sign/symptom classification, incidence/severity/pattern/trend evidence, biosecurity and treatment-authority boundaries.

### Production execution — 25

Score the primary propagation or canopy workstation plus shorter non-primary decisions. Preserve identity, sanitation, work-order thresholds, tissue/material condition and stop/hold decisions.

### Harvest/postharvest/traceability — 25

Score authorization, clean handling, source-to-output genealogy, holds, receiving and quantity reconciliation. Identity-breaking actions are governed by `CF-IDENTITY-001`; unauthorized hold release by `CF-HOLD-001`.

### Documentation/data integrity — 25

Score attributable, reconstructable records and preservation of original evidence. Falsification/forced matching is governed by `CF-INTEGRITY-001`.

### Prioritization/escalation/handoff — 20

Score shift triage, dynamic-event reprioritization, correct authority decisions, downstream impact awareness and end-of-shift continuity. Unauthorized treatment/repair/product-release decisions are governed by `CF-AUTHORITY-001`.

## Critical-failure handling

The development blueprint defines five rules that block acceptance of credential evidence for the affected performance:

- `CF-SAFETY-001`
- `CF-IDENTITY-001`
- `CF-INTEGRITY-001`
- `CF-AUTHORITY-001`
- `CF-HOLD-001`

If triggered:

1. record the exact observed evidence and time/phase;
2. identify the controlled rule triggered;
3. do not erase the score evidence;
4. mark the affected credential evidence as blocked pending remediation/reevaluation;
5. route through the approved adjudication process if evaluator interpretation is disputed;
6. require affected-domain reevaluation using an equivalent form after remediation.

Do not invent additional critical failures during administration.

## Prompt control

Credential mode must not provide answer hints. Any procedural prompt permitted by the controlled form must be standardized and logged. Unplanned coaching can invalidate comparability and must be documented.

## Equivalent forms

Equivalent forms may vary room maps, IDs, measurements, work orders, images, problem locations, primary production station, event injects and harvest exceptions. They must preserve the same blueprint, scoring opportunities, authority demands and approximate complexity.

## Evidence package

Retain at minimum:

- form/version;
- candidate identifier;
- evaluator identifier;
- timestamps/event stream where available;
- station outputs;
- category/domain scores;
- prompt/assistance log;
- critical-failure record if applicable;
- deviations/holds/escalations;
- final shift log/handoff;
- second-rater evidence when used;
- adjudication record when needed.

## Public/portfolio boundary

The sanitized learner/employer performance summary may report validated capability domains and approved artifacts. It must not expose secure form content, answer keys, protected event logic or private evaluator notes.

## Validation boundary

This guide does not establish inter-rater reliability, equivalent-form validity, fairness, cut-score validity or program approval. Those remain evidence-dependent release gates.
