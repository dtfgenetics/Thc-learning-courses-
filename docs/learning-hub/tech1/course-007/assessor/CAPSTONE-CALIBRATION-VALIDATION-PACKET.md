# Technician I Capstone Calibration & Validation Packet

**Capstone:** `CAPSTONE-TECH1-SHIFT-001`  
**State:** evidence-capture packet prepared; human validation not completed

## Purpose

Collect real evidence needed to determine whether the integrated capstone is consistently scored, equivalent across forms/delivery modes, operationally feasible, accessible and appropriate for credential decisions. This template does not itself validate the capstone or the 160/200 development target.

## Calibration session record

Capture:

- date;
- form/version;
- delivery mode;
- evaluator identities/qualifications;
- benchmark/sample performance;
- independent domain scores before discussion;
- total scores;
- critical-failure decisions;
- prompt/assistance differences;
- pass/status agreement under the development rule;
- domain-level disagreements;
- adjudication rationale;
- ambiguous source/form wording;
- proposed changes.

## Domain calibration prompts

For each of the eight scoring domains, evaluators should independently identify:

- evidence required for full credit;
- evidence supporting partial credit;
- behaviors that are merely stylistic and should not affect score;
- evidence indicating an authority-boundary problem;
- whether the same evidence is counted in another domain and, if so, how double counting is prevented.

## Critical-failure calibration

For each controlled critical-failure rule, benchmark at least:

- a clear trigger case;
- a near-miss/non-trigger case;
- an ambiguous case requiring adjudication guidance.

Record why the rule applies or does not apply based on observable behavior, not inferred intent alone.

## Equivalent-form review

Compare alternate forms for:

- all eight domain opportunities;
- primary production workstation balance;
- number/severity of event injects;
- safety/identity/integrity/authority/hold challenge burden;
- numerical/calculation burden;
- documentation burden;
- number of irreversible decisions;
- completion time;
- access to evidence needed for defensible responses.

Equivalent does not mean identical surface content. It means comparable opportunity and demand against the same blueprint.

## Delivery-mode equivalence

For digital simulation, instructor-run tabletop/lab and workplace equivalent, document:

- directly observed behaviors;
- simulated/proxy evidence;
- tool/sanitation behaviors that cannot be inferred from text responses alone;
- safety controls;
- evaluator visibility;
- record authenticity;
- whether scoring confidence differs materially by mode.

## Pilot evidence

Collect:

- candidate population and experience;
- sample size;
- completion time distribution;
- domain score distributions;
- total score distribution;
- critical-failure frequency/reasons;
- inter-rater differences;
- form differences;
- candidate questions/ambiguities;
- accessibility/accommodation issues;
- operational failures;
- evidence of floor/ceiling effects;
- qualitative employer/SME relevance feedback where appropriate.

## Decision-rule/standard-setting evidence

The current development target is 160/200. Formal review must document the method and rationale used to retain or revise:

- capstone total-score threshold;
- practical thresholds;
- non-compensatory/critical-failure rules;
- any domain minimums;
- relationship between practicals and capstone;
- retest consequences.

Do not infer validity from the mathematical fact that 160 is 80%.

## Required release evidence

Before a secure credential form can be approved, retain evidence of:

- SME/technical approval;
- instructional/assessment approval;
- accessibility/accommodation approval;
- pilot results;
- evaluator calibration/inter-rater review;
- equivalent-form review;
- standard setting/decision-rule approval;
- candidate-evidence retention/privacy approval;
- assessment security review;
- program release approval.
