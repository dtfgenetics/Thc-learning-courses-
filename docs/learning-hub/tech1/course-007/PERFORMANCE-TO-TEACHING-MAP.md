## Boundaries

- No encyclopedia topic alone can justify a scored practical/capstone requirement.
- No external reading alone can create a new scored performance criterion.
- Course 7 may combine previously taught tasks under time pressure and dynamic events because integration/reprioritization is taught directly in Course 7.
- Pesticide/treatment selection, unauthorized repair/bypass, and independent product-release decisions remain outside Technician I authority.
- The 80/100 practical targets and 160/200 capstone target remain development values until formal validation/standard setting approves final decision rules.
