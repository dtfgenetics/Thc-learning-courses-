# Course 7 Integrated Evidence Dossier

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-007 — Integrated Cultivation Technician Practice Lab`  
**State:** development evidence/control package

## Evidence architecture

Course 7 does not introduce a new scientific theory layer. It integrates evidence-backed principles and controlled operating boundaries already established across Courses 1–6. The integrated lab therefore depends on the canonical references attached to the task being performed and must not invent cross-domain conclusions that exceed those sources.

## Stable evidence rules across the integrated shift

1. **Safety evidence is contextual.** NIOSH/OSHA sources support hazard-recognition principles; site-specific assessment and procedures remain controlling.
2. **Plant/environment observations are not automatically diagnoses.** Preserve location, time, method and comparison context.
3. **pH/EC and irrigation evidence stays bounded.** One reading or symptom does not establish a specific nutrient/root-cause diagnosis.
4. **IPM evidence and pesticide authority are separate.** Scouting may support observation, mapping, isolation/hold and escalation; treatment selection/application requires separate authority.
5. **Propagation/canopy work follows controlled work orders.** Literature does not authorize personal technique preferences to override supplied thresholds.
6. **Traceability is genealogy plus events, not labels alone.** Source/output relationships, movements, transformations and destinations must remain reconstructable.
7. **Original evidence is preserved.** Discrepancies are investigated; measurements/counts/weights are not falsified to close the record.
8. **Product receiving/hold status is not independent release authority.**
9. **Uncertainty may remain open.** A defensible handoff can contain unresolved conditions and required follow-up.

## Assessment evidence boundary

The 12-item readiness assessment is low-stakes formative diagnostic evidence. Secure credential decisions are intended to rely on approved practical/capstone evidence under controlled forms and evaluator governance.

## Critical-failure evidence rule

Critical-failure decisions require observable evidence tied to the controlled rule. Evaluators should not infer a critical failure from vague intent or a harmless wording difference. If the evidence is ambiguous, use the approved adjudication process and preserve the uncertainty.

## Human technical review questions

Reviewers should verify:

- integrated scenarios do not create contradictions between prior-course evidence rules;
- task packets contain enough evidence for defensible decisions;
- authority boundaries remain consistent across stations;
- critical-failure rules target genuinely serious competency failures rather than stylistic differences;
- capstone scoring domains do not double-count the same behavior unfairly;
- alternate forms preserve comparable evidence opportunity;
- the integrated handoff reflects actual entry-level occupational expectations.

## Release boundary

This dossier documents evidence architecture. It does not validate the capstone, critical-failure rules, cut score, evaluator reliability, accessibility, privacy/security or professional credential.
