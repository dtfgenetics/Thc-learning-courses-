# Course 7 Remediation & Retest Matrix

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-007`  
**Profile:** integrated performance lab

Course 7 remediation is domain-based and evidence-based. It must not become simple answer review followed by the same form.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-007-01` — shift prioritization

**Corrective coaching:** reconstruct current state from handoff, holds, hazards, timing and authority before ordering tasks.  
**Return-to-practice:** fresh opening packet with different hazards, holds and time-sensitive work; learner must reprioritize and state evidence.  
**Retest:** equivalent form with new surface details and the same prioritization/authority demands.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-007-02` — room entry and observation

**Corrective coaching:** safe entry, sanitation sequence, representative observation, measurement context and stop-work boundaries.  
**Return-to-practice:** new room packet with different sensor/location discrepancy and crop observation.  
**Retest:** affected Practical A domain or equivalent integrated station as approved.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-007-03` — water and irrigation

**Corrective coaching:** sample/work-order identity, meter context, pH/EC evidence limits, representative delivery and actual-versus-planned records.  
**Return-to-practice:** new irrigation packet with a different local fault and root-zone pattern.  
**Retest:** affected Practical B domain or equivalent integrated station as approved.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-007-04` — IPM + propagation/canopy integration

**Corrective coaching:** representative scouting, sign/symptom evidence, biosecurity transitions, source/plant identity, supplied production thresholds and treatment-authority limits.  
**Return-to-practice:** fresh scouting plus alternate production-workstation packet.  
**Retest:** affected Practical C/D/E domain(s) or equivalent integrated station as approved.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-007-05` — harvest/postharvest transition

**Corrective coaching:** source/output genealogy, destination, contamination holds, receiving, reconciliation and release-authority boundaries.  
**Return-to-practice:** new harvest packet with different identity/destination/discrepancy conditions.  
**Retest:** affected Practical F domain or equivalent integrated station as approved.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-007-06` — dynamic event, integrity and handoff

**Corrective coaching:** recognize/stabilize, reprioritize, preserve evidence, escalate, reconcile and hand off.  
**Return-to-practice:** new event inject with different downstream effects and unresolved condition.  
**Retest:** equivalent capstone/event-inject form or affected practical domain as approved.

## Critical-failure remediation

If a performance triggers one of the five development critical-failure rules in `LABPLAN-TECH1-001`:

- credential evidence from that affected performance is not accepted;
- remediation is mandatory before reevaluation;
- the affected domain must be reevaluated using an equivalent secure form;
- the original event and evaluator rationale remain in the controlled evidence record;
- the learner must not receive the same secure form or exposed answer path;
- final retest rules require human governance approval.

## Retest governance

- equivalent form required;
- same competency blueprint and comparable demand required;
- secure credential forms remain non-public;
- accommodation must preserve the competency being evaluated;
- evaluator prompts/assistance must be controlled and recorded;
- repeated failure routes to formal instructor/assessor review;
- remediation completion alone does not create credential eligibility.

## Readiness assessment remediation

Misses on the 12-item M01 readiness assessment should route to the relevant prior-course lesson/job aid and a fresh scenario. The readiness bank must not be treated as a secure credential exam and should not be used as the sole decision rule for credential issuance.
