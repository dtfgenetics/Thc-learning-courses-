# Course 7 Integrated Lab Learner Packet

**Course:** Integrated Cultivation Technician Practice Lab  
**State:** development learner package

## Purpose

Course 7 integrates the preceding Technician I courses into realistic shift practice. The learner is expected to prioritize, execute controlled work, preserve evidence, respond to change and hand off unresolved conditions. This is not an invitation to act as cultivation director, pesticide applicator, qualified repair technician or product-release authority.

## Opening packet

A complete practice/readiness/capstone form may include:

- facility/room map;
- previous-shift handoff;
- shift schedule;
- assigned SOP excerpts;
- work orders;
- current room dashboards;
- crop/batch/cultivar identities;
- irrigation and scouting records;
- propagation/canopy status;
- harvest/postharvest schedule;
- hold/deviation information;
- escalation contacts and authority boundaries;
- blank shift log and station records.

## Lab sequence

### Station 1 — Shift triage

Classify work as immediate, time-sensitive, routine, defer or clarify-before-execution. State the evidence, consequence and authority boundary supporting the priority.

### Station 2 — Room inspection

Apply safe-entry/biosecurity rules, inspect representative crop/environment evidence, preserve sensor/location/time context and document conditions requiring follow-up.

### Station 3 — Water and irrigation

Verify work order/sample identity, record pH/EC with method and units, execute authorized irrigation, verify representative delivery and preserve actual-versus-planned results.

### Station 4 — IPM scouting

Follow the representative route, distinguish symptom/sign/insufficient evidence, use incidence/severity/pattern/trend evidence, maintain biosecurity and escalate treatment decisions outside Technician I authority.

### Station 5 — Production workstation

One equivalent form assigns propagation as the deep execution station and another assigns canopy. The other domain appears as a shorter decision scenario. Preserve identity, sanitation, supplied thresholds and stop conditions.

### Station 6 — Harvest/postharvest transition

Verify authorization/source identity/destination, preserve genealogy, hold suspect material, receive into the correct location and reconcile quantities without forcing records to agree.

### Station 7 — Dynamic event

A randomized event may include alarm, leak, damaged tool, pest finding, missing label, irrigation fault, count shortage, dry-room excursion or handoff discrepancy. Decide:

1. stop/continue/hold;
2. immediate safe action;
3. evidence/documentation;
4. escalation;
5. downstream tasks affected.

### Station 8 — Shift reconciliation and handoff

Close the shift with completed/incomplete tasks, measurements, observations, movements, holds, deviations, escalation and next-shift priorities. The handoff must stand alone for a person who did not participate in the shift.

## Reuse of governed learner job aids

Course 7 should reuse the relevant governed worksheets and visuals from Courses 2–6 rather than creating conflicting duplicate instructions. Practice forms may surface the latest controlled versions of observation, environmental, irrigation, scouting, propagation/canopy, harvest, reconciliation and handoff job aids.

## Readiness assessment

`ASSESS-LH-TECH1-007-M01` is formative readiness evidence only. It may diagnose weak domains and route remediation before practical/capstone work. It is not the professional credential examination.

## Practice, readiness and credential modes

### Practice mode

- hints/feedback may be available;
- reset/retry allowed;
- evidence and rationale can be shown after decisions;
- no credential evidence implied.

### Readiness mode

- limited hints;
- feedback after workstation or submission;
- competency/domain breakdown;
- routes learner to remediation;
- no credential evidence implied unless governance explicitly says otherwise.

### Credential mode

- secure equivalent form;
- no answer hints;
- full evidence/event record;
- evaluator controls active;
- form remains unavailable until `liveCredentialFormApproved` becomes true after all release gates.

## Critical-failure awareness

The current development blueprint identifies five behaviors that block acceptance of credential evidence: serious unresolved safety continuation, genealogy-breaking identity actions, falsification/forced evidence, unauthorized treatment/repair/release actions and unauthorized movement/release from an active hold. Practice/readiness forms should teach these boundaries without exposing secure credential-form specifics beyond the public competency rules.

## Completion boundary

Course 7 completion, readiness status or a practice capstone score does not by itself issue the Technician I credential. Professional issuance requires the approved credential decision rule, validated performance evidence and release authorization.
