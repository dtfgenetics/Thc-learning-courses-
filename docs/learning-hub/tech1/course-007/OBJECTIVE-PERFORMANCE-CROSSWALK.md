# Course 7 Objective & Performance Crosswalk

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-007 — Integrated Cultivation Technician Practice Lab`  
**Profile:** integrated performance lab; no ordinary course final  
**State:** development / machine package

Course 7 is intentionally different from Courses 2–6. Its 12-item assessment is formative readiness evidence. Credential-bearing performance is intended to come from validated Practicals A–F plus the integrated capstone, not from a conventional multiple-choice final.

**Instruction rule:** Course 7 integrates already taught Technician I competencies and directly teaches the integration workflow in four dedicated lab lessons. A readiness item, practical criterion, or capstone score domain may not introduce a new scored requirement that exists only in the 420 Encyclopedia, an external reading, or an untaught blueprint.

## Required evidence layers

A machine-complete Course 7 package must resolve and preserve:

1. four dedicated integrated-lab lessons;
2. six controlled integrated objectives;
3. 12-item formative readiness assessment with objective coverage;
4. six controlled practical definitions;
5. 200-point capstone blueprint;
6. five controlled critical-failure rules;
7. equivalent-form requirements;
8. evaluator evidence/calibration protocol;
9. remediation and equivalent-retest rules;
10. accessibility/accommodation review packet;
11. candidate-evidence retention/privacy draft;
12. secure credential-form separation;
13. explicit distinction between development scoring targets and validated credential decision rules.

## Practical set

- `PRACTICAL-TECH1-A` — Safe Room Entry & Crop Inspection
- `PRACTICAL-TECH1-B` — Water & Irrigation Shift
- `PRACTICAL-TECH1-C` — IPM Scouting Route
- `PRACTICAL-TECH1-D` — Propagation Workstation
- `PRACTICAL-TECH1-E` — Canopy Maintenance Work Order
- `PRACTICAL-TECH1-F` — Harvest-to-Dry-Room Handoff

Each practical currently has a 100-point development scoring model and target pass of 80/100. Those targets remain development rules until the applicable standard-setting/decision-rule process approves them.

## Capstone

`CAPSTONE-TECH1-SHIFT-001` contains eight score domains totaling 200 points with a current development target of 160/200. It integrates decisions and evidence across all Technician I domains and uses rotating equivalent forms.

## Critical-failure control

The controlled lab plan defines five development critical-failure rules that block acceptance of credential evidence until remediation/affected-domain reevaluation:

- `CF-SAFETY-001` — knowingly continue through an unresolved serious/life-safety hazard;
- `CF-IDENTITY-001` — knowingly break genealogy after an unresolved identity conflict;
- `CF-INTEGRITY-001` — falsify/overwrite/force evidence to hide a discrepancy;
- `CF-AUTHORITY-001` — perform unauthorized pesticide/treatment, repair/bypass or product-release decision;
- `CF-HOLD-001` — knowingly move/release material from an active hold without authorization.

These are development governance controls, not psychometrically validated final rules. Human review and pilot evidence remain required.

## Release boundary

Passing the readiness assessment, practice mode, or any unvalidated practical/capstone form does not authorize the professional credential. The secure credential form remains disabled until all release gates are met.
