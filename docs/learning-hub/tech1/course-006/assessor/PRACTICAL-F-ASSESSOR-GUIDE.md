# Course 6 Practical F Assessor Guide

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-006 — Harvest, Postharvest, Traceability & Shift Handoff`  
**Practical:** `PRACTICAL-TECH1-F — Harvest-to-Dry-Room Handoff`  
**State:** draft assessor support; validation/calibration evidence open

## Controlled scoring model

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| authorization/scope/safety verification | 10 |
| sanitation and clean handling | 15 |
| lot identity/traceability | 25 |
| harvest workflow execution | 15 |
| postharvest receiving/environment interpretation | 15 |
| quantity reconciliation | 10 |
| records/handoff | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Current target pass:** 80/100

This guide does not change those weights or validate the pass rule.

## Observable evidence

Evaluate whether the candidate can:

- verify current harvest authorization, room, batch and destination;
- check work area, PPE, pathways, tools and hazards before and during the task;
- stop or escalate changed unsafe conditions;
- prepare clean tools, cart, labels and receiving area;
- preserve lot identity through cut, movement and receiving;
- create/verify source-to-output genealogy when material changes container/lot/receiving identity;
- isolate or hold suspect material under the supplied process;
- keep dirty, mixed or unlabeled material out of normal flow;
- resolve destination/identity/hold/instruction conflicts without undocumented improvisation;
- record quantity and destination;
- receive into the correct identified rack/location;
- interpret basic dry-room/product-condition information without a fixed-day assumption;
- reconcile outgoing and receiving quantities without forcing false agreement;
- produce a complete handoff with unresolved conditions and next action.

## Category guidance

### Authorization/scope/safety — 10

Credit verification and changed-condition response. Do not give full credit merely because the candidate eventually reaches the correct destination if they ignored an unresolved hazard or unauthorized change.

### Sanitation and clean handling — 15

Observe actual preparation and response to dirty/suspect conditions. Clean technique must persist through movement and receiving.

### Lot identity/traceability — 25

This is the highest-weight category. Score whether source identity remains connected to new output/container/receiving identities and whether held material remains inside the genealogy. A correct physical move with a broken identity chain is not a strong performance.

### Harvest workflow execution — 15

Score adherence to the controlled work order, protection of material condition and handling of conflicts within Technician I authority.

### Receiving/environment interpretation — 15

Score correct receiving location/identity plus bounded use of supplied environmental/product-condition evidence. Do not reward unsupported final drying/release conclusions.

### Quantity reconciliation — 10

Both original attributable values must remain visible when they differ. The candidate should route the mismatch through the supplied discrepancy process, not overwrite or average it.

### Records/handoff — 10

The receiving role should be able to reconstruct source/output identity, destination, quantity/status, holds/deviations, actions, escalation and next required action.

## Evaluator rules

1. Score observed behavior and evidence, not presumed intent.
2. Use the controlled candidate packet and seeded conditions.
3. Record any permitted prompts consistently.
4. Preserve evidence for deductions and borderline decisions.
5. Do not invent unapproved critical-failure rules in the assessor session.
6. Do not award out-of-scope credit for independent product release/disposition decisions.
7. A plausible root-cause guess does not replace evidence, traceability or handoff quality.
8. Do not allow strength in one category to obscure a material traceability/safety issue unless an approved credential decision rule explicitly permits compensation.

## Evidence to retain for calibration/pilot

- practical/form version;
- delivery mode;
- assessor identity;
- seeded conditions;
- category scores and total;
- candidate artifacts;
- prompt/use-of-assistance record;
- evidence supporting deductions;
- assessor uncertainty;
- second-rater score where applicable;
- adjudication outcome where ratings differ.

## Validation boundary

This guide operationalizes an existing development practical. It does not establish evaluator qualification, inter-rater reliability, fairness, form equivalence, pass-score validity or credential-release approval. Those require recorded human evidence.
