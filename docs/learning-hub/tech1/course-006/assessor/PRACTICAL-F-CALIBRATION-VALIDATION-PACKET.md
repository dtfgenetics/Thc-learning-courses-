## Validation questions

Confirm whether:

- authorization, batch and destination information are sufficient and realistic;
- changed safety/pathway conditions yield observable stop/reroute/escalation behavior;
- sanitation and suspect-material handling are visible and scoreable;
- source-to-output genealogy can be judged consistently;
- destination/identity/hold conflicts have one defensible authority-boundary response;
- quantity reconciliation supplies enough information to preserve both original measurements and identify the discrepancy state;
- dry-room environmental/product-condition evidence supports bounded interpretation without implying release authority;
- the final handoff is operationally useful and reconstructable;
- completion time and materials are appropriate to an entry-level practical.
