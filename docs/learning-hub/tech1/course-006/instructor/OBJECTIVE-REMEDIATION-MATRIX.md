# Course 6 Objective Remediation Matrix

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-006`

Use after objective-level formative or summative gaps. Reassessment must use fresh, equivalent evidence rather than the exposed scored item.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-006-01`

**Corrective coaching**  
Rebuild the readiness sequence: authorization/source identity, destination, PPE, pathways, clean tools/carts/racks, labels, task hazards and changed-condition reassessment. Emphasize that a valid work order does not authorize continuing through a newly uncontrolled hazard.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give a new readiness packet with one blocked route, one tool problem and one destination conflict. Require proceed/stop/hold/escalate decisions and the condition required before work resumes.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent new harvest-readiness scenario. Require accurate safety/scope verification and correct stop/escalation decisions.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-006-02`

**Corrective coaching**  
Review clean handling, lot separation, suspect-material holds, verified receiving and the difference between monitored drying context and a fixed-day assumption.

**Return-to-practice**  
Use a fresh transfer scenario with two identified lots, one suspect contamination condition and one receiving-location issue. Require controlled movement and receiving records.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent receiving scenario and score clean handling, hold/isolation, receiving identity/location and bounded environmental/product-condition interpretation.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-006-03`

**Corrective coaching**  
Teach genealogy as source → transformation event → output identity → destination/status. A new container label is not enough unless the source relationship is preserved.

**Return-to-practice**  
Provide one source batch split across three outputs, including a held output. Require a complete genealogy map with identities, destinations, quantities and statuses.

**Reassessment**  
Use a different transformation packet. Require reconstructable source/output links without loss of identity during relabeling or movement.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-006-04`

**Corrective coaching**  
Reinforce sanitation and contamination-control boundaries: clean tools/receiving equipment, suspect-material isolation, lot separation and prevention of dirty, mixed or unlabeled material entering normal flow.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give a new harvest scenario containing a dirty cart, suspect material and two lots staged nearby. Require containment/hold decisions and factual exception records.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent scenario and require correct sanitation, identity and hold actions without independent release decisions.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-006-05`

**Corrective coaching**  
Separate controlled Technician I execution from decisions requiring authorization: destination changes, identity conflicts, hold conflicts, process redesign, protected equipment work and product release/disposition.

**Return-to-practice**  
Provide a mixed conflict set containing one routine correction, one identity discrepancy, one unavailable destination and one proposed release decision. Require proceed/clarify/hold/escalate choices.

**Reassessment**  
Use a fresh mixed-authority scenario and require correct boundaries without improvising undocumented destinations, release or repair decisions.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-006-06`

**Corrective coaching**  
Rebuild reconciliation and handoff as preservation of evidence: outgoing value, receiving value, method/units, discrepancy status, completed/partial/held work, condition, escalation and next action. Never force agreement by changing history.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give a new packet with an attributable quantity mismatch plus one held container and incomplete receiving task. Require both original measurements, discrepancy status and a receiving-shift handoff.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent packet. Require accurate reconciliation, factual completion state and a reconstructable handoff with unresolved conditions still visible.

## Reassessment governance

- Do not reuse the exact exposed scored item.
- Preserve objective and comparable cognitive demand.
- Record remediation activity, reassessment evidence, result and remaining gap.
- Repeated failure routes to instructor/assessor review rather than automatic progression.
- Academic remediation does not substitute for Practical F performance evidence.
