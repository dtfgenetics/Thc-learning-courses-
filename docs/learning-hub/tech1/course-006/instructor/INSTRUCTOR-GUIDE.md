# Course 6 Instructor Guide

**Course:** Harvest, Postharvest, Traceability & Shift Handoff  
**State:** draft instructor support

## Instructional intent

Course 6 trains entry-level technicians to move identified material through harvest and dry-room receiving without losing safety, sanitation, genealogy, quantity truth or unresolved conditions. The course should not teach learners to improvise destinations, erase discrepancies, use fixed-day drying rules as release criteria, or assume final product-release authority.

## Instructional sequence

1. **Harvest readiness and stop-work** — authorization, source identity, destination, PPE, pathways, clean tools/carts/racks and changed-condition reassessment.
2. **Controlled harvest and receiving** — clean handling, lot separation, suspect-material holds, receiving identity and monitored postharvest context.
3. **Genealogy and reconciliation** — source-to-output transformation links, destinations, quantities, holds/waste and discrepancies.
4. **Dry-room handoff** — normal receiving, partial completion, holds, unresolved conditions, escalation and next action.

## Core teaching rule

For every harvest transition, require the learner to answer:

- What material is this and what authorizes the move?
- Where is it allowed to go?
- What quantity and condition evidence were actually recorded?
- What changed identity during the transformation and how is the source relationship preserved?
- What is complete, held, discrepant or unresolved?
- What action is within Technician I authority and what must be escalated?

## Common misconceptions

### “If the work order says harvest today, we can start even if the destination is not ready.”

Correction: the receiving destination is part of the controlled transition. Unavailable/dirty/incorrect destinations require resolution before the affected material moves.

### “A little suspect material can be removed and the rest called clean.”

Correction: follow the supplied hold/isolation and review process. Technician I does not independently release suspect material.

### “The new lot label replaces the source identity.”

Correction: the new identifier must be linked to the source transformation so genealogy remains reconstructable.

### “If outgoing and receiving weights disagree, use the later measurement.”

Correction: preserve both attributable results and reconcile through the controlled discrepancy process.

### “Drying takes X days.”

Correction: elapsed time alone is not the controlled evidence. Drying depends on product/load/environment conditions and facility-defined criteria.

### “A clean handoff means every issue needs a final answer.”

Correction: a high-quality handoff can truthfully contain holds, partial completion, open discrepancies and unresolved root cause.

## Worksheet facilitation

### Harvest readiness worksheet

Require evidence of authorization, source identity, destination, PPE, pathways, sanitation/readiness and any changed condition. Learners should identify the condition required before work resumes when they choose stop/hold.

### Traceability/reconciliation worksheet

Require source-to-output links, destinations/statuses and both original quantity values where a mismatch is seeded. Learners must not overwrite, average or choose a preferred value merely to close the packet.

### Dry-room handoff worksheet

Require source/output identity, destination, quantity/reconciliation state, product/environment condition, hold/sanitation state, deviation, authorized action, escalation and next action.

## Academic assessment use

The M01 bank is formative and should route learners to objective-specific remediation. The 24-item final is summative academic evidence and remains separate from Practical F.

## Practical preparation

Prepare learners for the observable behaviors in Practical F without coaching exact seeded answers. Practical F uses a 100-point controlled scoring model and currently states an 80/100 target pass. Its validation and decision-rule evidence remain open.

## Scope and authority

Course 6 does not confer:

- independent product release or disposition authority;
- authority to override holds;
- authority to bypass facility traceability/seed-to-sale procedures;
- authority to perform qualified equipment repair merely because a problem was recognized.

## Human review points

Review this guide for technical accuracy, role boundaries, instructional clarity, practical alignment, accessibility/accommodation feasibility and compatibility with intended facility/jurisdiction workflows.
