# Course 6 Rendered Accessibility & Learner UX Review

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-006 — Harvest, Postharvest, Traceability & Shift Handoff`  
**Target:** WCAG 2.2 AA plus learner/practical usability  
**State:** review packet prepared; rendered approval open

## Required surfaces

Review the actual rendered experience for:

- course overview;
- all four Course 6 lessons;
- five embedded instructional visuals;
- three downloadable practice worksheets;
- formative assessment;
- approved summative surface when exposed;
- resource/download links;
- navigation, progress, errors and empty/loading states.

## Visual checks

Every governed SVG should retain:

- meaningful `<title>` and `<desc>`;
- useful lesson-level `alt` text;
- readable text and labels at zoom;
- meaning not dependent on color alone;
- no clipping/reflow failures at narrow widths;
- printable worksheets with sufficient contrast, writing space and clear fields.

## Keyboard/semantic checks

- keyboard access to all controls;
- visible focus;
- logical focus order;
- programmatic state for expandable content;
- descriptive resource links;
- no hover-only critical information;
- usable assessment options and error recovery;
- headings/landmarks that reflect lesson structure.

## Responsive review

Test representative phone, small-tablet, tablet, desktop and wide-desktop widths. Verify tables/diagrams/resources remain usable without page-level horizontal overflow and that sticky navigation does not obscure headings or fields.

## Learner comprehension checks

The interface must clearly distinguish:

- authorization from schedule/expectation;
- source identity from new output identity while preserving genealogy;
- normal flow from hold/isolation;
- outgoing from receiving quantity;
- discrepancy from resolved reconciliation;
- complete from partial/held work;
- receiving status from product release/disposition;
- academic completion from Practical F and professional credential completion.

## Practical/accommodation review

Determine whether the readiness, traceability and handoff worksheets support reasonable accommodations while preserving the underlying competencies. Document which Practical F behaviors require direct observation and what equivalent evidence, if any, is approved through credential governance.
