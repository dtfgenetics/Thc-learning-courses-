**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 24 summative items

## What changed

All 24 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality while preserving the controlled blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- harvest pathway and powered-equipment safety;
- receiving-rack sanitation and suspect-product holds;
- drying-process interpretation without universal time assumptions;
- source-to-output genealogy, split lots and detached-label response;
- sanitation, hold/isolation and lot-separation controls;
- destination, release, disposal and repair authority boundaries;
- quantity reconciliation, partial completion, missing data and decision-ready handoffs;
- replacing weak distractors and template rationales with plausible alternatives and explanatory feedback.

## What did not change

The revision preserves all item IDs, answer keys, objectives, competencies, controlled references, Bloom levels, difficulty labels, final item membership, blueprint weighting, taught-material mappings, test-to-teaching mappings, and the prohibition on Encyclopedia substitution or untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check distractor plausibility, harvest/postharvest and traceability reasoning, product-control and authority boundaries, data-integrity behavior, rationale proportionality to the controlled references, occupational authenticity, and accessible wording.
