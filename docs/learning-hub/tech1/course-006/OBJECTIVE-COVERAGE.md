## Machine learning-loop requirements

Every controlled objective must retain:

1. dedicated instruction;
2. applied practice;
3. item-level formative coverage;
4. item-level summative coverage;
5. evidence references on scored items;
6. corrective coaching;
7. fresh return-to-practice;
8. equivalent reassessment;
9. Practical F mapping where performance is required.

## Performance boundary

The Course 6 written assessments do not replace Practical F. Practical F independently tests authorization/readiness, sanitation, genealogy, harvest execution, receiving interpretation, reconciliation and handoff.

## Release-authority boundary

Course 6 trains receiving status, holds, discrepancy documentation and escalation. It does not confer independent final product-release, disposition or quality-acceptance authority.

## Traceability boundary

GS1 concepts support technology-neutral identification and event genealogy. Facility and jurisdiction-specific cannabis traceability/seed-to-sale requirements remain controlling and must be reviewed separately.
