# Course 6 Evidence Dossier

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-006 — Harvest, Postharvest, Traceability & Shift Handoff`  
**State:** draft evidence package; human technical review open

## Evidence policy

Course 6 separates durable scientific/quality principles from facility and jurisdiction rules. Reviewed sources support hazard recognition, postharvest process context, traceability concepts and data integrity. They do not replace site-specific hazard assessments, cannabis seed-to-sale requirements, facility SOPs or product-release governance.

## Core evidence roles

### `REF-NIOSH-CANNABIS-HAZARDS-2024`

Government occupational-health source supporting cannabis-work hazard recognition and prevention concepts across chemical, biological, ergonomic/physical and safety hazards.

**Use for:** pre-task hazard recognition, changed-condition reassessment, safe-work principles.  
**Do not use as:** a substitute for a facility-specific hazard assessment or local safety program.

### `REF-OSHA-1910-22-WALKING`

Use for walking-working-surface/pathway safety requirements where applicable. Facility-specific procedures and jurisdictional applicability remain controlling.

### `REF-POSTHARVEST-001`

Peer-reviewed 2022 cannabis postharvest review.

**Use for:** drying/postharvest process context, the importance of controlled postharvest conditions, and the principle that process outcomes depend on conditions rather than elapsed time alone.  
**Do not use as:** authority for one universal drying duration or facility release criterion.

### `REF-IPM-001`

Use for contamination/sanitation and suspect biological material context. It does not independently establish product disposition or pesticide rules.

### `REF-GS1-GLOBAL-TRACEABILITY`

Technology-neutral traceability standard supporting identification of traceable objects, capture of transformation/movement events, source/destination/location/time relationships and genealogy.

**Important limitation:** GS1 is not cannabis-specific and does not replace jurisdictional cannabis seed-to-sale or inventory-control requirements.

### `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`

General data-integrity model supporting attributable, contemporaneous, complete, consistent and reconstructable records.

**Important limitation:** this is not cannabis law and must not be represented as a jurisdictional cannabis requirement.

## Evidence rules enforced in the course

1. **Authorization precedes movement.** Schedule or expectation does not replace the controlled work order.
2. **Changed conditions trigger reassessment.** The initial readiness check is not permanent authorization to continue through a new hazard or conflict.
3. **Suspect material remains identified and controlled.** It is not blended into normal flow to simplify counts.
4. **New identifiers extend genealogy.** They do not erase the source relationship.
5. **Original evidence is preserved.** Outgoing and receiving measurements remain visible if they differ.
6. **A discrepancy is a workflow state, not permission to edit history.**
7. **Drying is monitored context, not a universal calendar rule.**
8. **Partial completion is valid.** An unresolved hold, quantity conflict or destination issue must remain visible.
9. **Technician I does not independently release or dispose of product.**
