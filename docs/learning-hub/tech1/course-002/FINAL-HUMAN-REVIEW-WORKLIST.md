## A. Subject-matter / technical review

Reviewer must verify:

- crop-observation/scouting principles are technically accurate and scoped correctly;
- developmental/reproductive morphology claims remain within the cited evidence;
- schedule timing is not presented as universal biological proof;
- observation, interpretation and diagnosis boundaries are defensible;
- photo-evidence guidance is accurate;
- Technician I authorization/escalation language is appropriate;
- source recency and source-role boundaries are acceptable.

**Required record:** reviewer, qualifications/role, course/source versions, date, issues, dispositions, approval/rejection.

**State:** OPEN

- remediation/reassessment guidance is objective-linked and usable.
