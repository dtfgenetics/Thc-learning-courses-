# Objective Remediation & Reassessment Matrix — COURSE-LH-TECH1-002

**Course:** Plant Observation, Growth Stages & Crop Records  
**State:** controlled development guidance; human assessment review remains open  
**Date:** 2026-09-15

## Use rule

Remediation is objective-based, not answer-key replay. A learner who misses an objective receives corrective coaching plus a fresh application task before equivalent reassessment. Reassessment should measure the same objective and cognitive demand without simply repeating the original item or scenario.

This matrix supports public academic course remediation only. It does not set professional credential retake rules and does not replace practical evaluator calibration or formal standard setting.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-002-01`

**Objective:** Execute a repeatable crop-observation route that samples representative locations and preserves room, zone, plant or bench identity.

**Corrective coaching**  
Re-teach why a conspicuous or convenient plant cannot automatically represent the room. Review purpose, crop/location identity, representative coverage, denominator, and the difference between systematic sampling and targeted follow-up inspection.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give the learner a new synthetic room map with edge, center, aisle/process-zone and visibly abnormal plants distributed differently from the original example. Require the learner to mark a repeatable route, observation points, denominator, identity fields and a room-level statement proportional to the evidence collected.

**Reassessment**  
Use a new route-selection/scouting scenario. The learner must choose or construct the representative approach and explain why convenience-only or abnormal-only sampling would not support a room-level statement. Do not reuse the original keyed scenario as the sole evidence of recovery.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-002-02`

**Objective:** Describe cannabis developmental and reproductive stage from observable morphology while distinguishing biological stage from calendar timing.

**Corrective coaching**  
Reinforce that developmental-stage labels require observable morphological evidence. Review visible vegetative/reproductive structures, mixed-stage populations, the difference between schedule timing and biological observation, and the rule that unexpected reproductive structures document what is present without proving why it occurred.

**Return-to-practice**  
Provide a fresh set of synthetic plant/crop records in which schedule labels and visible morphology do not perfectly align. Require the learner to identify visible structures, assign a morphology-supported stage description, preserve schedule timing separately, identify mixed development and state what requires escalation.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent but new developmental-stage scenario. The learner must select or produce the record that correctly prioritizes visible morphology while retaining calendar information only as context. For unexpected reproductive morphology, the learner must avoid inventing an unsupported cause.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-002-03`

**Objective:** Differentiate direct observation, spatial or population pattern, contextual evidence and unsupported causal diagnosis during routine crop inspection.

**Corrective coaching**  
Review the evidence layers: direct observation, distribution/pattern, contextual history or measurements, provisional interpretation, and confirmed cause. Emphasize that familiar symptoms, timing relationships and spatial patterns can narrow possibilities without establishing a unique diagnosis.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give the learner a new multi-factor crop case with several plausible explanations. Require classification of each statement by evidence layer, a rewrite of any causal overstatement, and a list of additional evidence or qualified review needed before a stronger conclusion could be supported.

**Reassessment**  
Use a new scenario in which one clue strongly suggests—but does not prove—a cause. The learner must distinguish what is known, what is contextual, what remains a hypothesis and what should be escalated. Equivalent reassessment should preserve the analyze-level demand rather than reducing the task to terminology recall.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-002-04`

**Objective:** Create a reconstructable crop-observation record with sample context, objective wording, useful photographs, timing, identity and unresolved uncertainty.

**Corrective coaching**  
Review reconstructability: correct crop/location identity, observation time, route/sample context, objective wording, useful photo sequence, relevant context, uncertainty, action/escalation and correction history. Reinforce that a technically accurate note or photo set becomes weak evidence if identity, time or context cannot be reconstructed.

**Return-to-practice**  
Provide a flawed synthetic observation packet containing combinations of unlabeled images, missing time/location, a close-up without context, vague causal language, missing denominator and incomplete correction history. Require the learner to identify every evidence defect and rebuild the record without inventing facts that were not observed.

**Reassessment**  
Use a different synthetic observation/photo packet. The learner must determine which record/image set is reconstructable or produce the missing fields and explain why the weaker alternatives fail. The reassessment should test record quality and evidence integrity rather than memorization of field names alone.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-002-05`

**Objective:** Follow supplied observation and escalation procedures, documenting abnormal conditions and handing off unresolved findings without exceeding Technician I authority.

**Corrective coaching**  
Review the Technician I boundary: document evidence, perform only supplied/authorized checks or actions, preserve unresolved conditions, identify who was notified, and transfer a clear next-check/ownership state. Contrast useful escalation with unauthorized diagnosis, treatment selection, controller redesign or silent closure of an unresolved problem.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give the learner a fresh end-of-shift scenario involving an unresolved crop abnormality plus limited authorized checks. Require a record of what was observed, what was checked, what was not authorized, who was notified, what remains unresolved, the next required check and a receiving-person cross-check.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent new abnormal-condition/handoff scenario. The learner must choose actions within the supplied role boundary and create a handoff that allows the next qualified person to reconstruct status and continue the investigation. A response that reaches the right conclusion by exceeding authority does not demonstrate the objective.

## Documentation requirements

For each remediation event, retain at least:

- learner/course/objective identifier;
- original evidence of the gap;
- corrective coaching assigned;
- fresh return-to-practice evidence;
- reassessment form/scenario identifier;
- reassessment result;
- unresolved issue or escalation where applicable;
- date and responsible instructor/evaluator where the operating system requires it.

## Quality boundary

These remediation/reassessment patterns are intentionally expandable and contain no artificial content ceiling. Human instructional-design and assessment reviewers must still confirm equivalence, cognitive demand, fairness, accessibility and sufficiency before Course 2 moves beyond development status.
