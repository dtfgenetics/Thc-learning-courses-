# Technician I Courses 3–6 — Production Briefs Batch A

These briefs govern the first 10 high-value embedded teaching replacements. They supplement the canonical asset registries; canonical lesson/reference IDs remain authoritative.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-003-001 — Environmental measurement context
**Learner purpose:** understand that temperature and RH are measured observations used together for VPD context, and that target, measurement and alarm are distinct record roles.
**Composition:** photoreal indoor canopy at working height; clearly visible temperature/RH sensor; secondary close view of operator recording location/time/unit; compact branded overlay connecting temperature + RH -> VPD context.
**Evidence:** REF-VPD-002; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Do not show:** one universal VPD target; unlabeled numbers; sensor directly against lamp/duct/door as the exemplar.
**Acceptance:** units legible; measurement location/time context visible; no universal target claim.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-003-002 — Repeatable PPFD grid
**Learner purpose:** show why canopy PPFD should be measured on a repeatable plane and spatial grid rather than inferred from fixture setting or one bright point.
**Composition:** photoreal canopy + quantum sensor; nine-point raster grid overlay; same measurement plane; example distribution visualization without prescribing a universal PPFD target.
**Evidence:** REF-LIGHT-001; REF-CANNABIS-LIGHT-2021-646020; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Do not show:** lux meter as PPFD meter; universal PPFD target; fixture wattage as equivalent to canopy PPFD.
**Acceptance:** sensor orientation plausible; grid is readable; PPFD units use µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹ when a unit example is shown.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-003-003 — Sensor representativeness triage
**Learner purpose:** distinguish a valid local reading from a representative crop-zone reading and teach verification before repair/control changes.
**Composition:** split photoreal grow-room view with representative canopy-zone placement versus obvious local bias near supply air/heat source; verification workflow overlay.
**Evidence:** REF-VPD-002 plus the canonical environmental-monitoring source recorded by the lesson.
**Do not show:** operator opening/repairing energized equipment; arbitrary ideal sensor height; diagnosis from one outlier.
**Acceptance:** local-bias examples are visually obvious; workflow ends in document/escalate when unresolved.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-003-004 — Alarm/trend/handoff
**Learner purpose:** distinguish current value, average/trend and alarm event; preserve evidence and handoff status.
**Composition:** raster dashboard-style teaching board with a short time-series, current reading marker, alarm threshold supplied by a hypothetical SOP, verification step and handoff card.
**Evidence:** REF-VPD-002; REF-LIGHT-001; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Do not show:** threshold as universal horticultural truth; auto-correction outside operator authority.
**Acceptance:** hypothetical/SOP-supplied threshold clearly labeled; chronology reconstructable.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-004-001 — pH/EC measurement context
**Learner purpose:** teach sample identity, meter readiness, method, units and the limit of what pH/EC measurements prove.
**Composition:** photoreal clean measurement workstation with labeled sample container, pH meter, EC meter, calibration standards, rinse/storage context; small sequence overlay: identify -> verify meter -> measure -> record -> interpret in context.
**Evidence:** REF-UMASS-PH-EC-MONITORING-001; REF-IRRIGATION-001; REF-WATER-ALKALINITY-EXT-001; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Do not show:** EC as a nutrient-specific diagnosis; unlabeled calibration liquids; universal cannabis pH/EC target.
**Acceptance:** pH and EC are visibly different measurements; units/method context present; diagnostic limitation stated.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-004-002 — Root-zone dryback trend
**Learner purpose:** teach repeated root-zone moisture/drainage behavior as a trend requiring comparable context.
**Composition:** photoreal container/root-zone context beside a branded time-series showing irrigation events, drainage and changing moisture state; contextual callouts for media/environment/crop stage.
**Evidence:** REF-IRRIGATION-001; REF-NUTRITION-001.
**Do not show:** one elapsed dryback interval as universally correct; moisture appearance alone as proof of root health.
**Acceptance:** repeated measurements visible; irrigation events and measurement timing distinguishable.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-004-003 — Nutrition-context differential
**Learner purpose:** prevent appearance-only nutrient diagnosis.
**Composition:** multiple photoreal leaf/canopy examples with similar-looking stress patterns; evidence checklist beside them: stage, pattern/location, pH/EC method, root zone, irrigation, environment, recent changes.
**Evidence:** REF-NUTRITION-001; REF-CANNABIS-PHOSPHORUS-2021-657323; REF-IRRIGATION-001.
**Do not show:** “yellow leaf = nitrogen deficiency” or other one-image certainty; unsupported treatment recommendation.
**Acceptance:** title/caption explicitly states similar symptoms can have different causes; no single-cause verdict.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-001 — Propagation identity/traceability
**Learner purpose:** connect donor/source identity, cutting/propagule identity, sanitation and downstream traceability.
**Composition:** photoreal propagation workstation; labeled donor/source record, sanitized tools, individual tray/plant identifiers and traceability arrows.
**Evidence:** REF-PROP-001; REF-CANNABIS-PROPAGATION-2020-958; REF-IPM-001; REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY.
**Do not show:** unlabeled mixed cuttings; dirty tools as normal practice; one rooting protocol as universal.
**Acceptance:** identity persists visually from source to propagule; sanitation is part of workflow.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-005-003 — IPM scouting routes
**Learner purpose:** show systematic scouting coverage and documentation rather than casual spot-checking.
**Composition:** photoreal cultivation room background with clean raster room-map inset, numbered scouting locations and route; evidence-record callout for signs/symptoms/location/severity/context.
**Evidence:** REF-IPM-001; REF-IPM-GREENHOUSE-2024-001.
**Do not show:** pesticide application as default response; symptom photo as definitive diagnosis.
**Acceptance:** route covers representative zones; observation and diagnosis are visually separated.

## VIS-LH-TECH1-006-001 — Harvest readiness/stop-work
**Learner purpose:** teach authorization, clean-work readiness, identity, destination and stop/escalate boundaries before harvest proceeds.
**Composition:** photoreal harvest staging area; clean tools/PPE/work area; batch/plant identity; destination container/record; decision overlay with READY / STOP & ESCALATE branches.
**Evidence:** REF-NIOSH-CANNABIS-HAZARDS-2024; REF-OSHA-1910-22-WALKING; canonical Course 6 traceability references.
**Do not show:** unsafe climbing/cutting posture; unlabeled material; repair of unsafe equipment; potency/readiness claims from appearance alone.
**Acceptance:** safety and identity checks precede action; stop-work path is prominent and non-punitive.
