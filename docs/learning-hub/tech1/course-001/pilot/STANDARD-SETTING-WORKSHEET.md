## Minimally qualified learner definition

Before rating items, the panel must agree on a concrete description of the learner who has just enough Course 1 knowledge and judgment to pass while still requiring supervision consistent with an entry-level Technician I role.

The minimally qualified learner should be able to:

- recognize and follow core safety/stop-work boundaries;
- preserve sanitation and biosecurity controls;
- execute controlled instructions without inventing or bypassing procedure;
- preserve identity, traceability and data integrity;
- recognize equipment/operator-care limits;
- document discrepancies accurately and escalate when authority is exceeded.

## Training check

Before Round 1, confirm that panelists understand:

- the minimally qualified learner definition;
- the Course 1 objectives and assessment blueprint;
- the meaning of the probability judgment being requested;
- that the task is to judge minimally qualified performance, not average or expert performance;
- that observed pilot difficulty may inform discussion but does not replace judgment;
- that panelists should identify flawed items rather than compensate for them with unusual ratings.
