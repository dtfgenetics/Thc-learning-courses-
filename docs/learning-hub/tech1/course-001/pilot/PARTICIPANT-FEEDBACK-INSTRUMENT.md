# Course 1 Pilot Participant Feedback Instrument

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-001`  
**Use:** controlled pilot only

Do not collect names, email addresses, employee IDs, medical information, or disability diagnoses in this instrument.

## Response scale

For items 1–12 use:

1 — Strongly disagree  
2 — Disagree  
3 — Neither agree nor disagree  
4 — Agree  
5 — Strongly agree  
N/A — Not applicable / not experienced

## Instructional experience

1. The course purpose and expectations were clear.
2. The lesson sequence made sense.
3. The explanations were understandable without oversimplifying important ideas.
4. Examples and scenarios felt relevant to routine cultivation work.
5. The job aids and workbook activities helped me apply the material.
6. The amount of content felt manageable for the stated course duration.

## Assessment experience

7. Practice questions matched what the lessons taught.
8. The final course-test questions were clear about what they were asking.
9. I could distinguish the best answer from distractors based on the course material and scenario.
10. The practical instructions were clear enough to understand the required task.
11. The practical scoring expectations were understandable from the candidate materials.
12. I had enough time to demonstrate what I knew and could do.

## Accessibility and usability

13. Did you encounter any barrier that made content, navigation, questions, forms, graphics, or practical materials difficult to perceive, understand, operate, or complete?
   - No
   - Yes
   - Prefer not to answer

14. If yes, describe the barrier without including medical or diagnostic information.

15. Did any accommodation or alternate format change what you believed the task was measuring?
   - No
   - Yes
   - Not applicable
   - Prefer not to answer

16. If yes, describe what changed.

## Safety, role boundaries and realism

17. Did any instruction or scenario appear to encourage work outside a Technician I role, bypass an SOP/hold, conceal a record problem, or continue an unsafe condition?
   - No
   - Yes
   - Unsure

18. If yes or unsure, identify the lesson, question, practical step, or wording involved.

## Open response

19. What was the most useful part of Course 1?
20. What was the least clear or least useful part?
21. Identify any question that seemed to have more than one defensible answer.
22. Identify any important routine Technician I task or decision that the course did not prepare you for.
23. Identify any wording, example, image, scenario, or assumption that felt unfair, confusing, culturally narrow, or unnecessarily difficult.
24. What should be changed before this course is released to learners?

## Analysis rule

Do not reduce this instrument to an average satisfaction score. Review open-text responses and safety/accessibility flags directly. A single credible safety, answer-key, accessibility, or role-boundary issue can require investigation even when overall ratings are high.
