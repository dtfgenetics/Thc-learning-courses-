# Course 1 Pilot Decision Rules

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-001`  
**Pilot plan:** `PILOTPLAN-LH-TECH1-001`

## Core rule

Pilot statistics are **diagnostic flags, not automatic disposition rules**. Every release, revision, re-pilot, or retirement decision requires human review of the item/content, evidence, intended construct, and pilot context.

## Evidence sufficiency states

### Insufficient evidence

Use this state when an item has fewer than 30 usable responses in the controlled pilot. Do not interpret a stable-looking percentage or discrimination value as decision-ready.

### Preliminary review evidence

Use this state when an item has at least 30 usable responses but fewer than the internal target of 50. Statistics may inform review but remain especially sensitive to sampling fluctuation and cohort composition.

### Internal target reached

Use this state when an item has at least 50 usable responses. This supports the planned preliminary Course 1 item review; it does not independently establish validity, fairness, reliability, or production readiness.

## Knowledge-item triage flags

The following internal thresholds trigger review. They do not automatically fail an item.

| Signal | Flag | Required action |
|---|---:|---|
| Proportion correct | < 0.20 | Investigate excessive difficulty, ambiguity, missing instruction, keying, or construct mismatch |
| Proportion correct | > 0.95 | Investigate triviality, cueing, answer leakage, or whether the item still contributes useful evidence |
| Point-biserial discrimination | < 0.10 | Urgent review for keying error, ambiguity, multidimensionality, instruction mismatch, or unstable sample |
| Point-biserial discrimination | 0.10–<0.20 | Review before activation |
| Omit rate | > 0.05 | Review wording, usability, timing, accessibility, and navigation |
| Response-time anomaly rate | > 0.10 | Review delivery, answer leakage, navigation, technical issues, and timing definition |
| Any distractor | never/rarely selected | Review whether the distractor is functioning; do not revise solely because of low selection |
| Participant challenge | credible one-best-answer concern | Immediate content/key review regardless of overall statistics |

A negative discrimination value receives immediate review and normally blocks activation until its cause is understood.

## Required qualitative review

For each flagged item, reviewers must consider:

- objective and competency alignment;
- evidence/source scope;
- keyed-answer defensibility;
- distractor plausibility;
- reading demand unrelated to the construct;
- ambiguity or hidden assumptions;
- role realism and SOP/legal boundaries;
- accessibility barriers;
- whether the pilot cohort was appropriate for the item.

## Item disposition values

After review, assign one of:

- **retain** — no change required;
- **retain-monitor** — keep but require additional evidence;
- **revise-repilot** — change requires a new item version and new pilot evidence;
- **retire** — item should not remain in the active bank;
- **insufficient-evidence** — no content decision yet; obtain more usable data.

Never overwrite prior item versions or prior pilot evidence.

## Course-level final assessment review

Do not set or confirm the final passing score from pilot pass rates alone. Before standard setting, confirm:

1. the form represents the intended Course 1 objectives;
2. flagged items have been dispositioned;
3. answer keys and scoring are stable;
4. timing and accessibility findings are acceptable or corrected;
5. candidate performance data are summarized for panel context without turning observed pass rates into the cut score.

## Practical decision rules

The practical cannot advance to operational use when any of the following remain unresolved:

- disagreement between assessors about whether an explicit critical error occurred;
- repeated rubric-domain differences greater than 2 points caused by ambiguous scoring language;
- inconsistent prompt practices that change candidate opportunity;
- evidence that Form A and Form B demand meaningfully different constructs or authority levels;
- accessibility/accommodation procedures that change the construct;
- a safety, traceability, data-integrity, or equipment-authority defect in the candidate materials.

Initial controlled-pilot readiness requires the calibration record specified in `PRACTICAL-CALIBRATION-RECORD-TEMPLATE.md`.

## Accessibility, fairness and legal/compliance overrides

A credible safety, legal/compliance, privacy, accessibility, or fairness issue can block release even when psychometric indicators look acceptable.

Do not average these concerns away with satisfaction scores or item statistics.

## Release disposition

Course 1 remains non-production until all of the following are documented:

- human review approvals for the release version;
- pilot evidence reviewed and dispositioned;
- practical calibration completed and accepted;
- accessibility findings resolved/accepted;
- legal/compliance findings resolved/accepted;
- standard-setting recommendation documented and approved;
- release version and approval record created.
