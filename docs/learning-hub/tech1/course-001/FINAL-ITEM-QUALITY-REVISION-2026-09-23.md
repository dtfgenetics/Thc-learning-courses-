# Technician I Course 1 Final — Item Quality Revision

**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH1-001-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 36 summative items

## What changed

All 36 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality while preserving the controlled blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- safety, hazard-control and PPE reasoning;
- biosecurity, sanitation, quarantine and contamination pathways;
- controlled-instruction and operator/servicing boundaries;
- traceability, movement, waste, reconciliation and genealogy;
- equipment-care escalation and maintenance handoff quality;
- truthful missing-data handling, record correction and contemporaneous documentation;
- replacing weak distractors with plausible operational mistakes;
- expanding rationales so they explain why the keyed response is strongest and why competing choices are weaker.

## What did not change

The revision preserves all item IDs, answer keys, objectives, competencies, controlled references, Bloom levels, difficulty labels, stimulus assignments, final item membership, blueprint weighting, test-to-teaching mappings, course-derived provenance, and the prohibitions on Encyclopedia substitution and untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check distractor plausibility, safety and authority-boundary accuracy, traceability/data-integrity accuracy, stimulus usefulness, rationale proportionality to the controlled references, occupational authenticity, and accessible wording.

## Validation boundary

This revision improves academic assessment quality but does not establish psychometric validity, reliability, fairness, standard setting, secure credential-form suitability, practical validity, or professional credential authorization.
