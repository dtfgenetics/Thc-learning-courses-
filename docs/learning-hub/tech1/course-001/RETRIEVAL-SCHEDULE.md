# Retrieval and Practice Schedule — COURSE-LH-TECH1-001

## Design rule

Use retrieval before rereading. Questions should require the learner to reconstruct the decision rule, not merely recognize a phrase from the lesson.

Retrieval should increasingly mix domains so the learner must decide **which rule applies**, not only recall the rule after being told the topic.

## Within each module

- Start: 3 prerequisite/retrieval prompts from earlier material.
- After each lesson: 3 short retrieval questions plus one decision scenario.
- During guided practice: require at least one learner-generated artifact or verbal decision explanation.
- End of module: **14-item formative module test** with immediate rationale-based feedback.
- Remediation: route the miss to the controlled objective using `instructor/OBJECTIVE-REMEDIATION-MATRIX.md`, require active targeted practice, then reassess with an equivalent scenario rather than memorizing the original answer.

The 14-item current module-test size is a present inventory description, not a maximum. Additional valid formative items or alternate practice may be added as coverage improves.

## Objective-linked remediation cycle

When remediation is needed:

1. identify the mapped objective;
2. ask the learner to explain the original decision before reteaching;
3. diagnose the misconception or performance gap;
4. assign the smallest useful lesson/workbook/exemplar practice;
5. require the learner to produce new evidence;
6. reassess in a changed context;
7. record the result using `instructor/REMEDIATION-RECORD-TEMPLATE.md` when a controlled remediation record is appropriate.

Do not count passive rereading or seeing the correct answer as evidence that the objective has been remediated.

## Spacing across the course

- Before Module 2: retrieve hazard recognition, changed-condition reassessment, PPE/source hierarchy, and stop/escalate boundaries.
- Before Module 3: retrieve contamination pathways, sanitation/recontamination logic, and quarantine/hold/restricted-entry distinctions.
- Before Module 4: retrieve controlled-instruction status, acceptance criteria, alternate escalation, and truthful incomplete-work rules.
- Before Module 5: retrieve traceability genealogy, physical-versus-record mismatch, disposition lineage, and evidence-preservation rules.
- Before Module 6: retrieve equipment-readiness boundaries, recurring-fault reporting, identity, biosecurity, and controlled corrections.
- Before the final course test: mixed retrieval across all six modules; do not provide the final item bank as a study sheet.
- Before the integrated practical: use a short mixed dependency case in which at least two controls independently affect one planned task.

## Delayed retrieval targets

Where the platform supports it, repeat mixed low-stakes retrieval approximately 1 day, 1 week, and 3–4 weeks after first successful performance. Use new contexts so success depends on the underlying rule rather than surface memorization.

Suggested delayed-retrieval formats include:

- one-minute decision explanation;
- critique of a weak workplace record;
- identify-the-boundary exercise;
- short source → route → receiver pathway reconstruction;
- physical-versus-record discrepancy triage;
- observation-versus-hypothesis sort;
- handoff read-back correction;
- mini dependency map showing which condition blocks which task.

## Cumulative retrieval principle

Later retrieval should intentionally reuse earlier competencies in new contexts. For example, an equipment scenario can also require safety authority and handoff quality; a movement scenario can also require biosecurity, controlled-document, and traceability reasoning. This prepares learners for the integrated practical, where competencies are not presented as separate chapters.
