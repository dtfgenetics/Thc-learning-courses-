# Retrieval and Practice Schedule — COURSE-LH-TECH1-001

## Design rule

Use retrieval before rereading. Questions should require the learner to reconstruct the decision rule, not merely recognize a phrase from the lesson.

## Within each module

- Start: 3 prerequisite/retrieval prompts from earlier material.
- After each lesson: 3 short retrieval questions plus one decision scenario.
- End of module: 12-item formative module test with immediate rationale-based feedback.
- Remediation: return to the mapped objective and retry an equivalent scenario rather than memorizing the original answer.

## Spacing across the course

- Before Module 2: retrieve hazard recognition, PPE/source hierarchy, and stop/escalate boundaries.
- Before Module 3: retrieve contamination pathways and quarantine/restricted-entry distinctions.
- Before Module 4: retrieve controlled-instruction and authorization rules.
- Before Module 5: retrieve traceability mismatch and record-preservation rules.
- Before Module 6: retrieve equipment boundary, fault-reporting, identity, and biosecurity decisions.
- Before final course test: mixed retrieval across all six modules; do not provide the final item bank as a study sheet.

## Delayed retrieval targets

Where the platform supports it, repeat mixed low-stakes retrieval approximately 1 day, 1 week, and 3–4 weeks after first successful performance. Use new contexts so success depends on the underlying rule rather than surface memorization.
