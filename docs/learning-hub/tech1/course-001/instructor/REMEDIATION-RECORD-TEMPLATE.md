## Record-quality rule

Record what the learner actually demonstrated. Do not mark remediation successful merely because the learner reviewed the lesson, saw the correct answer, or repeated the instructor's wording. Successful remediation requires evidence that the learner can apply the objective in a different but equivalent context.
