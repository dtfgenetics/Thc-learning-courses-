# Course 1 Human Review Packet

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-001 — Safety, Responsible Practice & Cultivation Workflows`

## Review objective

Determine whether Course 1 is ready to enter controlled learner pilot use. Reviewers must not approve content outside their competence; technical, assessment, accessibility, and operational reviews may require different reviewers.

## Package under review

### Curriculum and learner instruction

- canonical course, module, lesson, objective, competency, and reference objects;
- 6 expanded learner modules;
- student workbook and workbook templates;
- 6 job aids;
- retrieval schedule;
- evidence dossier and source-verification record.

### Assessment

- 6 formative module assessments / **84 current items**;
- final course assessment / 36 items;
- integrated practical;
- practical candidate Forms A and B;
- assessor guide and calibration protocol.

The formative bank is intentionally extensible. Review the live item inventory rather than treating 84 as a maximum or permanent target.

### Delivery/accessibility

- Academy learner web rendering;
- objective-aligned shuffled practice presentation;
- visual/accessibility plan;
- deterministic accessibility regression checks.

## Technical/content review checklist

Confirm that:

- each consequential statement is within the cited source's actual scope;
- transferred evidence is labeled as transferred rather than cannabis-specific law;
- role boundaries are consistent across lessons, questions, practicals, and job aids;
- examples do not imply authorization for pesticide application, electrical/HVAC service, protected servicing, or medical diagnosis;
- traceability examples remain vendor/jurisdiction neutral unless local training is intentionally added;
- no question depends on an unstated facility rule;
- no learner is required to invent a missing fact to get the keyed answer.

## Assessment review checklist

For every active candidate item verify:

- one defensible best answer;
- distractors are plausible but clearly less correct;
- stem tests the mapped objective rather than trivia;
- rationale explains the decision rule;
- source mapping supports the keyed reasoning;
- no answer-position cue, grammatical cue, absolute-language cue, or repeated wording gives away the key;
- reading complexity is no higher than necessary for the construct;
- item does not accidentally test local law/software not taught in the course.

## Accessibility review checklist

Review the rendered experience for:

- full keyboard operation and visible focus;
- screen-reader reading order and meaningful group/legend labels;
- 200% and 400% zoom/reflow where applicable;
- contrast of text, controls, and non-text indicators;
- no color-only meaning;
- accessible equivalents for maps, labels, diagrams, alarms, forms, and practical materials;
- reasonable assessment accommodations that do not change the construct.

## Review outputs

Each reviewer should create a controlled review record containing:

- reviewer role/qualification;
- object/package version;
- review type;
- findings by severity;
- required corrections;
- approval / approval-with-corrections / not-approved decision;
- date.

Course 1 must not be marked production-ready until all required review types and corrections are complete.
