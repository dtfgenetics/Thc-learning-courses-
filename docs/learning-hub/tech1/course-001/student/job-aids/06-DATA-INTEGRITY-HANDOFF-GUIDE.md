# Job Aid 6 — Data Integrity and Shift Handoff

## Reconstructable record

A useful record should allow another qualified person to understand **what happened, to what/where, when, by whom, and with what result**.

Use the transferable ALCOA+ concepts as a quality check: attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original/true copy, accurate, complete, consistent, enduring, and available.

## Correcting an error

Use the approved correction mechanism. Preserve original history and attribution. Do not backdate, erase inconvenient results, overwrite outside the audit trail, invent a missing value, or ask another person to sign for work they did not perform.

## Handoff structure

1. completed work;
2. room/material/asset identity;
3. important measurements/observations;
4. deviations or discrepancies;
5. unresolved hazards/equipment conditions;
6. holds, restrictions, or quarantine status;
7. unfinished work and next priorities;
8. owner/escalation status;
9. incoming-shift questions and cross-check of critical items.

Prioritize unresolved safety and identity/control risks before routine production tasks.
