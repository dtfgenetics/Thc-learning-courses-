# Job Aid 1 — Hazard Response and PPE/HazCom Guide

## Pre-task decision

| Question | If yes | If no / unknown |
|---|---|---|
| Is the task and work area clearly identified? | Continue review | Stop and clarify identity/location |
| Are required controls/PPE available and usable? | Continue | Do not begin; obtain/resolve requirements |
| Is the area under an alarm, hold, restricted-entry, or other access control? | Follow the controlling procedure | Continue normal pre-task review |
| Is the condition within routine Technician I authority? | Use the approved routine control | Stop/isolate where authorized and escalate |
| Does the task require servicing, energized work, pesticide-handler authority, respirator clearance, or other special authorization? | Only proceed if separately qualified/authorized | Continue within normal task scope |

## Chemical information lookup

Before using a workplace chemical, confirm the current product identity and locate the applicable label/SDS/site instruction. Look for hazard statements, precautionary statements, first-aid information, handling/storage guidance, exposure controls/PPE, spill/emergency information, and any application/re-entry restriction relevant to the task.

## Stop-and-escalate examples

- damaged electrical equipment near water;
- missing machine guard;
- unknown life-safety alarm;
- unlabeled/unknown chemical container;
- required PPE unavailable;
- restricted-entry control still active;
- task requires repair or servicing beyond assigned operator care.

## Documentation prompt

Record: **what you observed → what routine control you used → what remained unresolved → who was notified → task status**.
