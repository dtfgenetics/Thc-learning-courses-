# Job Aid 4 — Traceability, Movement and Reconciliation

## Minimum event logic

For a routine movement or transformation, capture enough information to reconstruct:

**what** moved/changed → **source** → **destination/new state** → **when** → **identity** → **quantity/count where relevant** → **who/system recorded it**.

## Identity-risk transitions

Pay extra attention when material is moved, divided, combined, transplanted, relabeled, sampled, harvested, moved between rooms, converted to waste, or transferred from paper to digital systems.

## Reconciliation workflow

1. Compare the physical state with the controlled record.
2. Recount/recheck only within the authorized procedure.
3. Preserve the original discrepancy.
4. Identify confirmed explanations separately from hypotheses.
5. Make corrections only through the controlled correction process.
6. Escalate anything still unresolved.
7. Do not destroy, relabel, combine, or move material in a way that erases the evidence chain before required resolution.

## Downtime rule

If the primary digital platform is unavailable, use the approved downtime method so identity, event time, source/destination, and quantity are preserved contemporaneously. Reconcile when the system returns.
