# Job Aid 3 — Controlled Work Instruction Checklist

Before beginning an assigned task, verify:

- task/work-order identity;
- room/material/asset identity;
- current effective SOP or controlled instruction;
- revision/effective status where shown;
- prerequisites and entry conditions;
- required PPE/controls;
- required sequence;
- measurements or acceptance criteria;
- records/forms to complete;
- explicit exceptions or stop conditions;
- escalation/deviation path.

## If documents conflict

Do **not** choose the easier instruction, average the requirements, or silently improvise. Stop at the conflict and use the authorized clarification/deviation/change process.

## Authority boundary

Technician I may execute defined work, verify assigned conditions, document results, and escalate. Course completion does not grant authority to redesign SOPs, approve permanent deviations, perform hazardous servicing, or override regulatory/product-label controls.

## End-of-task closeout

Confirm the physical result, required record, any deviation/open condition, identity/location, and whether the next task/shift needs a handoff.
