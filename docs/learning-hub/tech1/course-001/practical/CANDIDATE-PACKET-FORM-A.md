# Course 1 Practical — Candidate Packet Form A

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-001`  
**Practical:** `PRACTICAL-LH-TECH1-001-WORKFLOW`  
**Form:** A — routine cultivation shift with controlled discrepancies  
**Use:** pilot/calibration only until approved

## Candidate instructions

Treat every supplied document as part of a simulated controlled workplace. Work only within the authority stated in the packet. Do not invent missing facts. When a condition cannot be resolved from the supplied instructions, document what is known, preserve the evidence, and escalate.

Your score is based on observable decisions and records, not on speed alone.

## Scenario

You are beginning a routine cultivation shift in **Room V-2**. Your assigned work order requires a plant-count verification, a permitted material movement, a sanitation step, a routine irrigation-equipment readiness check, and a handoff to the incoming shift.

Before work begins, you receive the following information.

### Work-area conditions

- A hose is crossing the main aisle near the room entrance.
- A powered cart charger has visible cord damage and moisture is present on the floor nearby.
- A posted room-status card shows one bench on **plant-health hold**.
- A separate pesticide re-entry notice for an adjacent room remains active; that adjacent room is not part of your work order.

### Supplied PPE/HazCom information

The work order requires gloves and eye protection for the assigned sanitation task. A mock SDS/label packet is supplied. The packet directs the worker to use the facility sanitation SOP for dilution, contact time, handling, and disposal. No pesticide-handling task is assigned.

### Controlled instruction extract

1. Verify current work-order ID, room, and revision before beginning.
2. Correct or isolate routine trip hazards where authorized.
3. Do not use damaged electrical equipment. Report and tag out of routine use according to the supplied operator procedure; do not open or repair electrical equipment.
4. Complete sanitation using the current facility sanitation SOP.
5. Do not move material from a bench under hold until the hold is released by an authorized role.
6. Verify plant/material identity before any movement.
7. Record approved movements contemporaneously.
8. If physical identity/count and the record disagree, stop the affected movement and escalate the discrepancy without forcing reconciliation.
9. Operator-level irrigation checks are limited to visual condition, approved leak/clog checks, indicator status, and externally accessible controls listed in the operator checklist. No disassembly or electrical service is permitted.
10. Document unresolved conditions in the shift handoff.

## Plant/material record set

| Physical item | Physical tag | Recorded tag | Recorded location | Proposed action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plant 01 | V2-101 | V2-101 | Bench A | remain |

| Plant 03 | V2-103 | V2-130 | Bench A | move to Bench B |
| Plant 04 | V2-104 | V2-104 | Bench C — HOLD | remain |

The candidate must identify that Plant 03 has a physical-versus-record identity mismatch and that Plant 04 is under hold.

## Equipment condition

The room irrigation controller shows a low-flow alarm on Zone 2. The supplied operator checklist allows the candidate to inspect for visible leaks, a visibly kinked line, a closed external isolation valve, or an obvious emitter obstruction. It does **not** authorize opening the controller, pump, electrical panel, or protected service components.

Seeded observation: one accessible branch line is visibly kinked. After the line is restored to its normal routed position, the alarm remains active.

Expected boundary: document the permitted check and escalate the unresolved alarm; do not disassemble equipment.

## Record-correction task

A prior log states: `Room V-2 sanitation completed 09:00` but the supplied timestamp evidence shows completion at **09:20**. Correct the record using the supplied correction method while preserving the original history.

## Prior-shift handoff

> Bench A count completed. Plant 03 movement still pending. Zone 2 low-flow alarm appeared near end of shift. No maintenance ticket number recorded. Adjacent-room re-entry notice remains posted.

The candidate should recognize that Plant 03 and the irrigation alarm require follow-up and that the adjacent-room restriction should not be generalized to Room V-2 without evidence.
