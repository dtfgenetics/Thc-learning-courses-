## Candidate instructions

Follow the supplied work order, facility procedures, labels, and role boundaries. Separate observation from inference. If a discrepancy cannot be resolved from the evidence provided, preserve identity/status and escalate rather than inventing a correction.

## Scenario

You are assigned a routine shift in **Room F-1** involving sanitation verification, a batch-container movement, an operator-level irrigation check, a record correction, and shift handoff.

### Work-area conditions

- A rolling cart is blocking part of an emergency-access aisle.
- One portable fan has a missing guard and is unplugged at the time of inspection.
- A rack is marked **QUARANTINE — movement prohibited pending release**.
- A chemical container at the sanitation station has a readable product identity but the site use-instruction sheet is missing from the station.

### Controlled instruction extract

1. Maintain required access/egress routes.
2. Do not operate equipment with a missing required guard; remove from routine use and report according to the operator procedure.
3. Do not improvise chemical use when the controlling label/SDS/site instruction needed for the assigned task is unavailable.
4. Respect quarantine/hold controls until an authorized release is documented.
5. Verify all traceable container identities before movement.
6. Record source, destination, date/time, quantity/count, and related identifiers for authorized movements.
7. Do not overwrite discrepant records. Preserve the original entry and follow the correction/escalation process.
8. Operator-level equipment checks may use only the externally accessible checks listed in the supplied checklist.
9. Include unfinished or escalated conditions in the shift handoff.
