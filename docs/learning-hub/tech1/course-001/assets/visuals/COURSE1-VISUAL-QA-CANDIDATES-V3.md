## QA direction locked by this candidate set

1. **Clean-to-dirty flow** must remain facility-map/SOP specific. Generic lower-risk-to-higher-risk sequencing can be shown as an example, not a universal rule.
2. **Sanitation** must distinguish preparation, debris removal, cleaning, approved product verification, label/SOP-defined contact conditions, finish steps as applicable, inspection, protection from recontamination and documentation.
3. **Reconciliation** must compare physical evidence to records, preserve discrepancies, investigate and document confirmed facts, and correct records only through the authorized process. Forced balancing is prohibited.
4. **PPE** must be tied to hazard assessment, label/SDS, site procedure and authorization rather than a universal task/PPE matrix.
5. **Hierarchy of controls** examples must preserve Technician I authority boundaries; workers do not independently substitute chemicals, redesign engineering controls or perform unassigned technical changes.
6. **SDS** examples must be clearly training examples and must not represent fictional product data, QR codes, emergency contacts or chemical concentrations as real.
7. **Authority/escalation** roles must be framed as examples controlled by the facility's actual chain of command.
8. **Genealogy and movement records** must be presented as traceability models rather than one universal form or jurisdiction-specific compliance rule.
