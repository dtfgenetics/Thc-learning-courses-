## Required learner text alternatives

### 13 — Equipment pre-use check
Visual summary of a technician pre-use readiness check that emphasizes visible condition, guards and safety devices, cables/hoses/connections, cleanliness, unusual noise or loose parts, and the rule to stop and report unsafe equipment rather than perform unauthorized repair. Exact checks remain controlled by manufacturer instructions, facility SOP and worker authorization.

### 14 — Operator versus maintenance responsibilities
Comparison showing routine operator responsibilities on one side and maintenance/servicing responsibilities on the other. The purpose is to reinforce that technicians perform only assigned, trained and authorized checks or routine care and escalate repair, internal servicing, hazardous-energy work, calibration or technical maintenance to qualified personnel.

### 15 — Fault report
Example fault-report workflow separating observed facts, equipment/location, immediate permitted actions and escalation from unsupported root-cause diagnosis. The technician records what was seen or heard, protects safety, follows the approved reporting channel and does not perform unauthorized repair.

### 16 — Record correction and data integrity
Example showing that a record correction must preserve the original information or audit trail, identify the corrected information and follow the approved paper or digital correction process. The illustration is not a universal correction method; the controlling record system, SOP and applicable requirements determine how corrections are made.

### 17 — Shift handoff
Model shift-handoff checklist showing current crop/material status, open tasks, equipment condition, environmental conditions, safety concerns, inventory or traceability updates, unresolved issues and ownership for follow-up. Exact fields and sign-off requirements are facility-specific.

### 18 — Integrated technician workflow
Circular workflow illustrating how a technician connects planning/context, preparation, authorized execution, monitoring, truthful documentation, communication and continuous improvement. It reinforces that safety, SOPs, biosecurity, traceability, equipment boundaries and accurate records operate as one system.

## Release rule

These PNGs are production masters, not automatic learner-facing replacements. Before public deployment each candidate must:

1. match the canonical lesson and objective;
2. preserve Technician I role and escalation boundaries;
3. avoid treating facility-specific examples as universal rules;
4. use the learner text alternative stored outside the image;
5. pass mobile/readability review;
6. be registered in `visuals/ASSET-REGISTRY.json` with linked lesson, objective, references and public path;
7. be placed under `apps/web/public/assets/course1/` only after the replacement file exists in the repository;
8. update the canonical lesson JSON only after the public path has been verified;
9. retain the current public SVG until the new asset renders correctly.
