## Locked instructional boundaries

- Equipment visuals must preserve the boundary between ordinary pre-use/operator checks and trained/authorized servicing, repair, calibration or internal troubleshooting.
- Fault-report visuals must distinguish observed facts from suspected causes.
- Record-correction visuals must preserve original evidence/auditability and defer correction mechanics to the approved record system and SOP.
- Shift-handoff visuals must carry open conditions, ownership and unresolved risk, not just routine status.
- Integrated-workflow visuals must synthesize safety, controlled instructions, biosecurity, traceability, equipment boundaries, escalation and truthful records without expanding Technician I authority.

## Release policy

The existing learner-facing SVGs remain the public baseline until a replacement candidate passes the Course 1 visual QA gate. No generated image is approved solely because it looks finished.

A replacement may be promoted only after:

1. factual/copy review against the canonical lesson and cited sources;
2. removal or qualification of facility-specific or jurisdiction-specific claims;
3. Technician I role-boundary review;
4. meaningful caption and learner text alternative;
5. mobile/responsive readability review;
6. registration in `visuals/ASSET-REGISTRY.json`;
7. canonical lesson JSON mapping;
8. public path/render verification under `apps/web/public/assets/course1/`.
