## Rebuilt concepts

### VIS-LH-TECH1-001-06 — Clean-to-Dirty Flow

Canonical alignment target: `LESSON-LH-TECH1-001-04 — Contamination Routes and Zone Discipline`.

The corrected design must preserve these rules:

- movement direction is controlled by the facility's actual zone map, current risk status and SOP;
- cleaner-to-higher-risk sequencing may be used where the site defines it, but it is not a universal law;
- unnecessary backward travel should be reduced without claiming that all backtracking is categorically prohibited;
- people, PPE, tools, carts, hoses, plant material, containers and waste can all form contamination routes;
- sanitized status can be lost through recontamination;
- boundary breaches are documented and escalated under the controlling procedure.

### VIS-LH-TECH1-001-07 — Sanitation Sequence

Canonical alignment target: `LESSON-LH-TECH1-001-05 — Cleaning, Disinfection and Sanitation Verification`.

The corrected design must preserve these decision points:

1. control/prepare the work area;
2. remove gross debris and organic matter;
3. physically clean;
4. verify the exact approved product and current instructions;
5. verify preparation, worker precautions and compatibility;
6. apply and maintain label/SOP-defined contact conditions;
7. complete any required rinse, dry or other finish step as applicable;
8. inspect the result;
9. protect the item/area from recontamination;
10. document completion, exceptions and corrective action truthfully.

No generated graphic may imply that every product requires the same rinse, dry, concentration or contact-time instruction.

### VIS-LH-TECH1-001-12 — Reconciliation

Canonical alignment target: Course 1 traceability/reconciliation instruction.

The corrected design must preserve these principles:

- compare physical evidence to controlled records;
- preserve the discrepancy instead of forcing numbers to match;
- investigate recent movements, entries, waste/destruction records, locations and other relevant evidence;
- record confirmed facts separately from assumptions;
- correct records only through the authorized process;
- retain the original/audit trail where the record system requires it;
- escalate unresolved or significant discrepancies under facility and jurisdiction controls.
