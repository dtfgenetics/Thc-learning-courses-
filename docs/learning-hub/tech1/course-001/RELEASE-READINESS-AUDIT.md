The current 84-item formative bank may continue to expand or change. Its size is a current inventory, not a release cap.

## Gates that cannot be truthfully closed by code/content generation alone

### 1. Human technical/curriculum review — OPEN

A qualified reviewer must verify factual accuracy, source-role boundaries, job-task realism, and instructional coherence.

### 2. Human assessment-item review — OPEN

All **120 current public Course 1 knowledge-test items** (84 formative + 36 summative) require item-level review before operational learner use. Automated schema/coverage checks do not establish item validity. Future added or revised items enter the same review scope.

### 3. Practical assessor calibration — OPEN

The rubric and critical-error rules now have a calibration protocol and two equivalent candidate forms, but no real multi-assessor calibration evidence yet exists.

### 4. Rendered accessibility review — OPEN

Static regression checks are green, but manual keyboard, screen-reader, zoom/reflow, contrast, and assessment-accessibility review remain required against the rendered delivery.

### 5. Pilot learner evidence — OPEN

Pilot data are still needed for item difficulty/discrimination review, timing, confusing distractors, practical usability, and unintended barriers.

### 6. Standard setting / cut-score decision — OPEN

The current 80% values are development thresholds required by the present data model. They must be confirmed or replaced through documented review/standard setting before operational claims.

### 7. Final approval/release record — OPEN

After technical, assessment, accessibility, calibration, pilot, and standard-setting findings are resolved, the course requires an explicit versioned approval/release record.

## Current professional judgment

Course 1 is now **content-complete and structurally testable as a draft production package**. It is appropriate to enter formal human review and controlled pilot preparation. It is **not yet appropriate to market as a validated or production-ready certification course** until the open gates above are completed.
