# Course 5 Practical C/D/E Assessor Guide

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-005 — Propagation, Canopy, IPM Scouting & Crop Care`  
**Practicals:** C, D and E  
**State:** draft assessor support; validation/calibration evidence open

## Purpose

This guide helps evaluators apply the existing controlled practical definitions consistently. It does not replace the practical source documents, change their scoring weights, create pesticide-applicator authority or mark evaluator calibration complete.

Each practical is scored independently on a 100-point scale with the current **target pass of 80/100**. Course completion or a strong score on one practical does not automatically compensate for failure on another practical unless a future approved credential decision rule explicitly says so.

# Practical C — IPM Scouting Route

## Controlled scoring model

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| sanitation/biosecurity | 15 |
| route/inspection completeness | 15 |
| sign vs symptom classification | 15 |
| incidence/severity | 15 |
| spatial/trend interpretation | 20 |
| escalation choice | 10 |
| documentation/handoff | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Target pass:** 80/100

## Evaluator focus

Observe whether the candidate:

- prepares clean scouting tools/PPE;
- follows the supplied representative route;
- inspects the required plant/canopy/leaf/trap locations;
- distinguishes symptom, direct sign, mechanical damage and insufficient evidence;
- calculates simple incidence from the provided sample;
- uses the supplied severity categories consistently;
- maps findings spatially;
- compares repeated rounds and identifies stable/increasing/resolving trends;
- preserves clean-to-dirty and isolation/quarantine boundaries;
- escalates evidence without making unauthorized pesticide or treatment-selection decisions;
- produces a usable scouting record and handoff.

## Scoring cautions

Do not award diagnostic credit merely because a candidate names a plausible pest or disease. Score the controlled evidence behaviors. A candidate who guesses the correct name while failing route, distribution, biosecurity or documentation requirements has not demonstrated the intended competency.

# Practical D — Propagation Workstation

## Controlled scoring model

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| work-order/donor verification | 15 |
| sanitation | 20 |
| propagation sequence consistency | 20 |
| labeling/traceability | 15 |
| rooting/acclimatization interpretation | 10 |
| count reconciliation | 10 |
| documentation/escalation | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Target pass:** 80/100

## Evaluator focus

Observe whether the candidate:

- verifies donor identity, cultivar, target count and work order;
- prepares the clean workstation and stages labels before handling material;
- inspects donor material against supplied acceptance criteria;
- sequences sanitation correctly;
- follows the assigned propagation sequence without redesigning it;
- preserves unambiguous tray/lot identity;
- interprets supplied rooting/progress data without assuming all cultivars behave identically;
- recognizes batch exceptions without inventing a new protocol;
- preserves clean/quarantine boundaries for suspect donor material;
- reconciles started, rooted, lost and handed-off counts;
- completes the batch record and handoff honestly.

## Scoring cautions

Identity and traceability errors should not be hidden by otherwise clean technique. Do not reward relabeling based on appearance or memory. Uncertain identity is an exception that must be held and resolved through approved evidence.

# Practical E — Canopy Maintenance Work Order

## Controlled scoring model

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| work-order/objective interpretation | 15 |
| pre-task crop inspection | 10 |
| sanitation/workspace control | 15 |
| task execution and tissue preservation | 25 |
| consistency against criteria | 15 |
| exception/escalation handling | 10 |
| records/handoff | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Target pass:** 80/100

## Evaluator focus

Observe whether the candidate:

- verifies room, batch, stage and work-order objective;
- identifies exceptions before irreversible plant work begins;
- stages clean tools/support material;
- selects the least disruptive action that satisfies the supplied objective;
- applies the supplied criteria consistently;
- recognizes tissue-splitting, severe stress, pest concerns or ambiguous instructions as stop/hold/escalation conditions;
- maintains sanitation, waste and quarantine boundaries;
- compares the result to the work-order objective;
- records scope completed, damage, exceptions and follow-up needs;
- provides a concise receiving-shift handoff.

## Scoring cautions

Do not reward extra pruning, defoliation or training merely because the result appears visually uniform. The competency is controlled execution against the supplied work standard while preserving plant integrity.

## Common evaluator rules across C/D/E

1. Score observed candidate evidence, not presumed intent.
2. Do not coach a candidate through a scored decision unless the controlled practical explicitly permits a prompt; record any allowed prompt consistently.
3. Preserve the seeded condition and candidate response in the evidence record.
4. Separate a candidate's observation from diagnosis and from authority to act.
5. Do not treat a correct final outcome as sufficient if the candidate reached it through unsafe, unauthorized or non-traceable actions.
6. Record exceptions, ambiguous source material and any scoring judgment that required interpretation.
7. Do not invent critical-failure rules that are not in the controlled practical/credential blueprint; route proposed changes through formal review.
8. Pesticide recognition or a plausible treatment suggestion does not create applicator authority and should not earn out-of-scope credit.

## Evidence retention for calibration

For calibration or pilot sessions, retain at minimum:

- practical/form version;
- candidate/simulation identifier;
- assessor identifier;
- category scores;
- total score;
- behavioral evidence supporting deductions or borderline decisions;
- prompts given, if any;
- seeded conditions used;
- assessor uncertainty notes;
- second-rater scores when used;
- adjudication result when ratings materially differ.

## Validation boundary

This guide is machine-authored support around existing controlled scoring definitions. It does not establish evaluator qualification, inter-rater reliability, pass-score validity, fairness, equivalence across delivery modes or professional credential release. Those require recorded human evidence.
