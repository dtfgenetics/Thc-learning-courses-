# Course 5 Rendered Accessibility & Learner UX Review

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-005 — Propagation, Canopy, IPM Scouting & Crop Care`  
**Target:** WCAG 2.2 AA plus practical learner usability  
**State:** review packet prepared; rendered approval not completed

## Review scope

Review the actual rendered Course 5 learner surface, not only source JSON or SVG files. Test at representative mobile, tablet and desktop widths and with keyboard-only navigation.

## Required learner surfaces

- course landing/overview;
- all four dedicated lessons;
- eight embedded learner visuals;
- three downloadable practice worksheets;
- formative assessment surface;
- summative academic assessment surface when exposed in the approved environment;
- lesson navigation, progress controls and resource links;
- error/empty/loading states relevant to the course.

## Visual asset checks

For every Course 5 instructional raster asset:

- lesson-level text alternative communicates the instructional meaning, not merely visual appearance;
- caption and surrounding lesson copy provide the scientific context required to interpret the visual;
- text remains readable without relying on color alone;
- labels do not clip at narrow widths;
- diagrams remain understandable when zoomed and preserve legible labels at mobile, tablet and desktop widths;
- print/download worksheets preserve usable contrast and writing space;
- symbols used for quarantine, sanitation, warning or evidence have text equivalents.

## Interaction and navigation checks

- all interactive controls reachable by keyboard;
- visible focus indicator present;
- focus order follows learning order;
- expandable/collapsible content exposes state programmatically;
- links describe destination or action;
- no important instruction depends on hover alone;
- assessment controls are operable without pointer precision;
- validation/error messages identify the affected control and recovery action.

## Responsive checks

Test at minimum:

- narrow phone viewport;
- larger phone/small tablet;
- tablet;
- standard desktop;
- wide desktop.

Verify:

- no horizontal scrolling for ordinary lesson text;
- tables either reflow, scroll within a labeled region or use an equivalent accessible presentation;
- figures do not obscure captions or controls;
- downloadable worksheet links remain visible and usable;
- sticky navigation does not cover headings/content;
- assessment options remain selectable and legible.

## Cognitive/learner UX checks

Confirm that learners can distinguish:

- observation from diagnosis;
- authorized action from escalation;
- complete from partial/held work;
- clean from suspect/quarantine states;
- sign from symptom;
- incidence from severity;
- academic assessment from practical performance evidence.

The interface must not visually imply pesticide-treatment authority or professional credential issuance from Course 5 completion alone.

## Accessibility of practical preparation

Check whether the three practice worksheets and practical-preparation language can be used with reasonable accommodations without changing the underlying competency. Document any element where the competency itself requires a sensory/motor behavior and identify approved equivalent evidence only through the credential accommodation process.
