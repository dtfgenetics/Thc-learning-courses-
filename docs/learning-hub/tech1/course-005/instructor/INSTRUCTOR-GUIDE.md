# Course 5 Instructor Guide

**Course:** Propagation, Canopy, IPM Scouting & Crop Care  
**State:** draft instructor support

## Instructional intent

Course 5 should produce an entry-level technician who can execute controlled crop-care work, collect useful evidence and recognize when to stop or escalate. It should not train learners to substitute personal cultivation preferences for work orders or to assume pesticide/treatment authority.

## Instructional sequence

1. **Propagation identity and sanitation** — labels, donor/source verification, clean work, counts and exception handling.
2. **Canopy work under a controlled standard** — inspect plant condition, execute only the assigned action, protect tissue and sanitation, document damage or holds.
3. **IPM scouting as evidence collection** — representative route, signs vs symptoms, incidence, severity, spatial pattern and trend.
4. **Integrated crop-care handoff** — preserve identity, factual task status, quarantine state, observed evidence, authorized actions, unresolved conditions and next steps.

## Core teaching rule

Require learners to state three things separately:

- **what was observed;**
- **what action was authorized and completed;**
- **what remains unresolved or requires escalation.**

This separation prevents guesses from becoming records.

## Common misconceptions and corrections

### “If I recognize the pest, I can choose the treatment.”

Correction: recognition/scouting competence and applicator authority are different. Product selection, mixing, application, re-entry and other regulated actions remain governed by jurisdiction, product label, facility role authorization and required training/licensing.

### “A symptom means I know the cause.”

Correction: symptoms can overlap across pests, disease, nutrition, environment and mechanical damage. Have learners preserve sign/symptom, location, incidence, severity, pattern and trend before stating a supported interpretation.

### “Good canopy work means every plant looks identical.”

Correction: completion is measured against the supplied work standard and actual plant condition, not visual sameness achieved by excessive tissue removal.

### “I can relabel an uncertain tray if the likely identity is obvious.”

Correction: uncertainty is a traceability break. Hold movement and resolve identity from approved evidence; appearance and memory are not substitutes.

### “If most of the task is complete, I can mark it complete.”

Correction: complete, partial, held and not-started are distinct factual states. Records must reflect actual status.

## Facilitation of learner worksheets

### Propagation workstation worksheet

Look for:

- source/work-order identity verified before movement;
- labels staged and preserved;
- sanitation checkpoint applied;
- counts reconciled without invented identity;
- exceptions and quarantine/hold state documented.

### IPM scouting worksheet

Look for:

- full representative route rather than obvious-damage-only scouting;
- objective sign/symptom descriptions;
- correct incidence calculation where supplied;
- consistent use of the provided severity scale;
- spatial pattern and trend retained;
- no unsupported treatment-selection statement.

### Canopy work-order worksheet

Look for:

- pre-task condition check;
- action tied to the supplied work order;
- tissue/sanitation protection;
- stop/hold conditions recognized;
- before/after result checked against the defined standard;
- damage or incomplete work recorded truthfully.

## Assessment use

The 12-item M01 is formative. Use objective misses diagnostically. Route learners back to the relevant lesson and fresh practice scenario before equivalent reassessment.

The 24-item final is summative academic evidence, not a substitute for Practicals C, D and E. Do not coach to exact final answers or reuse exposed summative items as routine remediation.

## Practical preparation boundary

Prepare learners for the observable behaviors in the practical crosswalk, but do not guarantee a practical pass from classroom completion. Evaluators must use controlled practical instructions and scoring materials.

## Human review points

The instructor package still requires review for:

- technical accuracy;
- role/authority boundaries;
- instructional clarity;
- assessment alignment;
- accessibility and accommodation feasibility;
- practical equivalence across delivery modes.
