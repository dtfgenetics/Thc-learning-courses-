# Course 5 Objective Remediation Matrix

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-005`

Use this matrix after formative or summative evidence identifies an objective-level gap. Reassessment should use equivalent fresh evidence, not the exposed item or identical scenario.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-005-01`

**Corrective coaching**  
Rebuild the learner's identity-control sequence: verify donor/source, work order, lot/tray/plant and destination before movement; stage labels before handling; preserve sanitation checkpoints; reconcile counts without guessing missing identity. Emphasize that an identity conflict creates a hold condition.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give a new propagation packet with two similar donor IDs, one conflicting tray label, one count discrepancy and a sanitation checkpoint. Require the learner to decide what may proceed, what must be held and what evidence resolves the discrepancy.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent new propagation scenario. Require correct identity verification, label/traceability handling, count reconciliation, sanitation decision and factual exception/handoff documentation.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-005-02`

**Corrective coaching**  
Reinforce that canopy work is controlled execution, not personal technique selection. Have the learner identify the assigned structural objective, plant-condition checks, tissue-protection limits, sanitation requirements and stop/hold conditions before naming an action.

**Return-to-practice**  
Provide a new canopy work order containing one stressed plant, one support hazard and a defined tissue-removal standard. Require a plant-by-plant proceed/hold/action decision with reasons tied to the work order.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent canopy task with different plant conditions. Score work-order interpretation, action selection, tissue protection, sanitation, stop conditions and accurate completion/exception record.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-005-03`

**Corrective coaching**  
Separate symptom, sign, incidence, severity, spatial pattern and trend. Require the learner to state only what the evidence supports and to explain why a close-up or single trap cannot represent room-level distribution.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give a fresh room map, representative scouting route, 20-plant observation sample, trap counts and two prior rounds. Require classification, incidence calculation, severity use, mapping and trend interpretation without treatment selection.

**Reassessment**  
Use a different scouting packet. Require complete route logic, accurate sign/symptom classification, incidence/severity, spatial/trend interpretation and bounded escalation.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-005-04`

**Corrective coaching**  
Review clean-to-dirty movement, tool sanitation, suspect-material isolation, quarantine/hold status and breach reporting. Focus on preventing a routine production task from carrying contamination into a clean area.

**Return-to-practice**  
Provide a three-zone sequence in which a suspect finding appears midway through propagation, canopy or scouting work. Require the learner to preserve the boundary, control tools/material, document the hold and report any actual or potential breach.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent new movement scenario. Require correct sanitation/quarantine decisions, containment of suspect tools/material and a reconstructable breach/hold record where applicable.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-005-05`

**Corrective coaching**  
Have the learner sort actions into: routine Technician I work; action requiring clarification/lead approval; specialist work; pesticide/applicator-controlled work. Explicitly separate recognizing a likely problem from authority to select or apply treatment.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give a mixed scenario set containing routine pruning, an identity discrepancy, a suspected pest, a damaged plant and a proposed pesticide response. Require proceed/stop/escalate decisions and the controlling reason for each.

**Reassessment**  
Use a fresh mixed-authority scenario. Require correct role-boundary decisions and escalation without unauthorized protocol redesign, treatment selection, pesticide mixing/application or unsupported release decisions.

## `LO-LH-TECH1-005-06`

**Corrective coaching**  
Rebuild the handoff as a factual evidence chain: identity, task status, observation, sanitation/quarantine action, authorized action completed, exception/uncertainty, escalation and next action. Correct any tendency to convert a likely explanation into a recorded diagnosis.

**Return-to-practice**  
Give a new integrated shift with one held propagation tray, one partially completed canopy zone and one scouting trend. Require station records plus one receiving-shift handoff that preserves all unresolved conditions.

**Reassessment**  
Use an equivalent new integrated shift packet. Require a reconstructable handoff with accurate identity, completion state, evidence, biosecurity status, escalation and next action and no invented diagnosis, treatment, identity or completion.

## Reassessment governance

- Do not reuse the exact exposed scored item as the reassessment.
- Preserve the same objective and comparable cognitive demand.
- Record remediation completed, new evidence used, reassessment result and any remaining gap.
- Repeated failure should route to instructor/assessor review rather than automatic credential progression.
- Remediation completion does not replace practical-performance evidence where Practicals C, D or E are required.
