# Course 5 Evidence Dossier

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-005 — Propagation, Canopy, IPM Scouting & Crop Care`  
**State:** draft evidence package; human technical review open

## Evidence policy

Course 5 uses reviewed scientific sources for durable biological and operational principles, while facility SOPs, product labels, equipment instructions and jurisdiction-specific requirements control when they are more specific. No source in this dossier should be stretched beyond the conditions it actually supports.

## Core reviewed references

### `REF-PROP-001` — propagation biology

**Source type:** peer-reviewed review  
**Title:** *Cannabis sativa: From Therapeutic Uses to Micropropagation and Beyond* (2021)

Use for:

- propagation biology;
- rooting and acclimatization concepts;
- biological variability in propagation response.

Do not use it as the sole authority for every conventional cutting or facility-specific propagation procedure. The canonical reference record explicitly notes that conventional cutting practices require additional direct sources.

### `REF-CANOPY-001` — canopy architecture

**Source type:** peer-reviewed study  
**Title:** *Shape Matters: Plant Architecture Affects Chemical Uniformity in Large-Size Medical Cannabis Plants* (2021)

Use for:

- relationship between plant architecture and light penetration;
- canopy/microclimate context;
- rationale for controlled, observable plant-architecture work.

Do not generalize a study-specific training treatment into a universal pruning, defoliation or training prescription. The reference record identifies treatment outcomes as context-specific.

### `REF-IPM-001` — plant disease/IPM concepts

**Source type:** peer-reviewed review  
**Title:** *Emerging diseases of Cannabis sativa and sustainable management* (2021)

Use for:

- clean planting stock and sanitation principles;
- disease-management context;
- diagnostics and evidence collection;
- environmental disease suppression concepts;
- biosecurity and quarantine rationale.

Do not treat the source as authority for pesticide legality, registration, applicator licensing, re-entry requirements or product selection in a specific jurisdiction. Those remain separately controlled by law, label and facility authorization.

### `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY` — records/traceability model

Use as a general quality-system model for attributable, contemporaneous, accurate, complete and reconstructable records. It is not cannabis law and must not be presented as a jurisdictional cannabis requirement.

## Evidence rules embedded in the course

1. **Observation is not diagnosis.** A symptom is recorded as a symptom unless sufficient evidence supports a more specific conclusion.
2. **Direct signs still need context.** Organism evidence should be tied to location, distribution and identification context.
3. **Incidence, severity, pattern and trend are distinct.** One dramatic close-up does not describe room-level distribution.
4. **Work orders control task scope.** Scientific literature does not override a controlled facility task or safety boundary.
5. **Identity uncertainty causes a hold, not a guess.** Missing or conflicting plant/tray identity remains an exception until resolved from approved records.
6. **Sanitation/quarantine actions are evidence.** Movement controls and breaches belong in the record and handoff.
7. **Pesticide authority is external to this course.** Recognition of a likely pest does not grant product-selection, mixing or application authority.
