# Course 5 Final Human Review Worklist

**Course:** `COURSE-LH-TECH1-005 — Propagation, Canopy, IPM Scouting & Crop Care`  
**State:** review gates open

This worklist records evidence-dependent gates that cannot be closed by source generation or CI alone.

## 1. Subject-matter / technical review

Confirm:

- propagation biology and terminology are accurate;
- conventional propagation procedures do not exceed the evidence cited;
- canopy claims remain context-specific and do not become universal pruning/training prescriptions;
- IPM scouting terminology and evidence rules are technically sound;
- sanitation/quarantine concepts are appropriate to entry-level cultivation operations;
- pesticide/applicator statements are legally and operationally bounded.

**Status:** not approved

## 2. Instructional-design review

Confirm:

- lesson sequence builds from controlled task execution to integrated handoff;
- each objective has adequate instruction and applied practice;
- worksheets are understandable without instructor rescue;
- remediation uses fresh practice rather than answer exposure;
- practical preparation does not collapse into test coaching.

**Status:** not approved

## 3. Assessment review

Review all 36 scored items for:

- objective and competency alignment;
- technical accuracy;
- single-best-answer defensibility;
- distractor plausibility without trick wording;

- cognitive level;
- bias/sensitivity and accessibility;
- security/exposure risk.

Confirm the formative and summative banks remain distinct.

**Status:** not approved

- delivery modes are acceptably equivalent;
