# Course 5 Learner Materials

**Course:** Propagation, Canopy, IPM Scouting & Crop Care  
**State:** draft learner package

## Purpose

Course 5 is built around controlled crop-care execution rather than memorizing isolated cultivation techniques. Learners repeatedly practice identity control, sanitation, evidence capture, stop/hold decisions, task completion, exception handling and handoff across propagation, canopy and scouting work.

## Required applied practice sequence

### Practice 1 — propagation workstation

Learners receive a work order, donor/source identities, labels, tray map and count packet. The scenario includes an identity discrepancy, sanitation checkpoint and count exception. The learner must:

- verify donor/source and work-order identity before material movement;
- stage labels before handling material;
- preserve sanitation and clean-to-dirty boundaries;
- retain tray/lot identity through the workflow;
- reconcile starting, lost, usable and transferred counts;
- hold uncertain identity rather than guessing;
- create a factual propagation handoff.

### Practice 2 — canopy maintenance work order

Learners receive a defined canopy task and crop-condition packet. The scenario includes a weak plant, a pest-like concern, a support/tissue-risk condition and an accidental damage exception. The learner must:

- verify room/batch/work-order scope;
- inspect plant condition before modification;
- apply only the assigned canopy action;
- preserve tissue and sanitation;
- stop/hold when condition or authority requires it;
- compare actual completion with the supplied work standard;
- document damage, holds and incomplete work truthfully.

### Practice 3 — repeatable IPM scouting route

Learners receive a room map, route, observation stations, trap data and repeated-round evidence. The learner must:

- follow the complete representative route;
- inspect the supplied canopy/leaf/trap/high-risk locations;
- distinguish symptom, direct sign, mechanical damage and insufficient evidence;
- calculate simple incidence where data are supplied;
- apply the supplied severity scale consistently;
- map spatial pattern and compare trend across rounds;
- preserve quarantine/biosecurity boundaries;
- escalate without selecting or prescribing a pesticide treatment.

### Practice 4 — integrated crop-care shift packet

The final learner exercise combines all three workstreams. The learner completes the three station records, then builds one shift handoff that clearly separates:

- controlled identity;
- task status: complete, partial, held or not started;
- observed evidence;
- sanitation/quarantine actions;
- authorized actions actually completed;
- deviations and damage;
- uncertainty;
- escalation;
- receiving-shift next action.

The handoff must not invent cultivar identity, pest/disease diagnosis, treatment selection, release status or task completion.

## Formative learning use

The 12-item M01 assessment is formative. Instructors should use objective-level misses to route the learner back to the corresponding station worksheet and lesson, not simply reveal the correct answer and repeat the same item.

## Summative academic assessment

The 24-item final is separate from the formative bank and is derived from the six controlled objectives taught in the four dedicated Course 5 lessons. `TEST-TO-TEACHING-MAP.md` records the exact instructional provenance. Encyclopedia-only, external-reading-only, or unrelated-course material may not be introduced as scored Course 5 content unless first taught directly in this course. The assessment remains draft until assessment review and release approval. Passing the academic final does not substitute for Practicals C, D and E and does not confer pesticide-applicator or treatment-selection authority.

## Learner completion evidence

A strong learner portfolio may include simulated or approved workplace-equivalent copies of:

- propagation workstation/tray map and reconciliation record;
- scouting sheet, annotated room map, incidence/severity calculations and trend note;
- canopy work plan, before/after decision record and exceptions log;
- quarantine/isolation or sanitation-breach note where applicable;
- final integrated shift handoff.

Portfolio artifacts are learning evidence. They do not themselves authorize professional credential issuance.
