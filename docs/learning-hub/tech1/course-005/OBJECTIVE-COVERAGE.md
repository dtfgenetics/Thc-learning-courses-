## Controlled learning-loop requirements

Each objective must retain all of the following before Course 5 can advance beyond machine-package readiness:

1. at least one dedicated lesson mapping;
2. explicit applied practice in the mapped lessons;
3. item-level formative coverage;
4. item-level summative coverage;
5. evidence references on every scored item;
6. objective-specific corrective coaching;
7. fresh return-to-practice activity;
8. equivalent reassessment guidance;
9. mapped practical evidence where performance is required.

## Performance boundary

Course 5 academic assessment and learner exercises do not replace the three controlled performance demonstrations. Practical C covers representative scouting and IPM evidence; Practical D covers propagation identity, sanitation, tray/count control and batch records; Practical E covers controlled canopy work, tissue protection, sanitation, exceptions and handoff.

The current practical crosswalk is a development mapping. Real evaluator calibration, inter-rater evidence, pilot evidence and practical validation remain open.

## Regulatory/authority boundary

Recognition of a pest, disease-like symptom or likely cause does not authorize pesticide selection, mixing, application, re-entry decisions or other regulated pesticide work. Jurisdiction, product label, facility authorization and any required licensing/training remain controlling.
