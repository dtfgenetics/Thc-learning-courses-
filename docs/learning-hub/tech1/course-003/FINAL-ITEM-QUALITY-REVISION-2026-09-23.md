# Technician I Course 3 Final — Item Quality Revision

**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH1-003-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.3.0  
**Scope:** all 20 summative items

## What changed

All 20 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality while preserving the controlled blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- paired temperature/RH and VPD interpretation;
- target-versus-observation separation;
- measurement context, units and data-quality verification;
- repeatable PPFD mapping and spatial-distribution reporting;
- controller setting versus measured canopy light;
- sensor representativeness, verification status and event-linked anomalies;
- operator-care versus protected servicing boundaries;
- alarm/fault escalation;
- reconstructable environmental records and shift handoffs;
- replacing weak distractors and repeated-answer rationales with plausible alternatives and explanatory feedback.

## What did not change

The revision preserves all item IDs, answer keys, objectives, competencies, controlled references, Bloom levels, difficulty labels, final item membership, blueprint weighting, taught-material mappings, test-to-teaching mappings, course-derived provenance, and the prohibitions on Encyclopedia substitution and untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check distractor plausibility, environmental/VPD reasoning, PPFD measurement logic, sensor-data quality, operator-care authority boundaries, rationale proportionality to controlled references, occupational authenticity, and accessible wording.

## Validation boundary

This revision improves academic assessment quality but does not establish psychometric validity, reliability, fairness, standard setting, secure credential-form suitability, practical validity, or professional credential authorization.
