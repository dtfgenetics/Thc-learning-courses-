## Review rule

Machine checks may prove that source objects resolve, assets are delivered, objectives have instruction/practice/assessment/remediation coverage and required files are present. They cannot substitute for a qualified reviewer, evaluator calibration, learner pilot evidence, standard setting or release approval.

Each review decision must record the exact source/release version examined. If a material content, assessment, scoring or visual change occurs after approval, determine whether re-review is required rather than silently carrying approval forward.
