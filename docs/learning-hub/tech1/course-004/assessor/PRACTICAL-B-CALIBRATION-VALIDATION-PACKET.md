## Claims to examine

The validation process should determine whether evidence supports limited claims that a candidate can:

1. verify work order, sample/source and room/zone identity;
2. perform supplied pH/EC measurement readiness and obtain/document measurements;
3. use pH/EC, symptom, stage and root-zone context without unsupported nutrient diagnosis;
4. execute an authorized routine irrigation event;
5. verify representative delivery and recognize local/root-zone deviations;
6. create accurate records and escalate unresolved work outside Technician I authority.

Claims about independent nutrient formulation, advanced crop steering, protected control design or equipment repair require separate competencies and evidence.

## Phase 1 — technical/content review

Qualified reviewers should independently check:
- task authenticity for entry-level Technician I work;
- all six Course 4 objectives are represented by observable evidence;
- seeded conditions do not accidentally reveal the answer;
- pH/EC terminology and meter procedure boundaries are accurate;
- nutrition-context language avoids unsupported diagnosis;
- irrigation and representative-delivery tasks reflect controlled execution rather than advanced strategy design;
- scoring categories sample the intended construct;
- accessibility/accommodation provisions preserve the construct;
- equipment-specific knowledge is supplied when required.

## Phase 3 — response-process / administration review

Observe whether candidates/evaluators interpret directions as intended. Track:
- clarification requests;
- evaluator prompts/interventions;
- meter/scenario/work-order ambiguities;
- equipment or simulation failures;
- whether target values are confused with actual measurements;
- whether a local delivery fault is incorrectly turned into a room-wide decision;
- whether symptom cards encourage unsupported diagnosis;
- whether operator-authority boundaries are understood.

Revise the instrument when recurrent misunderstanding is caused by the task rather than candidate performance.
