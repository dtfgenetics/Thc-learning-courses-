# Evidence Dossier — COURSE-LH-TECH1-004

**Course:** Water, Root Zone, Nutrition & Irrigation Fundamentals  
**Status:** development evidence package; human technical review open

## Evidence policy

Course 4 uses reviewed scientific and quality-system references for durable principles, while facility SOPs, current work orders, labels, equipment manuals and jurisdiction-specific requirements govern specific operational decisions. A source that supports a principle is not automatically authority for a universal operating target.

## `REF-IRRIGATION-001`

**Source:** 2025 peer-reviewed Cannabis sativa study of subsurface drip irrigation in an outdoor tunnel production system.  
**Repository evidence level:** A.  
**Supports:** irrigation efficiency and root-zone moisture concepts.  
**Explicit boundary in repository:** do not generalize treatment-specific results into universal indoor recommendations.

Course 4 therefore uses the source to support measurement/trend reasoning and irrigation/root-zone concepts, not a universal frequency, dryback percentage, EC target or irrigation recipe.

## `REF-NUTRITION-001`

**Source:** 2024 peer-reviewed study, *Mineral nutrition for Cannabis sativa in the vegetative stage using response surface analysis*.  
**Repository evidence level:** A.  
**Supports:** plant-nutrition relationships in the studied conditions.

Course 4 uses it to support the principle that mineral nutrition is contextual and that visual symptoms should not be converted into a specific deficiency/excess diagnosis without supporting evidence. The course does not turn the study into one universal nutrient formula for all cultivars, stages or systems.

## `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY`

**Source:** UK MHRA Guidance on GxP data integrity (2018).  
**Repository evidence level:** standards/government.  
**Supports:** attributable, contemporaneous, accurate, complete and reconstructable records.  
**Boundary:** this is a general data-integrity model, not cannabis law and not a U.S. cultivation regulation.

## Claim-to-source map

| Course claim | Evidence role | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| pH/EC interpretation requires correct sample identity and method context | `REF-IRRIGATION-001` + data-integrity model | No claim that one sample method is universally sufficient |
| EC is useful for ionic-strength/conductivity trends but not nutrient-specific analysis | irrigation/nutrition evidence + controlled lesson scope | Does not identify each dissolved nutrient |
| Root-zone moisture/dryback is best understood from repeated comparable observations | `REF-IRRIGATION-001` | No universal dryback target or interval claimed |
| Plant nutrition symptoms overlap with root-zone, water, pH/EC and developmental context | `REF-NUTRITION-001`, `REF-IRRIGATION-001` | Visual resemblance alone does not prove a specific nutrient cause |
| Controller/event status is not the same as verified crop delivery | irrigation process logic + `REF-IRRIGATION-001` | Facility procedure defines required representative checks |
| Work records should separate plan/target from measured/actual and preserve unresolved deviations | `REF-MHRA-GXP-DATA-INTEGRITY` + irrigation evidence | General quality principle, not cannabis-specific legal rule |

## Assessment evidence rules

Each scored Course 4 item should:

1. map to one of the six controlled objectives;
2. carry relevant evidence references;
3. avoid universal pH, EC, dryback, frequency or nutrient prescriptions that the source does not support;
4. distinguish observed evidence from diagnosis;
5. keep Technician I authority at controlled measurement, execution, permitted verification, documentation and escalation unless the scenario explicitly grants a broader task.

Machine checks can verify nonempty references and objective distribution. Human assessment review still must judge whether each source actually supports the tested claim and whether answer/distractor wording remains technically defensible.
