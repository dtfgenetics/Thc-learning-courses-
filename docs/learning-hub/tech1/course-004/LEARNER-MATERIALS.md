# Learner Materials — COURSE-LH-TECH1-004

**Course:** Water, Root Zone, Nutrition & Irrigation Fundamentals  
**Status:** published owner-approved academic learner package  
**Boundary:** facility SOPs, current work orders, product labels, equipment manuals and applicable jurisdictional requirements control operational work when they are more specific than the educational examples here.

## What this course trains

Course 4 trains reliable measurement, root-zone observation, controlled irrigation execution, representative delivery verification and truthful handoff. It does **not** train learners to independently design nutrient recipes, advanced crop-steering programs, protected controller logic, root-cause diagnoses or unauthorized repairs.

## Lesson 1 — sample identity, pH/EC and meter readiness

### Activity: measurement record

Using a supplied work order and sample set:

1. identify whether the task calls for source water, prepared solution, runoff/drainage or another defined sample;
2. record sample source/location and time before interpretation;
3. perform only the supplied meter readiness/rinse/storage/calibration-or-verification sequence;
4. obtain and record pH and EC with the correct units/method context;
5. keep planned/target values separate from actual measurements;
6. repeat or verify a suspect result within procedure;
7. document unresolved meter/sample uncertainty and escalation status.

**Worksheet:** `/assets/course4/water-solution-measurement-practice.webp`  
**Visual:** `/assets/course4/sample-ph-ec-context.webp`

Key evidence rule: EC reflects overall conductivity influenced by dissolved ions; it is not a direct measurement of each individual nutrient. A pH value is context, not proof of a particular deficiency or root disorder.

## Lesson 2 — root-zone moisture, dryback and EC trends

### Activity: seven-day trend review

Using a simulated seven-day log:

- preserve comparable irrigation times and measurement methods;
- describe saturation, drainage and moisture-loss/dryback trajectory;
- compare repeated root-zone EC values made with the same method;
- identify whether behavior changed from the prior comparable period;
- add crop stage, media/container and environmental context supplied by the scenario;
- separate observation from possible explanations;
- write an escalation note without prescribing an advanced irrigation/nutrition strategy.

**Worksheet:** `/assets/course4/irrigation-verification-practice.webp`  
**Visuals:** `/assets/course4/root-zone-dryback-trend.webp`, `/assets/course4/nutrition-context-differential.webp`

Key evidence rule: dryback is a trajectory, not simply “hours since watering.” One runoff/root-zone result does not establish a complete cause.

## Lesson 3 — authorized irrigation/fertigation execution

### Activity: work-order run with seeded deviations

The simulation contains a room/zone identity issue, one failed representative delivery point and one leak.

The learner must:

1. match the current work order to room/zone and source/material;
2. stop and clarify an identity/instruction mismatch;
3. perform only the assigned irrigation/fertigation sequence;
4. inspect procedure-defined representative delivery points;
5. distinguish controller/event status from verified crop delivery;
6. apply only permitted routine actions;
7. document leaks, clogs, missed delivery or incomplete work truthfully;
8. escalate unresolved mechanical/electrical/process issues rather than performing unauthorized repair.

**Worksheet:** `/assets/course4/irrigation-verification-practice.webp`  
**Visual:** `/assets/course4/irrigation-work-order-delivery.webp`

## Lesson 4 — nutrition context, deviations and handoff

### Activity: integrated shift packet

Combine:
- a pH/EC measurement record;
- a root-zone moisture/EC trend;
- one plant symptom report;
- an interrupted irrigation event.

Create a handoff that distinguishes:
- planned target/work-order instruction;
- actual measured value or delivery result;
- sample/method context;
- crop/root-zone observation;
- verification or permitted routine correction;
- unresolved condition;
- escalation recipient and next action.

**Visuals:** `/assets/course4/nutrition-context-differential.webp`, `/assets/course4/irrigation-fault-handoff-workflow.webp`

## Integrated Course 4 scenario

A technician receives a work order for a nutrient-solution irrigation event. The prepared solution has a supplied target pH/EC, but the first meter reading is unstable. After verification, the actual solution measurements are documented. During the event, one representative emitter is dry even though the controller shows completion. A crop note also describes older-leaf discoloration in the affected zone, and the comparable root-zone EC trend has risen across multiple observations.

Produce a complete work record that:

- confirms sample/work-order identity;
- records the verified pH/EC measurement and units without copying target values into the actual field;
- records the failed delivery point;
- preserves the repeated root-zone trend;
- describes the leaf symptom objectively without assigning a nutrient cause;
- records only the permitted verification/correction actually performed;
- marks incomplete/unresolved work as incomplete/unresolved;
- gives the receiving role a specific escalation and next action.

## Self-check

Before assessment, the learner should be able to answer:

- What sample or work order did I actually use?
- What was the planned target versus the measured/verified result?
- Are pH/EC units, method, time and identity preserved?
- Am I comparing like sampling methods with like methods?
- Is my root-zone conclusion based on a repeated trend or one reading?
- Did I verify actual representative irrigation delivery?
- Have I separated plant symptoms from unsupported nutrient diagnosis?
- Did I stay inside operator authority?
- Can the next qualified person reconstruct what remains unresolved?

## Assessment boundary

The 12-item module assessment is formative. The 24-item final is derived from the six controlled objectives and the four dedicated Course 4 lessons; `TEST-TO-TEACHING-MAP.md` records that provenance. Encyclopedia-only, external-reading-only, or unrelated-course material may not be scored unless the concept is first taught directly in Course 4. Practical B is a separate development performance assessment. Academic course completion alone is not Technician I credential issuance and does not prove Practical B has been validated.
