# Technician I Course 4 Final — Item Quality Revision

**Assessment:** `ASSESS-LH-TECH1-004-FINAL`  
**Revision date:** 2026-09-23  
**Assessment version:** 1.2.0  
**Scope:** all 24 summative items

## What changed

All 24 conventional-final items were revised to improve assessment quality while preserving the controlled blueprint.

The revision focused on:

- sample identity and pH/EC measurement quality;
- dryback, moisture and root-zone trend interpretation;
- nutrient-supply versus nutrient-availability reasoning;
- symptom-context recording without unsupported diagnosis;
- work-order identity conflicts and irrigation authorization boundaries;
- representative delivery verification and deviation status;
- operator-care limits, meter reliability and recurring emitter faults;
- planned-versus-actual records, missed-irrigation handoffs and missing-data integrity;
- replacing weak distractors and template rationales with plausible alternatives and explanatory feedback.

## What did not change

The revision preserves all item IDs, answer keys, objectives, competencies, controlled references, Bloom levels, difficulty labels, final item membership, blueprint weighting, taught-material mappings, test-to-teaching mappings, course-derived provenance, and the prohibitions on Encyclopedia substitution and untaught scored material.

Each changed item version was incremented so object-scoped review tooling can reopen assessment review for the changed versions.

## Human review requested

Reviewers should check distractor plausibility, water/root-zone/nutrition reasoning accuracy, operator-care and authorization boundaries, sample/record integrity, rationale proportionality to the controlled references, occupational authenticity and accessible wording.

## Validation boundary

This revision improves academic assessment quality but does not establish psychometric validity, reliability, fairness, standard setting, secure credential-form suitability, practical validity or professional credential authorization.
