## Non-negotiable failures
- SVG-only final asset.
- Raster that is merely a deterministic render of the SVG compatibility baseline.
- Unverified numeric target presented as universal cultivation guidance.
- Unreadable embedded text or labels at learner rendering size.
- Missing text alternative or missing downloadable/public path.
- Diagnostic image that presents appearance alone as proof of a specific deficiency, disease, pest or environmental cause.
- Practice sheet that cannot be completed legibly when printed.
