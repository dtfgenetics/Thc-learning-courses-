2. Replace placeholder/development copy with complete instructional material.
3. Ensure each lesson has:

9. Improve mobile/responsive presentation after the instructional material is complete enough to review meaningfully.

- technical/occupational and assessment review;
- rendered/manual accessibility and learner-UX review;

- standard setting and decision-rule approval;
- secure operational form evidence where applicable;
- privacy/security/retention and production-control evidence;
- final credential-program authorization.

## Definition of “finished”

A course is not considered materially finished merely because its JSON objects, routes, schemas, or tests exist. For this project, “finished course materials” means a learner can move through the course without encountering placeholder content, thin shell sections, unexplained technical terms, missing required visuals, unsupported claims, missing applied practice, or assessments that test material the course did not teach.
