# THC Academy Assessment Item Review Rubric

Credential-bearing assessment items must pass human review before becoming active.

## Required review gates

1. **Objective alignment** — the item measures the linked learning objective and competency.
2. **Evidence alignment** — the keyed answer and rationale are supported by the cited reference(s).
3. **Scientific scope** — treatment-specific research is not rewritten as a universal cultivation rule.
4. **Single defensible key** — for single-answer items, one choice is clearly best under the stated conditions.
5. **Distractor quality** — distractors are plausible enough to test understanding but are not deceptive, absurd, or dependent on trivia.
6. **Cognitive level** — the item actually requires the Bloom level declared in metadata.
7. **Plain language** — wording is concise, avoids unnecessary negatives, and does not test reading tricks.
8. **Accessibility** — the item can be understood without color, visual position, audio, or inaccessible media unless an equivalent accessible representation is supplied.
9. **Security** — secure answers and rationales are not shipped to unauthenticated learner clients for credential assessments.
10. **Legal/safety boundary** — an item must not imply that educational completion authorizes regulated activity or that a specific control product is lawful in every jurisdiction.

## Lifecycle

`draft -> technical-review -> editorial-review -> pilot -> active -> flagged -> retired`

Moving an item to `active` requires documented scientific/technical and assessment review. AI-assisted drafting is permitted, but AI output alone cannot approve an item.

## Pilot statistics

When sufficient response volume exists, review:

- item difficulty / percent correct
- discrimination
- distractor selection frequency
- omit rate
- response time anomalies
- learner challenge/appeal history

Statistics are diagnostic signals, not automatic approval or retirement decisions.
