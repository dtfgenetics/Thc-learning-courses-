# THC Academy Specialist Credential Pathways

This release establishes the first draft specialist certificate pathways for Advanced Genetics and Breeding, Advanced Nutrition/Diagnostics/Plant Health, and Post-Harvest Quality Assurance.

Each pathway uses a dedicated credential-purpose final assessment rather than reusing module formative checks. Course objects are marked credentialBearing=true to express intended architecture only; all courses, assessments, items and credential definitions remain draft.

## Non-production gate

No credential in this set is eligible for production issuance until its final assessment items complete technical review, editorial review, assessment review, pilot, and activation. Draft items must never be treated as active secure-bank content. Passing scores are provisional policy settings until standard-setting evidence is collected.

## Assessment design

The initial development forms contain four scenario items per specialist course, two per competency. This is intentionally a development milestone, not a secure production bank. The next expansion target is broader objective coverage, parallel-item depth, pilot statistics, challenge review, and at least the configured active-bank minimum before production issuance.

## Credential meaning

These are educational assessment-based certificates. They are not occupational licenses, government certifications, accredited personnel certifications, or authorization to cultivate, possess, manufacture, distribute or sell cannabis. Legal requirements remain jurisdiction-specific.
