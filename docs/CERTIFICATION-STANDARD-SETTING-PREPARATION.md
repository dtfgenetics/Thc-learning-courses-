# Certification Standard-Setting Preparation Coverage

All 15 canonical Technician certification courses now have an explicit standard-setting path.

## Coverage

- **13 conventional-final courses** use the conventional standard-setting evidence workflow.
- **2 integrated-performance courses** — Technician I Course 7 and Technician II Course 8 — use integrated performance standard setting across required practical/capstone components.

## Conventional-final workflow

1. Complete exact-version assessment review.
2. Complete controlled pilot and item analysis.
3. Stabilize the scored form/version.
4. Conduct a documented standard-setting study using an appropriate supported method.
5. Record the panel-recommended cut score and impact/sensitivity information.
6. Keep the panel recommendation separate from governance adoption.
7. Record the final adopted production cut score only after governance review.

The current course pass targets remain **development/provisional values** until this process is completed.

## Integrated-performance workflow

1. Complete practical/capstone calibration and pilot evidence.
2. Freeze exact component versions.
3. Conduct a panel standard-setting study for every required component.
4. Record panel-recommended component thresholds.
5. Separately adopt the component thresholds and the decision rule.
6. Preserve the current non-compensatory design: required components must pass and critical failures cannot be averaged away unless the approved governance model is formally changed.

## Boundary

A packet, command, or prepared registry entry does not establish a defensible passing standard. Real panel judgments, evidence, rationale, impact review and governance adoption are required.
