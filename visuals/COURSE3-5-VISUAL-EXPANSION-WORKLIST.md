## Rejected visual pattern

Do not use generated posters that:
- invent universal “ideal” cannabis ranges without a controlled source;
- mix too many unrelated topics into one learner visual;
- use decorative cannabis imagery where a measurement or anatomy diagram is needed;
- imply diagnosis from a single symptom, pH value, EC value, VPD value or photograph;
- substitute DTF promotional branding for the THC educational identity.

The visual should teach a specific decision, mechanism, comparison or observation method.
